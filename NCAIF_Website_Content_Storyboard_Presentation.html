AUDIT METHODOLOGY Sitemap Evaluation Framework

Readiness Scale (Levels 1 to 5)

Operational evaluation model measuring content availability, analytical effort, and publication readiness
Sitemap › Audit Scale
TIER 5 Level 5 — Format
Direct Web Porting
Real, matched material covers the page in full. The remaining work is strictly technical formatting into web components.
TIER 4 Level 4 — Compile
Multi-Doc Compilation
Real material exists across multiple existing documents. Requires compiling into one coherent page without new research.
TIER 3 Level 3 — Synthesize
Data & Spatial Synthesis
Real underlying datasets exist but require spatial computation, analytical structuring, or synthesis before publishing.
TIER 2 Level 2 — Authoring
Original Authoring & Research
Little or no existing material. Original writing or literature research required, but no external dependency blocks execution.
TIER 1 Level 1 — Blocked
External & Policy Dependency
Cannot move forward until external agreements, DCCE executive decisions, or methodology work package outputs land.
