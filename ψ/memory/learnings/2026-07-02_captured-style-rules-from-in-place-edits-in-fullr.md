# Captured style rules from in-place edits in Full_Report_Final.md: 

Captured style rules from in-place edits in Full_Report_Final.md: 
1. Systems Thinking Over Silos: Emphasize cascading impacts across systems rather than treating NAP sectors as isolated buckets.
2. Analytical Critique Over Fact-Stating: Critically evaluate frameworks instead of just describing them (e.g., noting the limitations of human-centric disaster definitions).
3. Humanize the Macro-Impact: Anchor macro-economic risks to actual human well-being and quality of life.
4. Paragraph Cohesion: Respect sectoral boundaries instead of cramming different sectors into a single paragraph.

Also banned dramatic AI phrasing ('ตีกรอบความไม่แน่นอน') and replaced with formal policy terms ('เปรียบเทียบกับฉากทัศน์ทางเลือก').
