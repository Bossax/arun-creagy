# When editing or drafting documents based on user-provided drafts, prioritize pre

When editing or drafting documents based on user-provided drafts, prioritize preserving the user's specific language choices, key additions, and structural tone. Avoid overwriting draft paragraphs with templated AI transitions or generic wording, as this destroys user context and introduces rework. Conduct surgical edits to ensure compliance with style rules, and double check that no user-contributed points or analytical details are lost.
