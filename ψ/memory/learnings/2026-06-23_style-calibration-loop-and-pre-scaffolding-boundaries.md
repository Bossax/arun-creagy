# Learning: Style Calibration Loop & Pre-Scaffolding Boundaries
**Category**: Methodology | Workspace Hygiene
**Context**: TOR 5.5 Articles Initiation
**Date**: 2026-06-23

## Pattern
When starting a strategic writing project, avoid premature scaffolding of folders or assets based on tentative documents. Always insert a formal **Pre-Start Gate** where the human expert and the AI:
1. Lock in the finalized list of deliverables.
2. Ingest gold-standard writing samples and explicit term rules (lexicon) to calibrate the style context pack.

Only after this gate is cleared should the physical directory structure be created and Phase 1 (Core Extraction) be executed. This preserves directory hygiene and prevents AI naming drift.

## Why it works
This enforces parameter and workflow discipline. By preventing the AI from proactively building folder structures based on unfinalized synopses, we avoid context bloat and ensure that every drafted article aligns strictly with the human's finalized priorities and stylistic DNA from day one.
