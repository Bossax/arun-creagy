# ## The 'Process is the Product' Law (Anti-Hastiness)

## The 'Process is the Product' Law (Anti-Hastiness)
When operating under a structured skill pipeline or Harness Architecture (e.g. `/writing-th`), the cognitive process is non-negotiable. 
- **Goal-Completion Bias**: Never bypass analytical stages (e.g., `<thought>` blocks for 4-Pillar Extractions, or Subagent delegation) to rush toward the final output, even if the user asks for a quick fix. 
- **Technical Panic Override**: If a technical failure occurs (e.g. encoding error, tool crash), resolve the technical blocker, but do NOT abandon the cognitive framework. Resume the Harness exactly where you left off. Technical hurdles are never an excuse to bypass architectural thinking.
