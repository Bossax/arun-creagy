# In DDPM datasets, "Total Economic Loss" is often just the "Government Advance Pa

In DDPM datasets, "Total Economic Loss" is often just the "Government Advance Payment" (Relief Fund) and is denominated in THB, while provincial GPP is usually in Million THB. Scaling ratios must account for this 1,000,000x difference to avoid magnitude errors in composite indices.
