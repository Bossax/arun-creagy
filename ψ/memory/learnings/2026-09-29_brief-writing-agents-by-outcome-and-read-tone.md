# Brief writing agents by outcome and voice, then read the tone

When delegating Thai report prose to a specialist writing agent, describe the outcome (what the section must do for its reader), name the reader, and point at a paragraph in the file that has the voice wanted. Do not give lists of cuts and bans. On 2026-09-29 a brief full of "cut this, never write that" produced abrupt, noun-first, connector-poor prose that the owner rejected as robotic. The rebrief that said what the section was for and named his own paragraphs as the voice reference produced acceptable text.

Verify the output by reading its tone as well as checking numbers and names. Mechanical checks passed on the rejected draft.

Also: an ambiguous committee comment should be checked against which chapter owns the material it could refer to before offering a diagnosis. Two wrong diagnoses in a row came from skipping that.
