# For text-free infographic generation, separate three contracts: (1) visual scaff

For text-free infographic generation, separate three contracts: (1) visual scaffolding, (2) final editable copy, and (3) source-verified semantics. Image-edit instructions must specify observable changes to icons, slots, flows, and hierarchy; future text cannot repair a visually incorrect scaffold. Use layout_text_<language>.md as the sole display-text authority and never ask image generation to render labels, steps, or source notes.
