# In two-stage enterprise data platform procurement, Business NFRs (freshness SLAs

In two-stage enterprise data platform procurement, Business NFRs (freshness SLAs, regulatory compliance, critical pipeline availability) are Design-phase deliverables that must be authored by the blueprint architect (CRDB), whereas System NFRs (node sizing, index tuning) belong to the build contractor (TOR70). Fixed-price contract risk is mitigated by bundling Data Contracts and an Explicit Assumption Log into the technical baseline exhibit.
