2. In a numbered list that ties each item to an earlier list ("service X blocked by gap dimension N"), prefer self-contained narrative statement of the obstacle over citing the index number.
3. In a parallel bullet list describing states of the same object, anchor every bullet to one consistent subject noun phrase.
