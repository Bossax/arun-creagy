# Data governance in climate risk projects must not be evaluated by technical indi

Data governance in climate risk projects must not be evaluated by technical indicators alone (such as database clean-up, CDM entity integrity, or GDX connection status). Its ultimate value is determined by the downstream policy outcomes it enables for the Adaptation Division—specifically, establishing an institutional shield (หลักประกันเชิงสถาบัน) for public spending, justifying local budgeting under audit, translating climate projections into engineering codes (DPT standards), and validating Loss & Damage to secure international climate finance. Governance must always be framed in terms of the adaptation division's core mandate.
