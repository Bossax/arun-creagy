# When generating a Phase 4 draft based on a Structural Narrative Blueprint, the A

When generating a Phase 4 draft based on a Structural Narrative Blueprint, the AI must strictly adhere to the defined narrative structure. If required information is missing from the Phase 1/2 raw extractions, the AI must explicitly state that the information is missing (e.g. '[Information not available in current extraction]') rather than silently omitting the section or hallucinating connective facts. The blueprint structure is an immutable contract.
