# When comparing historical impact indices (like the CRI Pilot) to modernized vers

When comparing historical impact indices (like the CRI Pilot) to modernized versions (Phase 1) where metrics use different definitions (e.g., Agricultural vs. Total GPP, or Casualties vs. Households), avoid direct value/ratio differencing. Instead, use Spearman Rank Correlation to measure the stability of provincial risk profiles. A high correlation (rho > 0.7) validates proxy-shifting, while a low correlation identifies specific methodology 'disruptors' (like the Bangkok Paradox caused by denominator scaling).
