# Learning: Forensic Asset Auditing & The Synthesis Bottleneck
**Date:** 2026-06-04
**Category:** Strategic Information Architecture
**Status:** Validated

## 1. The Pattern
When mapping institutional data assets to a high-fidelity sitemap, there is a recurring tendency to equate "Data Availability" (e.g., a PDF report exists) with "Content Readiness" (e.g., the web page is ready to be built).

## 2. The Grounding Principle: Content Readiness != Data Existence
A "High Readiness" rating should only be applied if the content is **Actionable for the Frontend**:
- **Prose:** Is the text written in the final "Natural by Design" tone, or is it buried in a 200-page scientific report?
- **Data:** Is it a live API/DB, or is it an "Internal Library" folder of unstructured files?
- **Visuals:** Are the infographics designed, or are they just raw GIS layers?

## 3. The "Synthesis" Bottleneck
In Climate Data Platforms, **Synthesis** (the act of translating raw science into authoritative explainers) is the primary bottleneck. 
- **Learning:** 80% of project risk often resides in the "Low Readiness" nodes (Policy Dashboards, Provincial Summaries) even when the "Data Catalog" (raw files) is 100% complete.

## 4. Application
Always force a forensic comparison between the **Content Brief** (the promise to the user) and the **Technical Asset** (the source). If they don't match exactly, the readiness is **LOW**, regardless of how "available" the raw data is.
