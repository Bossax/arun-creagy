# Learning: GHG Protocol LUC for Flooded Land (Reservoirs)

**Date**: 2026-05-25
**Concept**: GHG Accounting, Land Use Change, Water Reservoirs
**Source**: GHG Protocol Land Sector & Removals Standard

## Pattern
When converting land (e.g., Forest) to Flooded Land (Water Reservoir), the Land Use Change (LUC) emissions accounting follows a different temporal pattern than terrestrial cropland.

## Key Rules
1. **CO2 Tracking**: 20 years (Standard assessment period for stock change).
2. **CH4 Tracking**: 100 years (Specific requirement for anaerobic decay in flooded systems).
3. **Ownership**: Current operators/owners inherit the reporting obligation if the reservoir is within its 20/100 year window, regardless of who originally performed the conversion.
4. **Mixed Land Use**: In a farm containing both cropland and reservoirs, the areas must be accounted for separately to reflect the differing assessment periods (20 vs 100 years).

## Why it Matters
Underestimating the CH4 tracking period (thinking it's the same 20 years as cropland) leads to significant under-reporting of Scope 1/3 emissions for companies with water-intensive value chains or hydropower usage.
