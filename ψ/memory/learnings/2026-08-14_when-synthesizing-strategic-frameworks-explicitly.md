# When synthesizing strategic frameworks, explicitly ground terminology and scope

When synthesizing strategic frameworks, explicitly ground terminology and scope in the provided evidence documents (e.g., FGD notes, specific slide decks) rather than defaulting to standard industry templates (like 'RACI'). This prevents scope creep (e.g., DCCE-wide vs. specific platform) and avoids violating project-specific jargon constraints.
