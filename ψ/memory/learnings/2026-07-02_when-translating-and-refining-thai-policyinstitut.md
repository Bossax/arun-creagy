# When translating and refining Thai policy/institutional documents under the NCAI

When translating and refining Thai policy/institutional documents under the NCAIF Style Pack:
1. Avoid "Overcompensation" with abstract academic jargon: When adhering to the rule against naked system terms (e.g. avoiding "ระบบ" without modifier), do not fallback to overly abstract words like "สถาปัตยกรรม" (architecture). Instead, use specific, spoken operational terms such as "ฐานข้อมูล" (database), "ระบบรายงาน" (reporting system), or "ระบบบันทึกข้อมูล" (data entry system).
2. Restrict English Parenthesis: English translations in parentheses should be limited strictly to exact database schema names (e.g., `DISASTER_EVENT`) or official acronyms (e.g., `MVD`). Banish conceptual English translations like `(Data Availability)` or `(Temporal completeness)`.
3. Objective Tone vs Moral Blaming: Banish subjective/moralizing verbs like "ละเลย" or "เพิกเฉย" (neglect) when discussing administrative or user data gaps. State systemic facts objectively, such as "ขาดกระบวนงาน" or "ขาดกลไกบังคับ".
