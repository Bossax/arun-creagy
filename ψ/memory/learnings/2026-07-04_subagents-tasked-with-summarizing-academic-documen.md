# Subagents tasked with summarizing academic documents will ruthlessly optimize fo

Subagents tasked with summarizing academic documents will ruthlessly optimize for length by cutting crucial detail and flattening complex narratives into bullets unless strictly constrained by a hard-floor target length boundary (e.g. 'Must be EXACTLY 75% of original length') and explicit format prohibitions ('Do not convert into executive summary').
