# Style-Pack TH gained two new rules (38, 39) from Boss's in-place review of the C

Style-Pack TH gained two new rules (38, 39) from Boss's in-place review of the CRDB chapter-1 plan (section 4, exec summary): (1) never leave a noun phrase's head noun implicit (ลำดับชั้น/เครื่องมือ/การนำทาง of *what*?) — Boss's own words were "ชอบละนามตลอด อ่านไม่รู้เรื่อง" and "you omit the essential noun very often making reading incomprehensible", recurring 3x in one pass; (2) in bullet-point outlines, replace abstract collective nouns (คุณค่า, ทุนเดิม, ข้อจำกัด, กลุ่มผู้มีส่วนร่วม, ประเด็นหลัก) with the concrete enumerated list they stand for. Also flagged an unresolved tension: this same edit added conceptual English parentheticals like (quality)/(Data Platform), which contradicts the pack's existing "Strict Parenthetical Anchor" ban on translating conceptual terms — only seen once, so not promoted, needs Boss's confirmation on whether planning docs get an exception.
