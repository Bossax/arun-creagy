# For multi-panel visual design projects, perform Stage 1 research and evidence ma

For multi-panel visual design projects, perform Stage 1 research and evidence mapping jointly across all panels to capture shared science, but execute Stage 3 to Stage 5 (generation handoff, vector asset extraction, and layout assembly) strictly one panel at a time to prevent prompt bleed and preserve asset precision.
