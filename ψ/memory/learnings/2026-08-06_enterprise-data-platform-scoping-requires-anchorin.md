# Enterprise data platform scoping requires anchoring requirements across three co

Enterprise data platform scoping requires anchoring requirements across three complementary frameworks: PMI/PMBOK (Business Case, RTM), IIBA/BABOK (BRD, FRS, NFRs, Data Dictionary), and DAMA-DMBOK (Governance Charter, Stewardship RACI, Source-to-Target Mapping, Lineage, DAMA 6-Dimension DQ). Omission of data-specific artifacts (STM, Data Acceptance Criteria, Assumption Logs) is the primary driver of vendor rediscovery, data swamps, and budget overruns.
