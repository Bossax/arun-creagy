# Thai institutional-writing workflow heuristic: (1) When a plan’s stated paragrap

Thai institutional-writing workflow heuristic: (1) When a plan’s stated paragraph count conflicts with its enumerated rhetorical jobs, count and preserve the jobs; compressing to the label recreates skipped logic and abrupt transitions. (2) Encode user-mandated terminology in the writing contract before verbalization, not as a late search-and-replace convention. (3) Place synthesis tables after their concepts have been explained but before the section-closing transition, so the final paragraph can interpret status and carry the reader forward. (4) Treat attribution as part of source fidelity inside tables: meeting feedback, consultant synthesis, and later design decisions must remain distinct. (5) Independent clean-context editorial review still requires a parent-level integration check for adjacent heading levels, cross-section flow, table placement, and source attribution. Any correction invalidates the receipt and requires a fresh review.
