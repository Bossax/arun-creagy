# Learning: For interconnected policy-report sections, draft quality improves when

Learning: For interconnected policy-report sections, draft quality improves when the writing process preserves the operational ancestry of the argument. Define one cumulative service-logic spine across sections: consultation evidence -> demand synthesis -> prioritized services -> readiness/gap analysis -> policy and technical recommendations. Also synchronize the visible reminder ledger immediately after delegated draft completion so artifact reality and process state do not drift apart.
