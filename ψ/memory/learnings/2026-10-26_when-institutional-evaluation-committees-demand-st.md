# When institutional evaluation committees demand strict adherence to contractual

When institutional evaluation committees demand strict adherence to contractual TOR wording that predates modern technical practices (e.g., agile delivery, in-house data talent), do not fight the contractual frame or dilute technical depth. Apply semantic dissection: decompose complex recommendations into their constituent operational mechanics and tuck each cleanly into the mandated contractual axes (Policy/Technical x Target Components). Furthermore, for LLM drafting workflows, always author narrative English outlines rather than bullet lists to convey logical connective tissue, and establish a machine-readable evaluation matrix (Toulmin claims, keywords, invariants) before verbalization begins.
