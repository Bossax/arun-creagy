# Learning: Service vs. Governance Boundary (Payload vs. Pipe)

**Date**: 2026-06-05
**Topic**: Institutional Architecture / Interoperability

## 🔍 The Pattern
In complex government digital transformations, technical requirements often masquerade as "User Services." Specifically, G2G data exchange gateways (Interoperability) are frequently proposed as top-level services.

## ⚠️ The Problem
Treating a "Gateway" as a service leads to:
1.  **Redundancy**: Overlapping with central digital agencies (e.g., DGA/GDX).
2.  **Expert Drift**: Focusing on the "Pipe" (Transport) rather than the "Payload" (Intelligence).
3.  **Liability Confusion**: Mixing technical uptime with scientific validity.

## ✅ The Correction
*   **User Service**: Focuses on **Payload Certification**. The user consumes "Authoritative Information" (Service 01).
*   **Governance Backlog**: Focuses on the **Interoperability Pipe**. The institution manages "Exchange Entitlements" and "Article 15 Compliance" as an administrative requirement.
*   **Constraint**: If the DCCE doesn't own the road (DGA owns GDX), it shouldn't sell the "Gateway." It should sell the **"Trust"** that travels through it.
