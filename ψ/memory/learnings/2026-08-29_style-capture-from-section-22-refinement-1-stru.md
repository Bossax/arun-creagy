# Style capture from Section 2.2 refinement:

Style capture from Section 2.2 refinement:
1. Structural: Avoid artificial causal links claiming demand-side data meta-needs 'determine' specific use cases. Report demand-side data needs as interview findings, and workshop proposals as specific use cases, then combine them at the final synthesis stage.
2. Micro-Structural / Parallelism: Ensure bulleted list item headings maintain consistent nominal phrases describing systemic attributes rather than mixing clauses and nouns.
3. Diction: Ground 'decision-ready' with concrete operational translation dimensions (numbers, language, units of measurement, variables) to prevent abstract IT boilerplate.
