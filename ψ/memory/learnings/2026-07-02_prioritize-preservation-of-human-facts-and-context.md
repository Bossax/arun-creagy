# Prioritize preservation of human facts and contextual details over rigid AI styl

Prioritize preservation of human facts and contextual details over rigid AI style templates; adjust tone and formatting surgically rather than overwriting blocks entirely. When editing human-drafted policy reports, over-aggressive compression to fit executive styles can destroy meticulously researched domain case studies (like exact yield drops, municipality infrastructure details, or storm evacuation numbers).
