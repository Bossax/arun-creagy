# When writing formal reports in Thai (writing-th), avoid literal translations of 

When writing formal reports in Thai (writing-th), avoid literal translations of scenario matrix pairings (e.g. Impact x Uncertainty prose) as they create complex and unnatural Thai grammar. Instead, use a structured 'Variable Listing' format with explicit headers or bullet points.
