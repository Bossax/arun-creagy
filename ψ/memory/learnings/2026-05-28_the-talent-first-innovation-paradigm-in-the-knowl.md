# The Talent-First Innovation Paradigm: In the knowledge economy, firms follow tal

The Talent-First Innovation Paradigm: In the knowledge economy, firms follow talent, and talent follows Quality of Life (QoL). 78% of tech firms prioritize talent concentration over tax breaks, while 92% of Gen Z workers demand walkable environments. An 'Exit-Proof City' must prioritize Layer 0-2 infrastructure (Environment, Mobility, Housing) to anchor talent before the innovation engine (Labs, Incubators) can function. Sprawl and long commutes (>45 mins) are direct 'Exit Triggers' for high-skill talent.
