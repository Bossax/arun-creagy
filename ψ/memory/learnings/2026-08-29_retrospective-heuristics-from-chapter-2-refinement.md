# Retrospective Heuristics from Chapter 2 Refinement & Style-Capture Overhaul:

Retrospective Heuristics from Chapter 2 Refinement & Style-Capture Overhaul:
1. Zero-Drop Lexical Discipline: Style capture must mandate sentence-by-sentence lexical scanning across 5 categories (collocations/calques, domain precision, formality, passive/defeatist syntax elimination, filler pruning) and log all into miss_register.db before high-level synthesis.
2. Decoupling Demand-Side Meta-Needs from Use Cases: In cross-agency climate data reports, report interview data needs as systemic readiness findings, separate from workshop use-case scenarios, combining them only in the final service synthesis.
3. Strategic Driver Framing: In institutional writing, replace passive administrative deferrals ('ถูกเลื่อนออกเนื่องจากข้อจำกัด') with positive-conditional developmental framing ('มีความสำคัญสูง แต่ยังต้องพัฒนาองค์ความรู้ร่วมกันระหว่างนักวิทยาศาสตร์ วิศวกร และผู้ออกนโยบาย') and position statutory reporting mandates (A-BTR) as platform drivers.
