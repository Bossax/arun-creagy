# For an evidence-led infographic, require a source-specific finding, its mechanis

For an evidence-led infographic, require a source-specific finding, its mechanism, its reader consequence, visual proof, and exact evidence anchor before concepting. If the central claim could be written without opening the supplied sources, reject it as generic. Treat familiar visual forms and lists as insufficient until each module earns space through evidence → mechanism → reader consequence → visual role.
