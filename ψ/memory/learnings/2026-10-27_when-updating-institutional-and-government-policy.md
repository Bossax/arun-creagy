# When updating institutional and government policy reports, write as an authorita

When updating institutional and government policy reports, write as an authoritative, self-contained truth ('originally in this state') rather than narrating diffs or temporal version changes ('we newly added 12 datasets in v6'). For government operational handoffs, technical requirement codes and barcodes (REQ-xxx, PUB-xxx) must be abstracted into human-centric Work Packages (DEL-0 to DEL-14) with one responsible division per deliverable and full document titles with direct links. In complex TOR deliverables, verify multi-section chapter composition against master spine documents (e.g. Chapter 5 spanning both TOR 5.3.8 Gap Analysis in §5.1 and TOR 5.3.9 Recommendations/Roadmap in §5.2).
