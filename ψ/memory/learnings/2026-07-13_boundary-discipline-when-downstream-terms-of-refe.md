# Boundary Discipline: When downstream terms of reference (TOR) shift toward syste

Boundary Discipline: When downstream terms of reference (TOR) shift toward system-architecture-heavy constraints, keep the current project's scope strictly limited to its core mandate (data and service blueprints). Do not stretch the project's outputs to become system architectures; instead, adopt a Product Owner perspective to harden the data/service blueprints as a zero-discovery procurement shield for the system contractor.
