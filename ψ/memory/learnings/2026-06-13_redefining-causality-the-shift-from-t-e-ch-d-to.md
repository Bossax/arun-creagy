# Redefining Causality: The Shift from T-E-CH-D to T-E-D-A

## The Pattern: Motive vs. Artifact
Oracle demands that **Files are not Triggers; Files are Evidence.** 
- **Evidence (E)**: The physical "Nouns" (Artifacts).
- **Trigger (T)**: The conceptual "Verbs" (Insights/Mandates).

## The Upgrade: T-E-D-A Ontology
1. **Trigger (T)**: Kinetic insight.
2. **Evidence (E)**: Grounding artifacts.
3. **Decision (D)**: Semantic Lock (Verified posture).
4. **Asset (A)**: Hardened technical reality.

## Implementation: Lens & Funnel
Discovery skills (/trace) act as a **Lens** (Hypothesis). Commitment skills (/seal) act as a **Funnel** (Hardening) via database-level bonding (arra_trace_link).
