# In complex multi-agency projects, the use of formal, industry-standard technical

In complex multi-agency projects, the use of formal, industry-standard technical terminology (e.g., DAMA-DMBOK) acts as a Strategic Shield and Empowerment Mechanism. Framing governance as 'Data Sovereignty' (Empowerment) rather than 'Data Stewardship' (Workload) secures buy-in from senior leadership by aligning technical goals with administrative self-interest. Maintaining 'Product Ownership' internally protects against the 'Portal Trap' and 'Technical Debt' by enforcing a Frozen Technical Baseline on contractors.
