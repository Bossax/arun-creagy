# Argument-map warrant/application_to_design fields in /writing-th are verbalized

Argument-map warrant/application_to_design fields in /writing-th are verbalized as prose payload by Stage 3, not treated as internal-only scaffolding. If a Stage 1 unit's justification includes a forward-reference to how a later chapter/section treats the same topic more deeply, that reasoning must be kept out of the argument map entirely -- not just excluded from the final prose -- because the verbalizer drafts directly from claim/grounds/warrant/application_to_design and will turn "developed further later" reasoning into a stated sentence in the reader-facing draft. Fix: ground the unit in facts that stand on their own (e.g. stakeholder demand + a contractual/TOR requirement) and instruct application_to_design to keep the unit self-contained with no forward references. Internal-only scoping rationale belongs in a separate note (contract commentary, handoff, chat), never in the argument map.
