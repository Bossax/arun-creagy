# For multi-section institutional reports, renumbering or moving a draft must trig

For multi-section institutional reports, renumbering or moving a draft must trigger semantic revalidation of the writing plan, content contract, exclusions, neighboring-section boundaries, and review receipt; it is not merely a filesystem change. Freeze canonical names in the contract before drafting, distinguish ontology terms from service architecture, and assign each evidence source a specific job. Quantitative prose must state denominators and whether classifications overlap. Ambiguous aggregate claims with unclear definitions or denominators belong in traceability notes, not executive prose. Re-run independent semantic review after every draft or contract change because linting cannot detect ontology conflation, recommendation drift, missing required structures, or competing canonical terms.
