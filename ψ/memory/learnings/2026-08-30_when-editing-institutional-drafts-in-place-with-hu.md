# When editing institutional drafts in-place with human review notes: (1) Affirmat

When editing institutional drafts in-place with human review notes: (1) Affirmative positioning over contrast: state project outputs directly against lifecycle phases (e.g. 70%/80% coverage) rather than contrasting before/after states that risk leaking scope assumptions; (2) Preserve and elaborate human-added conceptual clauses (such as Iterative Design Loops) rather than flattening them into generic bridges; (3) Verify primary source documents listed in writing contracts directly before escalating to broad trace passes; (4) Respect strict mechanical lexicon gates by choosing unencumbered synonyms (e.g., 'ที่สมบูรณ์' over 'ฉบับสมบูรณ์' to satisfy classifier bans).
