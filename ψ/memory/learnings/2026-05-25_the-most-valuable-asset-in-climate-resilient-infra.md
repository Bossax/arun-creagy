# The most valuable asset in climate-resilient infrastructure consulting is the 'T

The most valuable asset in climate-resilient infrastructure consulting is the 'Translation Layer' that maps climate hazard data (return periods, flood depths, wind speeds) directly into engineering design standards and asset sensitivities (fragility curves, damage functions). Sourcing these physical constants from engineering partners is essential for 'hardening' climate resilience databases.
