# For expert-level analytical document rewrites, do not apply rigid structural fra

For expert-level analytical document rewrites, do not apply rigid structural frameworks (such as 4-Pillars or writing plans) in a manner that compresses and strips out dense details, tables, and comparative schemas. Retain 100% of the analytical baseline's depth, and stage all drafts in temporary files before overwriting active repository assets.
