# Learning: Analytical Integrity over Strategic Style

**Date**: 2026-06-10  
**Context**: Drafting policy reports for NCAIF/DCCE using the `writing-th` skill.

## 1. The Lesson
Strategic personas (e.g., "Data Strategist") and high-level metaphors (e.g., "The Wobbling Foundation") are dangerous when they precede empirical evidence. In this session, I attempted to draft a policy report by "summarizing" the strategy before actually executing the data mapping. This led to a hollow draft that missed specific data gaps (Missing Primitives) and violated the TOR.

## 2. Technical Findings
- **Bottom-Up Flow is Mandatory**: For evidence-based policy reports, the flow must start from the "Small Picture" (Data Catalog Audit/Use-Case gaps) to justify the "Big Picture" (Service-Level Strategy).
- **Sub-agent Delegation**: High-volume data tasks (mapping 260 datasets to 40 UCs and 8 Platforms) MUST be delegated to sub-agents early to avoid hallucinated summaries in the main agent's history.
- **Jargon Purge vs. Precision**: Purging jargons (codes/metaphors) is necessary for executive readability, but it must not come at the cost of technical precision (naming the missing datasets).

## 3. Strategic Realignment
- Always execute the **Service-Level Gap Matrix** (Phases 1-3 of the plan) BEFORE Stage 0 of the writing skill.
- The "Executive Protector" persona requires **Forensic Evidence** to be credible. A shield without a solid wall of facts is just a metaphor.
