# Evidence-dense executive writing should preserve three separate completion state

Evidence-dense executive writing should preserve three separate completion states: design blueprint completion, source-content readiness, and operational system deployment. Advance human approval may satisfy workflow pauses, but the approved content contract, isolated draft, evidence sidecar, mechanical gate, and semantic receipt must still be materialized. Keep internal locators outside audience-facing prose. Bind editorial approval to the exact draft hash; any correction, including minor copy edits, invalidates the receipt and requires a fresh independent review. For MCP reloads, identify workers by exact process name and command line, terminate only the requested service, and verify its backing container remains healthy.
