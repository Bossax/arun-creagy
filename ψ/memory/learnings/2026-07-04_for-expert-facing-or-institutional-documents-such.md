# For expert-facing or institutional documents (such as NCAIF reports and academic

For expert-facing or institutional documents (such as NCAIF reports and academic articles), a word-level search-and-replace is insufficient to resolve translation or stylistic issues. Jarvis/AI-generated prose tends to leak weak transitions ("นอกจากนี้", "ในขณะเดียวกัน"), passive structures, and colloquialisms ("ลดลงแรง", "ฉากทัศน์การปล่อยสูง"). To address this:
1. Synthesize all active style packs (e.g., NCAIF Institutional and TOR 5.5 Articles).
2. Perform a line-by-line sentence-level rewrite rather than localized terminology swaps.
3. Replace English-based negative contrast scaffolds ("ไม่ได้...แต่...") and fill transitions with direct, active, and authoritative Thai institutional assertions.
