# In large-scale government IT procurement, infrastructure/hardware expertise is o

In large-scale government IT procurement, infrastructure/hardware expertise is often mistaken for domain expertise. Hardening strategies include: mandating pre-defined domain logic (CDM/NCAIF), explicit personnel bifurcation (Engineering vs. Domain Scientists), and mandating decoupled architectures (Data Spaces) to prevent vendor lock-in and manual portal traps.
