# Flag structural discoveries mid-execution, don't silently absorb them into an al

Flag structural discoveries mid-execution, don't silently absorb them into an already-approved plan. While executing an approved plan to edit a DRD document, four requirements assumed separate were discovered (via fresh sitemap-tree research) to actually be one consolidated node. Rather than quietly folding the fix into the edit pass, stopped, showed the evidence, and got explicit confirmation before deviating from the approved scope. A structural fact discovered during execution (not during planning) changes what the plan means, not just how it's carried out — it deserves a pause and explicit re-confirmation, not a silent scope change, even mid-flow.
