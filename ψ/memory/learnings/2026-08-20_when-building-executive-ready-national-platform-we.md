# When building executive-ready national platform web mockups (10:8 aspect ratio,

When building executive-ready national platform web mockups (10:8 aspect ratio, 1280x1024 px for slides):
1. Grounding in Real Domain Assets: Always prioritize embedding real empirical visualizations (CCIC Spatial Multi-Hazard Maps, Swiss Re GDP Loss Curves, World Bank Exposure & Heat Stress Maps, National M&E Platform Sectoral Dashboards) as self-contained base64 data rather than abstract wireframe placeholders.
2. Canonical Sitemap Integrity: Never alter, renumber, or invent new subtopic titles or groupings when redesigning mockups; preserve official hierarchical nomenclature verbatim to maintain 1-to-1 traceability with system architecture and DRD requirements.
3. Executive Readability (2x Typography): Scale typography (18-20px headers, 12-14px badges, 11-12px descriptive texts) and maximize viewport real estate by removing redundant helper boxes, letting key visuals expand to fill dedicated card containers cleanly.
4. Tools Hub Grid Structure: Use a modular 3x2 gridded card layout with interactive search and category filter pills (inspired by Climate Analytics Tools) to showcase heterogeneous data products (GIS maps, economic models, finance trackers, open data catalogs, solution libraries) uniformly.
