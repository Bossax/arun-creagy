# The Task-Executor Bias vs Strategic Synthesis. When aligning texts, don't just c

The Task-Executor Bias vs Strategic Synthesis. When aligning texts, don't just compress or copy-paste. Map the exact substantive logic (what was removed, what was highlighted) without violating the original scope constraint. The process is the product; blind compression destroys the product.
