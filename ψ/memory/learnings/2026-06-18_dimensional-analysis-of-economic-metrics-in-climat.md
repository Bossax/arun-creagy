# Dimensional analysis of economic metrics in climate indices requires careful syn

Dimensional analysis of economic metrics in climate indices requires careful synchronization of unit scales (THB vs. Million THB) and terminology (Relief vs. Total Loss) to prevent 'Bangkok Paradox' artifacts where safe urban centers appear artificially risky. Always verify the temporal denominator (e.g., 8-year average) and the specific fiscal recovery source (e.g., DDPM Relief) before calculating ratios against GPP.
