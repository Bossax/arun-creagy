# When delegating Thai report prose to a specialist writing agent, brief by outcom

When delegating Thai report prose to a specialist writing agent, brief by outcome, reader and a voice exemplar paragraph, not by lists of cuts and bans; lists produced abrupt, robotic prose that mechanical checks (numbers, names, lint) still passed. Verify by reading tone. Also check which chapter owns the material a review comment could refer to before diagnosing it.
