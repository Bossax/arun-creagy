# In institutional Thai report drafting: (1) Tool-level vs Governance Boundary: Di

In institutional Thai report drafting: (1) Tool-level vs Governance Boundary: Distinguish tool-level recommendations (Event Linkage Key, End Disaster Date, modular physical fields) from institutional policy/governance (inter-agency MOUs, cross-ministerial agreements), keeping each in its appropriate chapter scope. (2) Non-moralizing operator language: Prohibit words implying personal operator negligence (e.g., 'ละเลย'); formulate procedural failure neutrally ('ขาดขั้นตอนบังคับ', 'ขาดกลไกการเชื่อมโยง'). (3) Ban 'เชิงปฏิบัติ' and abstract 'เชิง...' boilerplate: Replace with concrete operational formulations ('นำไปใช้งานจริง', 'ระดับพื้นที่', 'กระบวนงาน'). (4) 3-Stream Synthesis ('Reconstruct, Ground, Elevate'): Elevates existing draft outlines into high-density institutional prose through MECE argument maps, mechanical gate linting against LEXICON_TH, and independent editorial rubric verification.
