# When comparing historical impact indices (like the CRI Pilot vs Phase 1), explic

When comparing historical impact indices (like the CRI Pilot vs Phase 1), explicitly map the administrative boundaries of the financial proxies used. The shift from an uncapped, sector-specific proxy (OAE agricultural relief) to a strictly capped, multi-sector administrative proxy (DDPM emergency advance payments) fundamentally alters the relative geographic distribution of risk, especially when diluted against a massively expanded Total GPP denominator. Never assume the denominator or numerator identity without validating the source workbook.
