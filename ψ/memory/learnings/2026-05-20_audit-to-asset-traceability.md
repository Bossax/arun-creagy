# Learning: The Audit-to-Asset Traceability Chain
**Category**: Methodology | Strategy
**Context**: CRDB Project Strategy Pivot
**Date**: 2026-05-20

## Pattern
Closing the gap between 'Meeting Observations' and 'Contractual Deliverables' through a 4-step chain: **Trigger -> Evidence -> Change -> Deliverable.**

## Key Principles
1. **Trigger**: Register every friction point in a Trigger Log immediately to prevent loss of signal.
2. **Evidence**: Anchor the trigger in empirical data (Audits/Transcripts) before formulating a response.
3. **Change**: Formulate the response as a strategic pivot (Constraint Adjustment) and document it in the Change Log.
4. **Deliverable**: 'Tuck-in' the change into existing mandated reports to ensure it becomes a contractually binding requirement.

## Why it works
This methodology prevents 'Expert Drift' and 'Semantic Drift' in complex public sector projects. It ensures that technical decisions are not made in a vacuum but are directly linked to real-world operational frictions, making them defensible and enforceable.
