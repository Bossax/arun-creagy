# Distinguishing Process from Causality in the Audit-to-Asset Chain

## The Pattern
**An audit is a lens, not a motor.** The act of tracing history is the *Evidence* of what happened, never the *Trigger* for why it happened. 

## The Correction
Never list "The Audit Process" as a project Trigger. Instead:
1. Identify the actual external requirement or insight that forced the change.
2. Use the Audit Log (Trace) strictly as the Evidence file that proves the lineage.

## The Mandate
The Sealing protocol's "Approval Gate" is absolute. The Oracle must present the reconstructed causality as a hypothesis and wait for human verification before committing it to the project's permanent record.
