# When writing Thai public articles (writing-th), formatting must strictly enforce

When writing Thai public articles (writing-th), formatting must strictly enforce paragraph-based flow and avoid dense lists, colons (":"), single quotes ("'") to stress words, images, or Mermaid diagrams, ensuring high-level policy narratives remain accessible and readable.
