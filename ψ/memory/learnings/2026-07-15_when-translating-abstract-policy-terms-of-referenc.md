# When translating abstract policy terms of reference (e.g. MERL) into technical r

When translating abstract policy terms of reference (e.g. MERL) into technical requirements, always anchor the abstract goals to specific structural database constraints (e.g. CDM, semantic metadata, dual-track schemas). This forces vendors to build systems that structurally support complex policy goals rather than reverting to flat, disconnected forms. Additionally, always explicitly cite source frameworks (like TAMD) during the initial drafting.
