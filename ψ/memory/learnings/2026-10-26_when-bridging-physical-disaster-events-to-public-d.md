# When bridging physical disaster events to public disaster relief in Thai public

When bridging physical disaster events to public disaster relief in Thai public administration, relief funds are legally unlocked exclusively by administrative decrees (ประกาศเขตการให้ความช่วยเหลือฯ under Disaster Act B.E. 2550 & MOF Reg B.E. 2562), exhibiting an M:N Cardinality Paradox with physical events. An EVENT_DECREE_BRIDGE table with population-duration weighting is required to prevent double-counting while preserving SAO audit compliance. Furthermore, to avoid field data under-reporting driven by SAO liability fears, enforce the 'Zero-Monetary-Valuation at Local Level Rule' (local technicians record physical counts only; central DCCE models compute economic loss), and restrict Stage 1 roadmaps to internal R&D and immediate agency allies rather than premature government-wide GFMIS mandates. In multi-agent workflows, strictly separate planning/packaging (plans/) from target drafting (ψ/incubate/drafts/).
