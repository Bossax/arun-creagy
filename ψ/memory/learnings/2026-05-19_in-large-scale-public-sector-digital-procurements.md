# In large-scale public sector digital procurements, success depends on delivering

In large-scale public sector digital procurements, success depends on delivering a "Hardened Inception Package" (Blueprint-as-a-Shield) that defines the domain logic (CDM, Glossary, Rules) up front. This prevents contractor drift and ensures DCCE retains product ownership. Tiering features into Must-Have (Engine) vs Nice-to-Have (Data Products) allows for budget flexibility without compromising structural integrity. Mandating internal stewardship from higher-level leadership (The Nid Layer) is essential to reclaiming policy content ownership from contractors/AI.
