# Inventory volume is evidence of catalogued stock, not evidence of operational ca

Inventory volume is evidence of catalogued stock, not evidence of operational capability. Before claiming that a data landscape can identify who or what is at risk, test row-level provenance, accessibility, spatial and temporal granularity, currency, verification status, licensing, stewardship, duplication, and linkage to hazard and impact data. In service narratives, anchor the business case in the canonical service identity; implementation blockers and enabling mechanisms should shape the rationale without redefining the service.
