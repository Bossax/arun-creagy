# In natural hazard database design, decoupling the spatial-temporal incident boun

In natural hazard database design, decoupling the spatial-temporal incident boundaries (e.g., location, duration, population affected) from economic impact assessments (direct asset shocks/damage and indirect flow shocks/losses) prevents structural contamination by administrative relief data. Furthermore, generalized templates must be extended into sector-specific child tables (e.g., agricultural crop yields vs housing replacement costs) to prevent NULL-heavy schemas and accommodate the highly diverse variables across sectors.
