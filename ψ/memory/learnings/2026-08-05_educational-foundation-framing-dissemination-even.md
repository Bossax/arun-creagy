# Educational Foundation Framing: Dissemination events for complex data platforms

Educational Foundation Framing: Dissemination events for complex data platforms are most effective when structured in two phases: first, spending the majority of the event educating the audience on data domains, architectures (Medallion/CDM), and planning use cases to establish a shared vocabulary; second, leveraging that technical maturity in the final session to present the actual project outputs (Sitemaps, IA, Inventories) and next steps.
