# For evidence-bound institutional drafting, record source authority in the conten

For evidence-bound institutional drafting, record source authority in the content contract before drafting and enforce it in the traceability sidecar and independent review brief. Distinguish reproducible inventory counts from reported aggregates, retain reader-facing caveats for verification limits, and never collapse high-priority, detailed-specification, and deferred statuses from different artifacts. Any human revision invalidates a hash-bound editorial receipt and requires fresh mechanical and independent review before merge.
