# The Translation Layer between Climate Science and Civil Engineering

The most valuable asset in climate-resilient infrastructure consulting is the "Translation Layer" that maps climate hazard data (return periods, flood depths, wind speeds) directly into engineering design standards and asset sensitivities (fragility curves, damage functions). 

When building logic rules for a Climate Resilience Database (CRDB), "hardening" the database requires sourcing these physical constants from engineering partners (like FRED Engineering) rather than relying on high-level climate policy theory. This transformation from "Conceptual" to "Physical" data models ensures that infrastructure investments are protected by a "Technical Shield" that is defensible to both engineers and financiers.
