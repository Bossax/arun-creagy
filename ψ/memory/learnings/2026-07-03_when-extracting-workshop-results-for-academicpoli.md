# When extracting workshop results for academic/policy reports, strictly separate 

When extracting workshop results for academic/policy reports, strictly separate the format/context from the raw data. Do not apply internal project terminology (e.g., 'Reality Audit', 'Sitemap') to audience-facing documents. Always preserve the original context paragraphs and append the raw findings without AI synthesis.
