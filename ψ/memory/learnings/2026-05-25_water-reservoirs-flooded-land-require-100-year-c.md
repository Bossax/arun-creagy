# Water reservoirs (Flooded Land) require 100-year CH4 tracking and 20-year CO2 tr

Water reservoirs (Flooded Land) require 100-year CH4 tracking and 20-year CO2 tracking for LUC emissions, significantly longer than standard cropland. Owners inherit this obligation until the period expires.
