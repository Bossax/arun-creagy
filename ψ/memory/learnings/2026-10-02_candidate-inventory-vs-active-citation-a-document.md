# Candidate Inventory vs Active Citation: A document present in a project's source

Candidate Inventory vs Active Citation: A document present in a project's source inventory or research intake is merely a candidate, not a confirmed citation. When generating citations or reference lists for deliverables (e.g., infographics, exhibition roll-ups, policy decks), always verify the asset's active evidence-to-visual mapping and content plan rather than relying on upstream source lists. Furthermore, public-facing citation registries must strip internal workspace paths and internal file representations to remain cleanly usable by external stakeholders.
