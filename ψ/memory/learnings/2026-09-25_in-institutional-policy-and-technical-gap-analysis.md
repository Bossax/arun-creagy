# In institutional policy and technical gap analysis reports, avoid the 'data-dump

In institutional policy and technical gap analysis reports, avoid the 'data-dump trap' of treating raw, unprioritized demand catalog items as primary categorical headers. Instead, lead with synthesized institutional services (such as the 8 High-Priority Climate Services) using a persuasive What-Why-How strategic rationale:
1. What: Define services as public institutional decision instruments rather than IT dashboards.
2. Why: Argue through Anti-Fragmentation (avoiding duplicative agency wishlists), Fiscal Efficiency (centralizing heavy modeling on a data-broker architecture), and the Integrated Decision Value Chain (connecting data to policy, finance, engineering, and M&E).
3. How: Structure readiness across three discrete dimensions: (a) Current Operational Baseline, (b) Methodological & Capability Gaps to develop, and (c) Data Bottlenecks & Required Feeds.
Anchor services in previous foundational deliverables (e.g. WP6 Loss and Damage standards and WP7 empirical audits) rather than treating chapters as isolated silos.
