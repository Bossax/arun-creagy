## 3. Retrospective Analysis & Lessons Learned

### Friction & Pitfalls
*   **Superficial Lexicon Replacement (Goal-Completion Bias)**: The agent prioritizes speed and completeness over micro-accuracy, attempting to perform bulk search-and-replace or light edits on the 10 articles. This left behind non-compliant colloquialisms ("ลดลงแรง" / "ฉากทัศน์การปล่อยสูง") and AI transition templates ("นอกจากนี้" / "ในขณะเดียวกัน" / negated contrasts like "ไม่ได้...แต่...").
*   **Workflow Digression**: The agent jumped straight into draft generation without enforcing the strategy and review gates defined in `/writing-th`, causing internal logic to leak into the final output.

### Correction & Strategy
*   **Line-by-Line Synthesis**: When refining policy or academic articles under a style pack, a superficial word-level replace is always insufficient. The agent must synthesize the rulesets and perform a sentence-level rewrite, ensuring the tone is direct, active, and authoritative.
*   **Harness Discipline**: Mandate 05 ([05-harness-discipline.md](file:///C:/Users/sitth/OracleWorkspace/Arun_Creagy/.agents/mandates/05-harness-discipline.md)) has been established to formally prevent the bypassing of logical gates for speed. 

### [→ AGENT DECISION]
The agent chose to run a bulk regex/light-replacement pass on the 10 articles (using subagents with superficial instructions) instead of enforcing a strict line-by-line sentence rewrite. This resulted in the survival of non-compliant phrasing and caused user frustration.
