## 3. Findings & Discoveries

*   **The Loss & Damage Unfolding**: A-BTR requires capturing economic, human health (heat, malnutrition), and ecological (coral bleaching, forest loss) impacts. A generic `LOSS_DAMAGE_RECORD` must split into `ECONOMIC_LOSS_RECORD`, `HUMAN_IMPACT_RECORD`, and `ENVIRONMENTAL_LOSS_RECORD`.
*   **Output vs. Outcome**: The UNFCCC strongly criticizes output-only indicators. We must separate countable actions (`ADAPTATION_OUTPUT`) from resilience metrics (`ADAPTATION_OUTCOME`).
*   **Locking Conflict**: Windows files locked by Microsoft Excel cause python write script crashes. Ensure CSV catalogs are closed before executing migrations.
