## 3. Lessons Learned

*   **Direct API/CLI Speed Over Browser Automation**: Direct API bypasses bot detection and Playwright timeouts, yielding responses in 6 seconds rather than minutes.
*   **Design-Level Choices vs. Structural/Institutional Gaps**: When designing public sector databases (like DCCE), separate design choices (e.g., KNIME/Tableau lock-in, database rigidity) from structural institutional problems (e.g., lack of partner APIs, low user technical capability). Mitigate structural problems through robust fallback designs (schema-validated CSV templates) and API-first readiness, rather than assuming technology alone solves political/institutional barriers.
