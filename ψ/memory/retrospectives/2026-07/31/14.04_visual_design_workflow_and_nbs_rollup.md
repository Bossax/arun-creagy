## Executive Summary

This session reframed `visual_design` from a document-heavy process into an operational handoff workflow for a 60 × 160 cm Nature-based Solutions (NbS) rollup. The essential correction was to make each artifact serve a specific ChatGPT action: a rendered HTML layout controls composition, a self-contained review note controls revisions, and Stage 5 delegates asset analysis and prompt sequencing to ChatGPT instead of having the workflow invent illustration assets.

The rollup is not final. Its information architecture, Thai copy, research base, HTML prototype, and revision instructions exist; the next external generation pass must reset the vector illustration style against an isolated style sheet while retaining the rendered layout as the composition authority.

## Anti-Rationalization and Reflection

### [→ AGENT DECISION]

I initially treated Stage 5 as an agent-led asset-planning step and began deciding which illustrations to create, even though Boss had specified that Stage 5 must only instruct ChatGPT to do that thinking and prompt-writing itself.

**Why this was wrong**: It recreated the exact artifact bloat and unclear authority that the workflow was meant to remove. It also risked substituting an agent's assumptions for the visual generator's own asset decomposition.

**Corrective action**: Stage 5 now has one narrow deliverable: a delegation prompt. It directs ChatGPT to inspect the approved rollup, propose the minimal asset manifest, write only the first asset prompt after approval, and continue one image at a time.
