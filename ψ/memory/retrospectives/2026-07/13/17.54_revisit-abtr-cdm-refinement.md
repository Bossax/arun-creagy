## 3. Underlying Dynamics & Architecture Decisions
*   **Scientific Decoupling**: Rather than building custom database tables for complex geological/hydrological parameters, these were grouped under `ENVIRONMENTAL_DATA` pointing to source STAC registries. This keeps the database lean while tracking data custodianship and quality scores.
*   **Loss & Damage (DOM_024) MVD**: Structured as `DISASTER_EVENT` -> `ASSESSMENT_CONTEXT` -> `LD_PHYSICAL_DAMAGE` / `LD_ECONOMIC_LOSS` / `LD_RECOVERY_RECONSTRUCTION_NEEDS`. This accommodates multi-phase assessments (rapid vs final audits) and handles non-economic losses.
*   **MEL Outcome Bridge**: Resolved output/outcome hierarchy mismatch by mapping uncountable outcome metrics to spatial units (`ADAPTATION_OUTCOME`) and bridging them to projects via `ADAPTATION_PROJECT_OUTCOME_CONTRIBUTION`.
