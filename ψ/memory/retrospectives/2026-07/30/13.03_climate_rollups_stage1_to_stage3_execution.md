## 💡 Lessons Learned

- **Joint Stage 1 vs Single-Rollup Stage 3-5**: Stage 1 research and evidence mapping cover the full series jointly because the scientific evidence is shared, but Stage 3-5 execution (generation prompts, vector asset extraction, Canva assembly) must be executed strictly one rollup panel at a time to prevent prompt bleed and preserve precision.
