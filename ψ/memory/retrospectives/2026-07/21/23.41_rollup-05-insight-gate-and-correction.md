## What did not work

The first Roll-up 5 treatment was too process- and gap-oriented. It risked presenting a national diagnostic rather than teaching the reader a useful decision test. The user correctly redirected the work toward response risk, source-specific outcome evidence, and a clearer informational payoff.

[→ AGENT DECISION] I created a separate `infographic-insight` skill after the user referred to `ψ/lab/visual_design/SKILL.md`, instead of editing the explicitly named file. I removed that mistakenly created skill and made the intended change directly in the visual-design workflow.

## Lesson

For an evidence-led infographic, do not let a familiar visual form or a tidy process substitute for insight. Before concepting, require one source-specific finding, its mechanism, its consequence for the reader, and the visual proof that makes it legible. If the panel could be written from general knowledge, it has not earned the reader's time.
