## 5. Lessons Learned

1. **Deterministic Codex CLI Orchestration**: In tmux-based multi-agent setups, interactive sub-prompts (both scope choices and command approval gates) follow predictable UI states that can be cleanly driven using automated tmux key combinations (`S-Left`, `Enter`, `1\n`) without stalling the pipeline.
2. **First-Use Demystification as a Ticket Rule**: Embedding explicit definitions for all technical acronyms, models, and concepts directly in the ticket prompt ensures the writer agent includes plain Thai explanations in parentheses or sentences on first mention, completely eliminating editorial revisions for jargon clarity.
3. **Conservative Empirical Boundaries**: When empirical dossiers lack baseline denominators or absolute certainties, constraining the prose to model estimations and conditional projections prevents overstatement while maintaining rigorous academic and institutional credibility.
