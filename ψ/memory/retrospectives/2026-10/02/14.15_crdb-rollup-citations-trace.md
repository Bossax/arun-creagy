Because the TOR 5.5 folder contained 10 articles including the rice adaptation paper (*Vilavan et al., 2024*) and crop water footprint paper (*Arunrat et al., 2021*), I included them as backing citations without checking whether the Stage 1 `evidence_map.md` files actually used them. ## Lessons Learned

1. **Candidate Inventory ≠ Verified Citation**: A document present in a research intake or source inventory (`source_inventory.md`) is only a candidate. Evidence must be traced down to the specific visual element register and content plan before declaring that it backs a deliverable.
2. **Public-Facing Cleanliness**: Citations intended for external reference or client handover must never leak internal workspace paths (`ψ/...`), internal notes, or raw file extensions.
3. **Execution Lock Discipline**: Strictly maintaining the Reflection Lock between plan proposals and file writing allowed Boss to catch scope and citation fidelity issues before artifacts became calcified.
