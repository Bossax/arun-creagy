## Lessons Learned
1. **Literal Substring Collisions in Lexicon Checkers**: When a style rule bans a generic term like `ฉบับ` (preferring `รายการ`), any standard compound containing that substring (such as `ต้นฉบับ` or `ฉบับแรก`) triggers a mechanical failure. Proactively phrasing as `ข้อมูลต้นทาง` or `ระยะแรก` avoids false positive collisions while adhering to institutional style.
2. **Canonical Lexicon Standardization Over Organic Transliterations**: Technical drafts written across multiple phases frequently introduce ad-hoc transliterations (such as `เมทาดาทา`). Checking project memory revealed that Boss explicitly established `ข้อมูลอภิพันธ์ (Metadata)` as canonical on 2026-08-30. Systematically enforcing this across §3.4 restored terminological coherence across the entire report.
3. **Chunked Two-Way Precision Execution**: Breaking substantial sections (such as §3.3's 91 lines of 8 services and §3.4's 46 lines of statistical catalog analysis) into 2–3 structured chunks allows simultaneous pruning of bloating adjectives (`เชิงประจักษ์`, `อย่างมีนัยสำคัญ`) and enrichment of technical qualifiers (`Data Lineage`, `Controlled Vocabularies`, `Spatial Resolution`) without risking cognitive overload or accidental truncation.
