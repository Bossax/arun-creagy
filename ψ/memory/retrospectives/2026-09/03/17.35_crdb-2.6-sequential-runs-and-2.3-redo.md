## Lessons Learned
1. **Sequential Multi-Run Architecture Prevents Fatigue and Hallucination**: Large institutional report sections (like §2.6 with its 6 complex website sections, 3-tier subpage specifications, and 3-part data governance model) become diluted or corrupt when written in a single broad pass. Splitting into sequential runs (Vision ➔ Sitemap & Subpages ➔ Governance & Handoff) maintains deep empirical fidelity and eliminates syntax/lexicon defects.
2. **Dissemination Deck Cross-Check as Grounding Anchor**: The raw drafts often contain only high-level conceptual summaries. Cross-checking against the final Dissemination Deck recovers vital empirical evidence (e.g., 20-year economic loss statistics of 1.4 trillion baht, 250+ adaptation measures, 77-province M&E platform with 25 indicators, 59 Belem indicators) that makes the document truly build-ready for subsequent procurement.
3. **Hard Boundary Isolation Between Sections**: Enforcing strict content boundaries (§2.3 owns design process and reasoning; §2.5 owns workshop logistics; §2.6 owns finished deliverables and minimum specifications; Chapter 5 owns procurement and implementation roadmap) ensures that the report does not repeat itself and maintains high decision-making altitude.
