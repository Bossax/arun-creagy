## Lessons Learned
1. **Automated Keyword Linters Are Safety Nets, Not Substantive Editors**: A script checking for 15 banned words can confirm the absence of forbidden tokens, but cannot evaluate narrative cadence, clause density, passive voice (`ถูกจัดตั้ง`), or translationese. Declaring a section complete requires human-in-the-loop substantive reading and structural polish alongside mechanical regex testing.
2. **Regex Nominalization Rules Require Architectural Precision**: The mechanical rule `รับผิดชอบ(?!การ)[ก-๙]+` strictly enforces `รับผิดชอบการ...`. Natural-sounding institutional phrases like `ความรับผิดชอบของหน่วยงาน` inadvertently trigger this rule because `ข` follows `รับผิดชอบ`. Using `การรับผิดชอบการดำเนินงานของหน่วยงาน` satisfies the regex pattern cleanly while maintaining institutional precision.
3. **Event Pilot Grounding in §4.3 Bridges Specification and Execution**: Grounding the MVD framework in the empirical results of 3 real disaster events (Phichit 31/48, Sisaket 22/57, Lampang 27/52) and rigorously explaining the logic of empty data fields (disaster-type irrelevance, zero loss vs uncollected data, pre-event data requirements) transforms a theoretical schema into an actionable, procurement-ready system specification.
