## Lessons Learned

- For multi-section report drafting, the logic chain between sections must be preserved explicitly; otherwise each section tends to become a stand-alone summary instead of part of a cumulative argument.
- When a subtask completes the substantive writing, the visible reminder ledger should be synchronized immediately so the operational record matches the artifact reality.
- Backup drafts are valuable when generating long Thai policy prose because they preserve working history without contaminating the final section file.
- Absence of local session logs should be treated as a normal degraded mode for `/rrr`, not as a reason to invent timestamps.
- In report work, process traceability is not overhead; it is part of the evidentiary credibility of the final deliverable.
