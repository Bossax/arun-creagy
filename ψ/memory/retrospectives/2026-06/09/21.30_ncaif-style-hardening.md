## ⚠️ Challenges & Blockers
- **Redundancy Blindness**: I initially created a separate column for "Regulatory Justification," which caused repetitive "Left-to-Right" reading fatigue.
- **Filler Phrase Leaks**: Defaulted to "สถานะความพร้อม" (Readiness Status) as a safe-but-empty placeholder when metaphors were removed.

## 💡 Lessons Learned
- **Bake-in the Why**: Regulatory legitimacy (ความชอบธรรมตามกฎข้อบังคับ) is more effective when woven into the purpose/narrative rather than isolated in a column.
- **Purpose-First Syntax**: Starting sections with proactive intent ("เพื่อแก้ไข...", "เพื่อคุ้มครอง...") instantly elevates the tone from "technical" to "strategic."
- **Institutional Thai ≠ Formal Translation**: Professional Thai requires specific bureaucratic procedural verbs, not just high-level vocabulary.
