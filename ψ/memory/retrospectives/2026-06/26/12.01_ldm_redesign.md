## Lessons Learned

- **Database Separation:** A disaster event database must isolate event parameters (the bounding box) from financial assessments. This ensures sectoral ministries can conduct independent DaLA evaluations without corrupting the primary incident log.
- **Sectoral Sub-schemas:** Generalized damage and loss tables are only useful as base templates. Specific child tables (e.g., crop yield loss, housing reconstruction) must be implemented to prevent schema bloat or structural mismatch.
- **Compensation Decoupling:** Administrative relief payments do not reflect economic losses; keeping them out of the DaLA core tables is essential for maintaining mathematical and analytical validity.
