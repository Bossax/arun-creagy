## Lessons Learned
- **Symmetry is a Quality Seal**: When two clusters (like Vulnerability and Resilience) are functionally related, their logical structure should be symmetrical. This reduces cognitive load for developers and analysts.
- **The "Truth" resides in the Sealing**: In this project, "Sealing" a pillar means it becomes the master for all others. Pillar 4 (Glossary) is the semantic master; Pillar 5 (CDM) must bow to its naming, even if canonical ancestors suggest otherwise.
