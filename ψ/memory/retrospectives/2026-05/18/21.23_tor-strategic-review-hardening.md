## Lessons Learned
- **Domain vs. Infrastructure**: In large government contracts, \"IT expertise\" is often mistaken for \"Domain expertise.\" The TOR must explicitly split these roles.
- **Decoupled by Default**: For national data systems, \"Website\" and \"Data Space\" must be decoupled in the TOR to prevent monolithic vendor lock-in.
- **Stakeholder Needs as Code**: Validated needs are more durable anchors than \"MVP\" labels.
