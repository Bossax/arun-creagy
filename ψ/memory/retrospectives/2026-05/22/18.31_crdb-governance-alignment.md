## Lessons Learned
Formal technical terminology is a critical tool for project sustainability. In government environments, using industry-standard terms (DAMA-DMBOK) provides "borrowed authority" that helps bridge bureaucratic divides. Furthermore, "Data Governance" should be sold as "Empowerment" (Data Sovereignty) rather than "Workload" (Data Stewardship) to secure buy-in from senior leadership who may be wary of additional technical burdens.
