## Lessons Learned
- **Deliverables as Pillars**: In high-stakes government procurement projects (like CRDB), the "Framework" must be a direct map of the "Contract." Technical hardening should be an *attribute* of the deliverable, not a separate silo.
- **Information Scent vs. Wiring**: A Sitemap is a visual promise, but an Interface Map is the technical contract. They must be hardened together to prevent contractor "discovery."
- **Logical Sovereignty**: DCCE retains power only if they own the "Logic & Rules" (Pillars 1-8). The SI's only power should be "Execution" (Pillar 9).
