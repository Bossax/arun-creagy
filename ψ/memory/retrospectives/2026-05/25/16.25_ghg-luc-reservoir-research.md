## Lessons Learned
- **Reservoir Duration:** Water reservoirs (Flooded Land) have a 100-year CH4 tracking period, significantly longer than the 20-year standard for Cropland CO2.
- **Ownership Obligation:** The duty to report LUC stays with the current owner/controller as long as the reservoir is within its 20/100 year assessment period.
- **Land Classification:** "Farmland" is a broad term; in accounting, reservoirs must be isolated as "Flooded Land" (Wetlands) even if located within a farm.
