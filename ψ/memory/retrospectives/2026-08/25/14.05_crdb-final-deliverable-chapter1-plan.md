## What worked

The most useful design decision was treating the Executive Summary as a derivative synthesis rather than a shortened copy. This preserves one truth across both reports while giving each report a distinct reader and purpose.

The plan also separated three layers that had been mixed in the original guideline:

- research findings;
- consultation and revision evidence;
- the final NCAIF and data-management structure.

That separation makes the Full Report auditable without allowing the Executive Summary to become a meeting log or artifact catalogue.
