## Lessons Learned

**Pattern**: When asked a general/abstract question about a specific named example ("does X need Y" with a concrete instance named), check whether the example is real in the project's own corpus before reasoning about it in the abstract. The abstract reasoning can be sound and still be answering the wrong question if the example turns out to be a real, uninspected artifact.

**Why it matters**: In this session, treating "Thailand climatology dashboard" as a hypothetical for two full turns delayed finding that it was DEL-1, a real, specified, partially-blocked DRD deliverable with zero justification anywhere in the project — a genuine gap, not a thought experiment.

**How to apply**: On any question that names a specific system, page, product, or component, do a quick existence check (grep the corpus) before answering in general terms, even when the question reads as philosophical or exploratory.
