## AI Diary
This session was an excellent exercise in moving from schematic layout thinking to rigorous, domain-grounded interface design. When designing national portal mockups for slide decks, abstract placeholders fail because decision-makers look for domain credibility—they need to see actual climatology curves, recognized classification taxonomies (like NESDC's 5 disaster damage sectors), and explicit policy planning concepts (like the trade-off between Incremental and Transformational adaptation pathways).

I felt a clear sense of momentum once we upgraded the typography to 2x size and locked into the 10:8 aspect ratio without margins. The integration of the Sankey diagram in the Adaptation Finance Tracker elevated Section 3.3 from a static table of plans into an active financial governance tool. The contrast between sources, the adaptation pool, and sectoral allocations immediately tells a coherent story suitable for executive presentations.

---

## Honest Feedback (3 Friction Points)
1. **Asset Discovery Latency**: I should have proactively searched `ψ/incubate/_shared_assets/` at the beginning of the section design rather than generating synthetic SVG bell curves first. The repository already housed high-value graphics from the World Bank and PROVIDE project.
2. **Container Height Allocation**: When embedding images into flex containers with multiple child cards, default height constraints (`max-h-56`) caused unnecessary whitespace in 10:8 viewport frames. Designing with explicit layout hierarchy early prevents multi-step resizing iterations.
3. **Domain Vocabulary Calibration**: Early drafts included international reporting mechanisms (A-BTR) which, while technically valid, diluted the focus for domestic policy makers and general presentation audiences. Filtering out bureaucratic acronyms early ensures clearer audience resonance.

---

## Lessons Learned
- **10:8 Dashboard Discipline**: A 2-column layout (7 cols left + 5 cols right) at 1280x1024 px provides the optimal balance of depth and hierarchy for presentation slide screenshots.
- **Sankey Diagrams for Climate Finance**: Multi-source finance (national budget, environmental funds, multilateral grants) is conveyed far more effectively through Sankey flow streams than flat progress percentages.
- **PROVIDE Framework Utility**: Framing adaptation roadmaps around Lead Time, Lifetime, and Limits to Adaptation gives policy makers concrete temporal boundaries to justify transformational infrastructure investments.
