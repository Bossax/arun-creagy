## Lessons learned

1. Read the task-specific execution plan before deriving workflow choices from the broader writing plan.
2. A Style Pack is the judgment layer; the lexicon is a narrow mechanical enforcement layer. New patterns require repeated independent human evidence before promotion.
3. Human comments and typing slips are different data: comments identify defects, while typos must be excluded from any learned preference.
4. Contract-plan conflicts must be resolved before drafting; otherwise a compliant draft can still violate the governing report scope.
5. Do not allow the drafting agent or a same-family reviewer to be the final judge of causal validity and evidence sufficiency.
