## Lessons Learned

1. **Separate mechanical assurance from editorial assurance in both language and control flow.** A linter can certify only the patterns it encodes. Rename its verdict accordingly and require a distinct semantic receipt.
2. **Bind review evidence to immutable content identity.** A review without draft and contract hashes becomes stale silently; a hash-bound receipt fails immediately after any revision.
3. **Use transformation modes instead of a universal compression rule.** Character-ratio checks are meaningful for comparable rewrites, misleading for executive synthesis, and irrelevant for new writing.
4. **Independent review earns its cost when the drafting agent has already rationalized its own prose.** The blind reviewer found a source-fidelity failure that the drafting agent and all mechanical rules accepted.
5. **Content contracts can be approved as a bundle while execution remains sequential.** Boss can approve contracts for 1.2–1.4 once, then the harness can process each section independently without weakening section-job isolation.
6. **Skill synchronization must include referenced resources.** Entry-point equality is insufficient when runtime instructions live in `references/`.
7. **A robust gate must fail safely and explain why.** The new merge path refuses missing receipts, unresolved critical or major findings, unreviewed mechanical warnings, contract mismatch, and stale hashes without touching the destination.
