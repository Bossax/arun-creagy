## Lessons learned

1. **A Writing Plan is an instruction manual for a drafter, not the draft report itself or a project retrospective**: A writing plan must use imperative instructional verbs ("สรุปผล...", "ชี้ให้เห็น...", "เปรียบเทียบ...", "แจกแจง..."), specify required visuals/tables, and define clear negative boundaries ("ไม่รวม"), rather than arguing the substantive points in long prose paragraphs.
2. **Strict Institutional Naming Governance**: Always enforce full Thai formal acronyms ("กรม สส." rather than informal "สส.") as mandated by institutional writing standards and explicit user directives.
3. **Cross-Deliverable Template Discipline**: Before drafting or revising a chapter writing plan, always inspect preceding approved plans in the same deliverable pack (Chapters 1 and 2) to strictly inherit their section structure, instructional tone, and formatting constraints.
