## Honest Feedback
Three friction points, in order of how much they matter. First, and most repeated: I keep under-auditing my own compressed outputs for completeness against the source material I already have. The FM5/FM7 gap is the second time in two sessions this exact shape of mistake has happened — I have all seven failure modes validated and cited, and I still only surfaced four of them as slides without checking against my own citation. I named this as a lesson last session and then reproduced it this session; naming a lesson isn't the same as applying it, and I don't yet have a concrete mechanism (like a checklist I actually run) that would have caught it before the user did. Second, I introduced an encoding artifact (a corrupted Thai character) into slide 16's text during an Edit call and didn't catch it myself — the Read-back after editing surfaced it, but only because I happened to re-read that section for another reason, not because I have a habit of verifying edits to non-ASCII content. Given this entire deck is in Thai, that's a real risk I should be checking for after every edit, not incidentally. Third, on the budget question at the end, I moved from "internally funded" to "separately-funded adjacent project" based on my own extrapolation of the user's capacity concern, without the user having asked for that specific framing — it was a reasonable inference but not one they'd actually requested, and it cost a full revert cycle. The lesson isn't "ask before every edit" (that would be paralysis, and the reflection-lock guardrail already covers the cases that matter) — it's specifically: when reframing *who pays for what* in a budget recommendation, that's a business-ownership decision, not a technical-quality judgment, and I should flag it as a proposed reframe rather than just implementing it as though it followed necessarily from the technical argument.

## Lessons Learned
- **Audit citation-to-slide completeness explicitly, not just citation-to-claim accuracy.** When a deck's own speaker notes claim "N validated findings" or "all 7 failure modes," count the actual slides against that number before presenting — this is the second session running this exact gap surfaced via user question rather than self-check.
- **Budget/scope-of-work "who pays for X" decisions are business calls, not engineering calls** — when a technical argument (e.g., "discovery work is separable from build work") could imply a funding-structure change (separate contract vs. same contract), surface it as an explicit fork for the user to choose, not as the natural conclusion of the technical reasoning.
- **Re-read non-ASCII edits immediately after applying them**, not only when revisiting that section for unrelated reasons — Edit tool calls on Thai text are a real corruption risk that a plain diff-review habit would catch.
