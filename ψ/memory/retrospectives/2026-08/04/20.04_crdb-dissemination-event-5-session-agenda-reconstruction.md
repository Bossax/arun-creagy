## 3. Key Learnings & Strategic Insights

* **Strict Scope Discipline for Line-Agency Events**: Dissemination events hosted under specific consulting contracts (like CRDB) must remain strictly grounded in that project's deliverables and scope rather than mixing in broader multi-year agency budget defense narratives.
* **5-Stage Narrative Alignment**: Structuring complex data governance events into a clear progression—*Vision $\rightarrow$ Ecosystem $\rightarrow$ Architecture $\rightarrow$ Use Cases & Reciprocity $\rightarrow$ Agency Strategy*—ensures that technical concepts remain accessible to line-agency planners while demonstrating concrete institutional value.
