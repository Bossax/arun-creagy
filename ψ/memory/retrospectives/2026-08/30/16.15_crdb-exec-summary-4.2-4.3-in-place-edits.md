## Lessons Learned

- **Respect Human In-Flight Additions**: When a draft contains human-written clauses (like the `Iterative Design Loop` explanation), do not overwrite or replace them with standard boilerplate. Expand and refine around the user's explicit intent.
- **Affirmative Presentation over Comparative Contrast**: When situating project achievements, state what the project delivered directly (e.g., "covers ~70% of Phase 1 and ~80% of Phase 2") rather than constructing artificial "before vs. after" contrasting narratives that risk drifting into speculative scope comparisons.
- **Verify Evidence Locally First**: Before triggering heavy external search or trace workflows, verify the designated primary source documents listed in the writing contract (`source_paths`). The TOR70 SDLC briefing deck contained the exact coverage table needed.
- **Automated Lexical Linting as a Strict Gate**: Strict lexicon rules will catch compound phrases (e.g., `ฉบับสมบูรณ์`); adjusting prose to cleaner, unencumbered alternatives (`ที่สมบูรณ์`) ensures institutional tone consistency across all sections.
