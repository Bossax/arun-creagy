## 4. Lessons Learned

1. **Distinguish Tool-Level Fixes from Institutional Governance**: Chapter 3 is strictly about the data model, minimum viable dataset, and field reporting forms. Recommendations here must be tool-level (Event Linkage Key, End Disaster Date validation, modular sector units). Inter-agency policy agreements (MOUs) belong strictly to Chapter 4.
2. **Institutional Voice Prohibits Moralizing Operators**: Using words like "ละเลย" assigns moral blame to local field staff. Institutional reports must articulate systemic failure neutrally ("ขาดขั้นตอนบังคับ", "ขาดกลไกเชื่อมโยง").
3. **Strict Ban on "เชิงปฏิบัติ" and Abstract "เชิง..." Boilerplate**: In Thai institutional drafting, "เชิงปฏิบัติ" is an unnatural, empty crutch that weakens authority. Writing concrete actions ("นำไปใช้งานจริง", "ระดับพื้นที่", "กระบวนงาน") delivers far superior impact.
4. **The 3-Stream Synthesis Model Works**: Reconstructing the argument map from the existing v5.0 outline, grounding it in specific TOR facts, and elevating the prose through the 3 gates (Argument, Lint, Editorial) allowed 5 sections to be elevated seamlessly without losing coherence.
