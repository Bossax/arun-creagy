## Architecture Decisions

- **Global `.agents/` Canonical Specifications**: Keep subagent system prompts in `references/subagent-prompts.md` at the workspace level rather than locking them inside host-specific silos, ensuring Antigravity, Claude Code, and Codex read from a single source of truth.
- **Model Tiering Strategy**: Assigned high reasoning (`pro` / `sonnet`) to Stage 1 (Mapper) and Stage 5 (Reviewer), while assigning fast, natural idiom (`flash`) to Stage 3 (Verbalizer).
- **Automated Mirror Sync Discipline**: Maintained strict zero-drift enforcement between `.agents/skills/` and `.claude/skills/`.
