---

## Lessons Learned

1. **The Argument Map is a Cognitive & Token Boundary**: Decoupling argument planning from prose drafting prevents working memory overload and cuts context token usage roughly in half by ensuring the verbalizer only loads the approved map and prose kernel, not raw research sources.
2. **Missing Warrants Create "Light" Prose**: In Toulmin's model, a Claim without an explicit Warrant (why the grounds compel the assertion) leaves the reader asking "So what?". Forcing a mandatory `warrant` and `application_to_design` field eliminates ungrounded abstractions.
3. **Hooks Over Prose Instructions**: Prose guidelines are easily forgotten or skipped under load. Non-bypassable PreToolUse hooks (`.agents/hooks.json`) that physically deny drafting until `argument-map.json` is approved ensure harness integrity.
