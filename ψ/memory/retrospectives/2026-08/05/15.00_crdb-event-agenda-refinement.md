## 3. Key Learnings & Strategic Insights

* **The Educational Foundation Sequence**: Spending the morning and early afternoon educating the audience on climate data domains, Medallion architectures, and planning cycles (Sessions 1–3) builds the shared vocabulary and technical maturity required to understand and appreciate the actual project outputs (Session 4).
* **Tangible Government Deliverables**: At dissemination events, government focal points and policymakers respond much better to concrete, visual front-end structures like **Sitemaps & Information Architecture (IA)** rather than abstract back-end database schemas (`CDM/LDM`), even if both represent the same underlying logic.
