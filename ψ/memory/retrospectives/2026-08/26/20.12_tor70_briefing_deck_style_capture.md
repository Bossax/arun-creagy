## Lessons Learned

- **Whitespace is an ambiguous delimiter in Thai tables**: Unlike English, Thai words are written without inter-word spaces. When listing multiple deliverables or metrics in table cells, separating items only by spaces causes run-on confusion. Explicit commas and conjunctions (และ, รวมถึง, พร้อมด้วย) are essential for visual scanning.
- **Introductory fragments break reading cadence**: Starting slides or summaries with detached count phrases (สไลด์หลัก 14 แผ่น...) creates a jarring preamble. Merging them into a single compound sentence (เอกสารนี้ประกอบด้วย... ซึ่งจัดทำขึ้นเพื่อ...) anchors the reader immediately.
- **Maintain parallelism across slide bullet lists**: Mixing full declarative sentences with bare noun phrases in the same list makes the presentation feel disjointed. Enforce consistent grammatical shape (e.g. relative clauses ที่สามารถ... / ซึ่งผ่าน...).
