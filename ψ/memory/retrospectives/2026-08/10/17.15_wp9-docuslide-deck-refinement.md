## 4. Key Lessons Learned

- **Executive Mindset Shift**: Technical specifications must translate schema tables (`VARCHAR`, `DECIMAL`) into executive governance reasons (Single Source of Truth, GIS layering, Audit trail, True Loss market pricing, BBB budgeting).
- **Pure Thai Headings Rule**: Removing parenthetical English translations from `# Slide X` titles improves executive readability without diluting domain rigor when essential acronyms (`DaLA`, `DesInventar`, `MVD`) remain anchored in body prose.
