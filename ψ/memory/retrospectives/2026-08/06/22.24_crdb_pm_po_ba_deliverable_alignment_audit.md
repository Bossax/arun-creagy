## 4. Key Takeaways & Lessons Learned

- **Framework Trinity Alignment**: Data platforms require anchoring to PMI/PMBOK (Business Case, RTM), IIBA/BABOK (BRD, FRS, NFRs, Data Dictionary), and DAMA-DMBOK (Governance Charter, Stewardship RACI, STM, Lineage, DQ 6 Dimensions).
- **Core Micro-Specification Gaps**: While CRDB's macro architecture is strong, developer-level specifications require adding **Source-to-Target Mapping (STM)**, **Data-Specific Acceptance Criteria (row counts, null fallbacks)**, and an **Assumption Log (Client Dependency Register)** for TOR70 vendor handoff.
