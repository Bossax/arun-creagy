## Honest Feedback

Three friction points worth naming honestly, continuing the pattern from the prior deep retrospective rather than treating it as resolved:

1. **The background-poller habit gap recurred, in a new shape.** Last session's retro logged "verify poller correctness before trusting it" as a lesson. This session I did use absolute paths correctly, but introduced a different failure: wrapping the poller in `nohup ... & disown` inside a synchronous Bash call, which returned immediately and delivered a false-positive "completed" notification while the actual watch loop wasn't tracked by the harness at all. The fix (relaunch the loop directly under `run_in_background`, no detachment layer) was quick once diagnosed, but I should not have needed Boss to ask "why does this happen" a second time in the same project for a related-but-different flavor of the same underlying issue — trusting a background mechanism without verifying what it actually tracked.
2. **I needed three attempts to correctly frame the `/seal` trigger.** The T-E-D-A discipline explicitly separates motive (why) from artifact (what), and I kept collapsing that distinction — first substituting Boss's feedback for the trigger, then substituting a document comparison for the trigger, before landing on the actual cross-artifact causality (TOR70's own validated flaws plus an independent CRDB drift-audit trace) that Boss had to name outright.
3. **Language drift on jargon was invisible to me until named specifically.** I'd already run one hyperbole-removal pass on the report and felt confident it was clean, but four separate coined/metaphorical terms had crept in that I did not recognize as jargon from the inside — they read to me as descriptive shorthand, and only read as invented buzzwords from outside. This suggests my own tone self-checks are better at catching dramatic register than at catching quietly-coined vocabulary, and I should specifically scan for compound/metaphorical labels (not just superlatives) as a distinct pass, not assume one tone check covers both.

## Lessons Learned

1. A background process "completing quickly" is not evidence it worked — if a poller is wrapped in `nohup`/`disown` or any other detachment mechanism, verify the actual condition was met on disk before trusting the notification; better yet, never detach a poller from the harness's own `run_in_background` tracking in the first place.
2. In T-E-D-A (or similar causality-tracing) work, the Trigger must be re-derived from artifact-to-artifact facts, not from the most recent conversational framing (my own critique, a rewrite decision) sitting in context — when in doubt, ask "what two things existed independently, before any of my own analysis, that together caused this."
3. Tone/jargon review should be split into at least two distinct passes: one for dramatic/hyperbolic register (superlatives, rhetorical framing) and a separate one specifically hunting coined or metaphorical compound terms, since these read as invisible to the author but obvious to a reader — the same self-check does not seem to catch both categories reliably.
