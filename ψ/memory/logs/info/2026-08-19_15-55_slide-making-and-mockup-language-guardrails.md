🚩 **Slide & Webpage Mockup Language Guardrails: Zero Jargon, Pure User-Centric Terminology**

When creating visual mockups and content for presentation slides, adhere strictly to the following language and framing discipline:

### 1. Zero Hyperbolic / Marketing Jargon (Adhere to Source Terminology)
- **Prohibition**: Never introduce startup or marketing buzzwords not grounded in the source specifications (e.g., *Pulse, Vitality, Ecosystem, Infrastructure* when unspecified).
- **Enforcement**: Strictly use the locked institutional and technical terms established by DCCE/CRDB (e.g., *ระบบศูนย์กลางข้อมูลการปรับตัวต่อสภาพภูมิอากาศของประเทศไทย (NCAIF)*, *สรุปข้อมูลบริบทประเทศ (National Overview Summary)*, *ค้นหาข้อมูลรายพื้นที่*).

### 2. Purge Internal Architecture and Developer Logic from Presentation Artifacts
- **Prohibition**: Never expose internal information architecture (IA) logic, developer taxonomy labels, or database codes on executive slides or user-facing mockups.
- **Examples of Banned Internal Labels on Presentation Artifacts**:
  - Do NOT display IA design labels like `"Task-Based Routing"` or `"Task-Oriented Pathways"`.
  - Do NOT display structural node codes like `"(1B)"` in user navigation menus, or raw deliverable IDs like `"SIT-xxx"`.
  - Do NOT display internal database variable IDs (e.g., `DDPM_2_3`, `RFD_1_2`) in public-facing dataset cards.
- **Enforcement**: Translate all internal logic into natural, human-friendly, user-centric terms (e.g., `"บริการและข้อมูลสำหรับผู้ใช้งานแต่ละกลุ่ม"`, `"ภาพรวมประเทศ"`, and plain Thai agency/dataset titles).

### 3. Visual Mockup Slide Optimization (Slide-Ready Framing)
- Ensure mockups are visually optimized for 16:9 presentation slide screencaps with clear typography, high contrast, clean container frames, and distinct visual hierarchy.
