## Candidate Rules

- **structural**: Do not restate a count, department name/acronym, or framework name in a section if it was already established earlier in the report — cross-reference instead of repeating.
- **structural**: When a numbered list ties each item to an earlier numbered list (e.g., "service X blocked by gap dimension N"), prefer self-contained narrative statement of the obstacle over citing the index number, unless the section's job is explicitly cross-reference/traceability.
- **structural**: In a parallel bullet list describing states of the same object, anchor every bullet to one consistent subject noun phrase, even if the draft used varied phrasing.
- **lexical/register**: Cut "ตามที่มักเข้าใจกัน" (myth-correction / contra-assumption framing) and its surrounding defensive scaffolding; replace with a direct forward-looking institutional statement.
