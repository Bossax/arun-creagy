## 1. How the Workshop Was Designed (The Mandate)
- **Design Philosophy Shift (T-019)**: The approach shifted on April 30 from "heavy co-creation" to **"guided validation + guided expansion"** to reduce the technical burden on participants. The afternoon session functioned explicitly as a **"Feasibility Assessment"** rather than a design ratification session.
- **Session 3 (S3)**: Dedicated to a deep dive into the **NCAIF Sitemap**. Participants were asked to take their dataset sticky notes from the morning and categorize them within the presented sitemap. The goal was to identify **Taxonomy Gaps** (datasets that couldn't be categorized or sections with no datasets).
- **Session 4 (S4)**: Focused on **Governance Implementation Assessment**. Participants used red indicators for implementation barriers (e.g., budget, legal processes) and green indicators for high-value services (e.g., limitations shield).

## 2. Outcome and Stakeholder's Opinions
- **Pivot to Production (CH-011 / CH-012)**: The workshop outputs triggered a massive reorientation towards the July 6 deadline, moving the project from "design" to "production & ratification". Content was now reframed as **"governed assets"** rather than just loose website pages.
- **The "Grounding Crisis" and Sitemap Pivot (T-028 / CH-021)**: Post-workshop analysis (and subsequent readiness audits) revealed that simplifying the sitemap caused a loss of vital scientific granularity. This led to a mandatory restoration of the **May 12 scientific granularity** (creating Sitemap v6.1 and rejecting meta-references) to prevent a "Synthesis Bottleneck". 
- **Stakeholder Sentiments (Sampled from Group 7-8 Activity 2)**: 
  - Major implementation barriers noted were institutional restrictions, data standard fragmentation, and budget/manpower limitations.
  - Strong demand was expressed for API endpoints, spatial dashboards at granular levels, and historical/forecast data integration.
