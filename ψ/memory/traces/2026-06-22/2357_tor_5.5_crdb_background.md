## 1. Context & Origin
Under the DCCE Climate Risk Database (CRDB) project, **TOR 5.5** specifies the creation of a "Knowledge Set" on climate risk and adaptation impacts in Thailand. It consists of two sub-items:
- **TOR 5.5.1**: Review and collect studies over the last 10 years across 6 National Adaptation Plan (NAP) sectors (Water, Agriculture, Tourism, Health, Natural Resources, and Human Settlements).
- **TOR 5.5.2**: Select at least 10 significant studies and produce a Summary Report (Technical Synopses) to be used for producing public dissemination media (articles, roll-ups, infographics).

### E. The Deliverable (D-041)
- **Status**: Sealed
- **Evidence**: [สรุปสาระสำคัญของชุดบทความและรายงานสถานการณ์ความเสี่ยง-TOR5.5-Article-Synopses.md](file:///C:/Users/sitth/OracleWorkspace/Arun_Creagy/ψ/writing/สรุปสาระสำคัญของชุดบทความและรายงานสถานการณ์ความเสี่ยง-TOR5.5-Article-Synopses.md)
- **Content**: A formal technical synopsis of 10 selected studies spanning the 4 main groupings:
  1. *Water & Food Security*: CMIP6 water footprint crop impacts (Arunrat et al., 2021) and sustainable Chao Phraya rice adaptation (Vilavan et al., 2024).
  2. *Public Health*: Geographic projections of 9 infectious diseases (PLOS NTD, 2024) and severe dengue early warning triggers (PMC, 2019).
  3. *Natural Resources*: Coastal erosion hard vs. soft defense structures (PMC, 2022), wildlife habitat climate corridors (PMC, 2018), and adaptive marine governance via tourist closures for reef recovery (Reef Resilience, 2020).
  4. *Tourism & Human Settlements*: Climate risk ranking of tourist destinations (Griffith Univ/GIZ, 2018), Urban sprawl flood impacts in BMR (J Flood Risk Manag, 2024), and industrial depth-damage vulnerability curves for loss & damage (Int J Disaster Risk Reduct, 2025).
