# แผนงานด้านการเปลี่ยนแปลงสภาพภูมิอากาศระดับจังหวัด (Provincial Climate Change Action Plan)

Funded by the environmental fund, TGO led the development of provincial climate action plans in capable provinces. The local MNRE officers are responsible for the projects.  general topics are
1. Provincial GHG Inventory
2. GHG emission reduction plan
3. Climate Risk Profile
4. Climate Adaptation Action Plan

However, there is no standard of making these assessments. Different provinces produce reports with different content, details, and methodology. For example, Lopburi province started the data collection with onsite interviews with locals and provincial officers to scope the analysisม while Phayao province is more data intensive and does not include interviews as primary data collection tool for scoping vulnerability and risk assessment. The analysis of Lopburi province relies on DCCE risk map for prioritization without details on quantitative analysis to assess the magnitude of the hazards while Phayoa province's approach gives weight to quantitative information. 
Both provinces apply *Impact Chain Analysis* to identify and assess risk. However, justification of impact magnitude still relies on historical data, not projections since such information is unavailable.  
*Points*
- Risk prioritization is not conducted in Lopburi province's analysis
- Phetchaburi province's analysis uses data that could go down to district level and identifies impacts to the 6 sectors
- Counter measures and prioritization criteria 
- Project level indicators
- CC Board of Phetchaburi has selected 2 priority projects to implement. these two projects are funded by the central government budget through สำนักงานเกษตรจังหวัดเพชรบูรณ์ (DOAE) and other local central government agencies.
- Since there is no legal mandate to take the projects from this analysis into consideration under other plans, they could just be left there.

# GCF-UNDP Increasing resilience to climate change impacts in marine and coastal areas along the Gulf of Thailand
This project enhances the climate information and adaptation plan of coastal provinces.
The outputs are
1. Concept note: include risk profile, adaptation measures and prioritization criteria, proposed adaptation areas (with main components and activities), preliminary project concepts, expected outputs and outcomes
2. Integration doc: how can the proposed adaptation projects be integrated into agencies' action plan. It assigns the activities under the proposed adaptation areas to the lead agency who is best at execution of the task. It also maps the projects to adaptation sectors
>What interesting is the design of the projects are development issue oriented rather than NAP sector oriented.  The action plan table maps these projects to the adaptation sectors in later stage

*Point*
- This is to help coastal provinces without the Provincial Climate Change Action Plan to be informed. However, the content is potentially repetitive of what the Provincial Climate Change Action Plan will cover. 
- Using other information source for hazard might add more fragmentation to the provincial analysis
