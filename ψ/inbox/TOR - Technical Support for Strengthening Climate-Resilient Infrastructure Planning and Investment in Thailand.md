# TERMS OF REFERENCE

Technical Support for Strengthening Climate-Resilient Infrastructure Planning and Investment in Thailand

### A. Introduction

Human wellbeing and economic prosperity rely heavily on resilient and sustainable infrastructure systems, including transport, energy, and industrial infrastructure. As countries across Asia accelerate efforts to achieve the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and implement their climate commitments under the Paris Agreement, there is a growing need to ensure that infrastructure investments are aligned with low-emission, climate-resilient, and sustainable development pathways.

Thailand has made important commitments toward climate action and sustainable development, including through its Nationally Determined Contribution (NDC), long-term low greenhouse gas emission development strategy, and national climate and infrastructure policies. At the same time, increasing climate-related risks, including flooding, extreme weather events, and rising temperatures, pose significant challenges to infrastructure systems and long-term economic resilience.

In this context, the Sustainable Infrastructure Programme in Asia (SIPA), a regional initiative led by the Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD) and implemented in partnership with UNDP and other international organizations, supports countries in strengthening sustainable infrastructure planning, investment, and policy frameworks.

In Thailand, SIPA supports the integration of climate resilience considerations into infrastructure planning and investment processes, particularly in the transport sector, through collaboration with key national partners including the National Economic and Social Development Council (NESDC) and the Office of Transport and Traffic Policy and Planning (OTP).

### B. Project Description

Under previous phases of SIPA in Thailand, a Climate Risk Assessment (CRA) and Cost-Benefit Analysis (CBA) guideline and analytical framework were developed to support decision-makers in identifying, assessing, and prioritizing climate-resilient infrastructure investments.

Building on these achievements, the final phase of SIPA in Thailand focuses on the operationalization, pilot application, institutional uptake, and dissemination of these approaches through pilot implementation, technical assistance, capacity building, and policy engagement activities.

UNDP therefore seeks to engage a qualified consulting firm or institution to support the implementation of these activities and strengthen the application of climate-resilient infrastructure investment approaches in Thailand. The selected firm/institution will be expected to support the operationalization, pilot application, capacity development, and institutional uptake of climate-resilient infrastructure investment approaches in Thailand, with a particular focus on the transport sector.

### C. Scope of Services, Expected Outputs and Target Completion

The Firm/institution will be selected for this assignment through a competitive process and will be expected to deliver on the following:

#### Task 1: Refinement and Operationalization of Climate-Resilient Infrastructure Tools

Review, refine, and operationalize the existing Climate Risk Assessment (CRA) and Cost-Benefit Analysis (CBA) guideline and analytical framework developed under SIPA to ensure practical applicability by national and subnational stakeholders. This task includes:

- Reviewing existing methodologies, tools, and analytical frameworks;
    
- Refining technical approaches based on stakeholder feedback and implementation needs;
    
- Aligning the guideline with Thailand's policy, planning, and infrastructure investment frameworks;
    
- Supporting the preparation of user-oriented technical materials and practical application approaches.
    

#### Task 2: Pilot Application and Case Study Development

Support the pilot application of the CRA/CBA framework in selected locations in Thailand to demonstrate practical implementation and generate lessons learned for future scaling-up. This task includes:

- Conducting pilot applications of the CRA/CBA methodology in selected areas;
    
- Undertaking climate risk assessments and cost-benefit analyses for selected infrastructure contexts;
    
- Assessing financial and implementation considerations relevant to future infrastructure investment;
    
- Developing case studies and technical summaries documenting findings, lessons learned, and recommendations.
    

#### Task 3: Stakeholder Engagement and Policy Dialogue

Facilitate stakeholder consultations and policy dialogue to strengthen institutional engagement and support the integration of climate resilience considerations into infrastructure planning and investment processes. This task includes:

- Organizing coordination meetings, technical consultations, and stakeholder engagement activities;
    
- Supporting the organization of a national policy dialogue/workshop;
    
- Providing technical presentations, facilitation support, and discussion summaries;
    
- Engaging relevant ministries, local authorities, and technical institutions throughout implementation.
    

#### Task 4: Capacity Development and Training

Strengthen the technical and institutional capacity of national and subnational stakeholders on the application of climate-resilient infrastructure planning tools. This task includes:

- Designing and delivering Training of Trainers (ToT) sessions and technical trainings;
    
- Developing training materials, manuals, and practical toolkits;
    
- Facilitating hands-on application of CRA/CBA methodologies;
    
- Supporting knowledge transfer and institutional learning among relevant stakeholders.
    

#### Task 5: Knowledge Products, Dissemination, and Sustainability Support

Develop knowledge products and support dissemination and sustainability efforts to promote institutional uptake and future scaling-up of climate-resilient infrastructure approaches in Thailand. This task includes:

- Preparing policy briefs, technical summaries, presentation materials, and communication products;
    
- Supporting dissemination and visibility activities;
    
- Providing recommendations for institutional integration and future application of CRA/CBA approaches;
    
- Supporting engagement with stakeholders to promote sustainability and potential follow-up initiatives.
