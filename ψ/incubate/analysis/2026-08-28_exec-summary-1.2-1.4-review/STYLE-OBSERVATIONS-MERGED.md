# Merged Style Observations — Executive Summary 1.2–1.4

## Corpus and method

This synthesis compares each committed agent draft with Boss's working-tree revision. It is a diagnosis only: the drafts and `%%...%%` annotations remain untouched. The three source observations are `obs-1.2.md`, `obs-1.3.md`, and `obs-1.4.md` in this directory.

All working-copy typing slips were quarantined. They are evidence of an in-progress human edit, never a preferred spelling or style rule.

## Candidate register results

Ten non-lexicon candidates were observed once each in the miss register:

1. Do not convert evidence into causation.
2. Do not overclaim from a pilot or predecessor.
3. Prefer a specific project referent over a generic support modifier.
4. Prefer `เชื่อมโยง` when linking risk to a measure.
5. Later-section naming continuity.
6. Name the governance artifact when it is meant.
7. Complete a navigation claim with its decision-use consequence.
8. Explain the data foundation before the presentation layer.
9. Use concise executive-summary subsection headings.
10. Describe navigation as a mechanism.

No candidate has two independent sightings. None is promoted into `STYLE_PACK_TH.md` as a ranked rule or into `LEXICON_TH.json`.

## Harness implications

The 11-dimension editorial receipt needs an explicit human-review question for causal validity: “Does the evidence establish the claimed mechanism, rather than merely coexist with the proposed implication?” It also needs a plan-boundary check so a contract cannot require reader-facing content that the approved chapter plan excludes.
