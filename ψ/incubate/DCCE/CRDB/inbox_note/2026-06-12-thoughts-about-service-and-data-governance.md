
# 1. Reflection on DCCE best role
As a policy maker on the climate change issue, DCCE should aim at creating an ecosystem of climate services in Thailand.

>[!question] Q1
>what are enabling factors of infrastructure that no one but the state actor would invest in?

At present, Director Toey focuses on how DCCE can provide climate intelligence to the users, maintain their data and info platform. In his view, DCCE is like another firm/agency that aims to increase the numbers of running projects, which mean more budget. 

In my opinion, DCCE should start investing in building ecosystem. 


# 1. Linking the value chain
There are actors in this ecosystem. DCCE must organize this community. Apply a community organizer mindset to the engagement strategy. basic questions are
- What is the goal of the community?
- What are the perks of agencies/organizations joining and contributing to the community

>[!note] Director Tory's reflection
>He said an idea about setting up sectoral committees to share data and stuff in  [[ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_note/2026-06-11-Meeting-with-Director-Toey-Reflection|2026-06-11-Meeting-with-Director-Toey-Reflection]]. To me, it sounds very bureaucratic, and that why it fails. Committee like this is additional workload in other stakeholder's views. Without legal mandates to join and contribute, no one as individuals would contribute. Since we do not have legal mandates and will not have, we need to use a bottom-up community building approach. It must become something that community members are willing to skip their work tojoin and contribute. Incentives need to be clear, avoid using hard power at all cost.

>[!question] Q2
>How do private companies build the community of client/customers? what are the principles and frameworks that DCCE could adopt?



# 2. Laying the foundation of data as public resources

For a data ecosystem, data is the primary and public resources to begin with. Without data, value adding activities and services wont be born. DCCE must sow the seeds of data generation and capture among the stakeholders first. Data is constantly generated, but is it captured? is it processed into usable and exchangeable resource? is it value-dense? Like oil deposits, if they are not accessible and economically-viable to produce, they won't become oil reserves and have no economic values.

So, we need to balance "policy maker" and "state actor" roles of DCCE with their mindset as an individual actor, incentivized by the budget amount.

# 2. Data governance

If DCCE were a small startup company, data governance will be the thing they need to consider after shipping their first MVP and ready to move to production and operation. However, for DCCE, their released information and products must avoid failing too hard and minimize compliance risk since its inception. Furthermore, they have settled on building a platform, even though I don't fully agree if an info platform is something highly urgent because it is just a website. Without clear business objectives of the website to serve the users and retain them, launching too early could backfire. Anyway, since they want to build a platform, I think establishing data ownership is of high importance to act as guardrail for bad or mis- information. 

That is why I proposed 3 things; DCCE must 1.  from now on, take full responsibility of requirement analysis for the products and services 2. Assign data owners  and 3. Set up minimal technical data governance artifacts. Data policy documents and processes can come later.
