## 0. Executive Summary
This Entity-Attribute-Relationship (EAR) Catalog serves as the logical backbone for the CRDB data system. Version 3.0 represents the final transition from a "Conceptual Blueprint" to an **"Institutional Operating System."** It incorporates four critical strategic anchors: (1) The **Hazard Hub** for forward/historical data parity, (2) The **Administrative Anchor** for village-level reporting alignment, (3) The **Closed-Loop MEL** for dynamic resilience tracking, and (4) **Platform Decoupling** to maintain a pure logical model.

## 2. Strategic Logical Anchors (v3.0 Decisions)

The model has been hardened to resolve institutional gaps identified during the research phase:

1.  **The "Hazard Paradox" Solution (Map as Central Commodity)**:
    `HAZARD_MAP` is the polymorphic hub. `RISK_ANALYSIS` consumes the map regardless of whether it was generated by a forward-looking `HAZARD_MODEL` or a historical `SATELLITE_OBSERVATION`. This solves the gap between science and current practice.
2.  **The "Administrative Anchor" (Institutional Dominance)**:
    In Thai operations, `DISASTER_RECORD` (DDPM reports) is the primary owner of the `SPATIAL_UNIT` (Village list). The `HAZARD_MAP` inherits its administrative extent from the disaster report, ensuring scientific data aligns with village-level official reporting.
3.  **The "Closed-Loop MEL" (Dynamic Accountability)**:
    Restored the link between `INTERVENTION_RESULT` and `COMPOSITE_INDEX`. Project successes physically move the needle on national resilience scores, providing the empirical proof needed for NAP reporting to the UNFCCC.
4.  **Platform vs. Core Model Decoupling**:
    System components (e.g., `CURATED_PAGE`, `COMMUNICATION_ASSET`, `EVIDENCE_PACKAGE`) have been removed from the CDM to maintain a "Pure Logic" layer. These are recognized as application-level features rather than core conceptual data entities.
