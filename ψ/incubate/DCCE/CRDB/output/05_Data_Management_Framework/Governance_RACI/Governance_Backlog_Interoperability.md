## 1. Context
These requirements were migrated from the Pillar 2 Service Framework to ensure the NCAIF remains strictly user-centric. These are **Institutional Mandates** that the DCCE must execute to enable the 7 National Services.

---

## 2. Interoperability Mandates (The GDX Interface)

### G7-M01: Article 15 Compliance (Digital Government Act)
*   **Requirement**: DCCE must establish an authoritative exchange entitlement on the **DGA GDX platform**.
*   **Stakeholder**: DGA (สพร.), DCCE IT.
*   **Function**: Automated machine-to-machine payload delivery for Tier 2 agencies.
*   **Legal Basis**: Digital Government Administration and Provision Act B.E. 2562.

### G7-M02: The Institutional Liability Shield
*   **Requirement**: Implementation of an automated "Domain Certification" workflow that attaches the **DCCE Director's Publishing Seal** to released data.
*   **Function**: Removes personal liability from operational staff for "uncertain" predictive data.
*   **Validation**: Must be integrated into the Publishing RACI (Pillar 7).

### G7-M03: Entitlement-Based Access Control
*   **Requirement**: Define a matrix of "Access Entitlements" based on agency mandates (e.g., "Bankers are entitled to Portfolio-scale Tensors; Planners are entitled to EA-level Shapefiles").
*   **Function**: Bypasses manual MOU negotiations for each data request.
