## 3. Adaptation Information by Cycle (The Scientific Backbone)
*Purpose: Evidence-based documentation of the full climate risk and adaptation chain.*

### 3.1 Climate Science & Hazards (Cycle 1)
*   **Content Brief**: The physical basis of climate change.
    *   **Observation Data**: Short/Medium-term weather (Met Dept), Satellite observations (Forest, Land Cover, Water, Reefs, Ecosystems).
    *   **Climate Phenomena**: Major drivers (ENSO, AMOC), Climatology of Thailand, and key climate variables.
    *   **Future Scenarios**: Explainer on Climate Scenarios, Downscaled climate data for Thailand, and examples of scenario application in strategy.
*   **Source**: Presentation Page 38.

### 3.2 Risks and Impacts (Cycle 2a)
*   **Content Brief**: Analysis of why we are at risk.
    *   **Vulnerability Framework**: Detailed explainers on Exposure, Sensitivity, and Adaptive Capacity.
    *   **Cascading Impacts**: Narrative on Chain Impacts and Impact Chain Analysis (Resilience frameworks).
    *   **Damage Functions**: Technical notes on calculating impacts (High-attention item).
*   **Source**: Presentation Page 39.

### 3.3 Loss and Damage (Cycle 2b)
*   **Content Brief**: Forensic tracking of realized impacts.
    *   **Framework**: UNFCCC Loss and Damage framework and national definitions.
    *   **Statistics**: Historical disaster overview and realized Loss/Damage dashboard.
    *   **Methodology**: Technical manuals for calculating L&D and impact assessments.
*   **Source**: Presentation Page 40.

### 3.4 Adaptation Planning & Implementation (Cycle 3)
*   **Content Brief**: Moving from analysis to action.
    *   **Planning Guidelines**: Sector-specific adaptation measures and financial analysis for projects.
    *   **Strategic Roadmap**: Thailand's National Adaptation Roadmap and implementation pathways.
    *   **Measures Library**: Database of adaptation measures with descriptions, sources, and case studies.
    *   **Current Action**: Status tracking of ongoing projects and organizational risk management plans.
*   **Source**: Presentation Pages 41 & 42.

### 3.5 Results and Monitoring (Cycle 4)
*   **Content Brief**: Measuring success and learning.
    *   **M&E Guidelines**: Theoretical frameworks for tracking adaptation projects and strategies.
    *   **GGA Indicators**: Global Goal on Adaptation metrics and national indicators.
    *   **Platform**: Introduction to the Thailand Adaptation M&E Platform.
    *   **Lessons Learned**: Repository of successful case studies and "lessons learned" summaries.
*   **Source**: Presentation Page 43.
