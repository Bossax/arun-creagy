# Executive Summary: Pivoting from a "Static Portal" to a "National Climate Asset"

**To**: Director Toey, DCCE
**From**: Project Team (Strategic Review)
**Subject**: Avoiding the "Portal Trap" and Securing the CRDB Investment

---

## 1. The Critical Risk: The "Portal Trap"
The current draft TOR for the 25M THB National Climate Adaptation Database (CRDB) is at risk of falling into the **"Portal Trap."** This occurs when a project focuses on building a static "info" website that relies on manual file uploads by busy staff. 
*   **The Result**: Within 6–12 months of contract end, the data becomes stale, the links break, and the 25M THB investment loses its utility for national reporting and provincial planning.

## 2. The Solution: The "Guided Synthesis" Model
We recommend pivoting the platform's vision to a **"Guided Synthesis"** engine. This shift moves the Department from a "Librarian of Files" to a **"Broker of Knowledge."**

### Core Selling Points of the Pivot:
*   **Automated Productivity (The Synthesis Engine)**: Instead of just hosting datasets, the platform *automatically generates* 3–5 page **Provincial Briefing Packs**. This directly supports DCCE’s reporting mandates with zero additional manual labor.
*   **Built-in Trusted Baselines (The Trust Engine)**: The platform enforces the use of "Official" datasets (vetted by TMD, ONEP, etc.), ensuring all government departments use consistent numbers for risk assessment.
*   **Infrastructure for the Future (The Data Lake Runway)**: By mandating a **Decoupled Architecture**, we ensure that this system isn't a "dead end." It establishes the backend foundation (the Data Lake) needed for future AI-driven analytics and long-term national security.

## 3. Strategic Recommendations for the TOR
To secure this vision, we have introduced three critical "Hardening" measures into the TOR:
1.  **Mandate "Climate Brain" Personnel**: We have redlined Section 11 to require the contractor to provide **Climate Science experts** and **Geospatial Architects**, ensuring the project isn't just handled by generic IT staff.
2.  **Mandate Automated Ingestion**: We have replaced "Manual Collection" with **"Automated Harvesting"** from external agencies, ensuring the system stays "alive" without staff intervention.
3.  **Mandate Vision Alignment**: A new **Architecture & Vision Alignment Workshop** milestone ensures the contractor cannot "black box" the design and must prove they are building the synthesis engine we require.

**Recommendation**: Approve the inclusion of these Strategic Redlines in the final TOR package to ensure the CRDB becomes a durable, automated national asset.
