# Critical Competency Audit: Ditto (Thailand) PCL

**Date**: 2026-05-18
**Context**: Scoping the technical vs. domain expertise of Ditto (Thailand) PCL for the National Climate Adaptation Database.

---

## 1. The Core Competency: "Engineering-First Integrator"
Following a critical re-evaluation of evidence, Ditto's competency is clearly identified as a **Technical System Integrator**. Their primary expertise lies in the **Delivery** of complex hardware/software systems, not in the **Scientific Discovery** of climate risks.

*   **Evidence of Technical Role**: They build the "Surveillance and Early Warning System" for the DMR (99M THB). Their role is to install geotechnical sensors, IoT monitors, and SCADA/telemetry systems. They build the "pipes" and the "dashboards."
*   **Evidence of Domain Gap**: In their flagship "Blue Carbon" projects, the **Domain Expertise** (Ecology, Carbon Accounting, Hydrology) is explicitly provided by their partner **TEAMG** (e.g., specialists like Wichuda Meeros and Chawalit Chantararat).

---

## 2. Differentiating "Carbon Credits" from "Climate Risk & Adaptation"
The user's correction is verified: **Carbon Credits (Mitigation)** and **Climate Risk & Adaptation** are distinct domains.

| Domain | Ditto's Role (via Siam TC) | Competency Type | Gap in "Climate Adaptation" |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Carbon Credits** | Mangrove planting, monitoring (T-VER/Verra), and Tokenization. | **Asset Management & BPO** | This is a "Binary Monitoring" problem (Is the tree there? How much carbon?). It does not require complex climate risk modeling. |
| **Early Warning** | Installing soil/rain sensors for the Department of Mineral Resources. | **Instrumentation & IoT** | Focuses on "Trigger Events" (Nowcasting). It does not require long-term climate scenario modeling or vulnerability analysis. |
| **Data Systems** | Government e-Filing and Digital Twins. | **IT Infrastructure** | Focuses on "Data Availability." It does not require domain-specific logic like the **Conceptual Data Model (CDM)** for climate. |

---

## 3. The "TEAMG Dependency" Rule
Ditto’s domain competency in climate is almost entirely **derived from their partnership with TEAMG**. 
*   **Ditto/Siam TC**: The "Hands" (Sensors, Software, Capital, Concessions).
*   **TEAMG**: The "Brain" (Engineering Design, Hydrology, Environmental Impact, Policy Compliance).

**Strategic Implication**: If Ditto bids on this project, they will likely rely on a partner like TEAMG or external academics for the **Climate Science** components.

---

## 4. Hardening the TOR (Section 11)
To prevent the "Carbon Credit Scam/Surface-Level" trap, the TOR must explicitly separate the **Systems Engineer** from the **Climate Domain Expert** and mandate specific expertise that Ditto lacks in-house:

| Role Needed | Ditto's Likely Profile | TOR Requirement Upgrade |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Climate Risk Modeler** | N/A (likely outsourced) | Must have published research in **CMIP6/Downscaling** or **Climate Economics**. |
| **Data Architect** | IT Generalist | Must be a **Geospatial Data Architect** with OGC/DCAT experience. |
| **Metadata Steward** | BPO/DMS Specialist | Must have experience in **Federated Data Governance** (not just document indexing). |
| **User Researcher** | UX Designer | Must have experience in **Climate Service Design** for Policy-Makers. |

---

## 5. Final Scoping Note
Ditto is an **Infrastructural Powerhouse** with a high capacity for system delivery and government coordination. However, they are **Domain-Light**. They understand the *mechanics* of data, but not the *semantics* of climate risk. 

**Review Directive**: Use the TOR review to mandate that the contractor **Must Utilize the CRDB CDM and NCAIF** as pre-defined domain logic, rather than letting the contractor "invent" their own (which would likely be a shallow DMS-style model).
