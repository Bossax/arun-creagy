EVIDENCE APPENDIX

What CRDB established

Product Owner mindset; CRDB as planning, requirements and design; data ownership and stewardship; standards, inventories and service roadmap.

What TOR70 must now do

Turn the foundation into technical design, governed data pipelines, web services, test evidence, operating procedures and a supportable handover.

Continuity

Do not reintroduce a disconnected methodology. Apply the established Product Owner logic to procurement.

Boundary

CRDB did not build the system. TOR70 retains the real implementation, integration, testing and deployment work.

Reference

Detailed evidence and citations remain in the SDLC storyline and CRDB reports, available when needed.
