## Part 1: Industry Best Practice (The Professional Standards)

### Slide 1: Roles in an Enterprise Data Ecosystem
*   **Key Message**: Success requires specialized experts performing distinct functions across the lifecycle.
*   **The Global Standard Roles**:
    *   **Product Owner**: Accountable for **Business Value**. Sets the vision and prioritizes features to meet policy goals.
    *   **Data Owner**: Accountable for **Accountability**. Final executive authority on domain policy, classification, and legal standing.
    *   **Data Architect**: Responsible for the **Logical Blueprint**. Defines the data structures, math rules, and standards *before* code is written.
    *   **System Integrator (SI)**: Responsible for the **Physical Build**. Executes the coding, server setup, and technical implementation.
    *   **Data Steward**: Responsible for **Content Integrity**. Daily guardians of data definitions, quality rules, and metadata accuracy.

### Slide 2: The Enterprise Data System Lifecycle (EDSDLC)
*   **Key Message**: Success is sequential. You cannot "Build" (Physical) what you haven't "Designed" (Logical).
*   **The 7 Sequential Stages**:
    1.  **Planning**: Aligning system capabilities with organizational policy goals.
    2.  **Analysis**: Formalizing the semantic "Knowledge Baseline" (Glossary/Gap Analysis).
    3.  **Design (Logical)**: Freezing the structures and rules independent of technology.
    4.  **Design (Physical)**: Translating the logic into database tables and ETL architectures.
    5.  **Implementation**: Coding the data pipelines, user interfaces, and dashboard features.
    6.  **Integration & Test**: Certifying the physical build complies with the logical logic and quality rules.
    7.  **Operations**: Sustaining trust through ongoing stewardship and infrastructure stability.

## Part 2: The Project Essence (The CRDB Blueprint)

### Slide 3: DCCE Sovereignty – The Non-Outsourceable "Strategic Core"
*   **Key Message**: DCCE must retain ownership of the system's logic and accountability to prevent vendor-led "Black Box" development.
*   **The Strategic Core (In-house Roles)**:
    *   **Product Owner & Data Owner**: Accountability for policy objectives, budget, and data sovereignty cannot be delegated to a contractor.
    *   **Data Steward**: DCCE staff must remain the final authority on data definitions and quality standards to ensure long-term trust.
*   **The Consultant's Role (Arun / Logical Architect)**:
    *   **Authorship**: Hardening the **Logical Baseline** (Stages 1-3) through the **8 Pillars**.
    *   **Audit Gate**: Acting as the technical proxy for DCCE to verify that the next contractor's build complies with the design.
*   **Current Phase Deep Dive (Stages 1-3)**:
    *   **Planning**: Aligning system capabilities with BTR and CRI v2 objectives.
    *   **Analysis**: Formalizing the **Business Glossary (Pillar 2)** to eliminate semantic confusion.
    *   **Logical Design**: Authoring the **EAR Catalog (Pillar 1)** and **Deterministic Logic (Pillar 3)** to freeze the requirements.
*   **Outcome**: DCCE owns the "Logic & Rules"; the upcoming contractor only provides the "Physical Build."

### Slide 4: The 8 Pillars – The "Mandatory Input" for the Build
*   **Key Message**: These 8 technical assets constitute the **Frozen Technical Baseline** for Project B.
*   **The Assets**:
    1.  **Pillar 1: CDM (EAR Catalog)**: The blueprint for the contractor's database tables.
    2.  **Pillar 2: Business Glossary**: The mandatory labeling standard for the system.
    3.  **Pillar 3: LDM Logic (MVD)**: The non-negotiable "Math" for the Loss & Damage module.
    4.  **Pillar 4: Interface Map**: Instructions on exactly where to pull data (ThaiWater/GISTDA).

    7.  **Pillar 7: Ref Data Matrix**: The standard codes (Provinces/Agencies) to be used.
    8.  **Pillar 8: Building Block Catalog**: The tiered list of features to be built.
