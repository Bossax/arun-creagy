# Revised Pillar 6 Execution Plan: Market-Driven Data Governance

## Strategic Objective
Abandon the traditional "bureaucratic committee" approach to data sharing. Instead, establish **Governance-as-a-Service**. DCCE will create an "Authoritative Seal" for climate data. By aligning this seal with SEC and Bank of Thailand (BoT) compliance requirements, DCCE uses market demand from the financial sector as the irresistible incentive for line agencies to voluntarily submit and standardize their data.

## Bounded Concrete Deliverables

Pillar 6 will focus entirely on the mechanics of certification and compliance alignment, producing three specific, bounded artifacts:

### Deliverable 1: The "DCCE Authoritative Seal" Certification Criteria
*   **What it is:** A strict, yes/no checklist evaluating data provenance, spatial resolution, temporal baseline, and methodology transparency.
*   **Why it's bounded:** It is an operational rubric, not a philosophical policy. If a dataset from TMD or GISTDA passes the checklist, it gets the seal; if not, it is marked as "Uncertified/Raw."
*   **Target Completion:** End of Week 1.

### Deliverable 2: Financial Compliance Mapping Table (SEC/BoT)
*   **What it is:** A cross-walk matrix mapping DCCE's certified climate variables directly to the reporting fields required by SEC Form 56-1 One Report-S (IFRS S2) and the BoT Taxonomy (Phase 1 & 2).
*   **Why it's bounded:** It provides the exact translation layer showing corporations *why* they must use DCCE data to avoid regulatory penalties, thereby creating the demand pull.
*   **Target Completion:** Week 2.

### Deliverable 3: Data Owner "Intake & Endorsement" Workflow
*   **What it is:** A standard operating procedure (SOP) with a defined flow chart detailing how an external agency (e.g., DDPM) submits a dataset, how DCCE evaluates it against the Certification Criteria, and how it is published to the registry.
*   **Why it's bounded:** It replaces vague "data sharing agreements" with a mechanical submission and review pipeline.
*   **Target Completion:** Week 3.

## Execution Steps
1.  **Draft the Checklist:** Develop the technical criteria for the "Authoritative Seal" (Deliverable 1), ensuring it catches issues like missing data (POM) and spatial mismatches.
2.  **Align with Regulators:** Cross-reference the criteria with the draft Climate Change Act and SEC/BoT reporting templates (Deliverable 2).
3.  **Define the Funnel:** Map out the exact step-by-step SOP for an agency to upload their data to get certified (Deliverable 3).
4.  **Package for Leadership:** Present this to Director Toey as a "Soft Power" strategy that leverages private sector demand to organize public sector data.
