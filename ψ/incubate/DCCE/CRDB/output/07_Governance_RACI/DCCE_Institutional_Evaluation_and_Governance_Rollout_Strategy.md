## 1. Institutional Diagnosis: The "Durable Asset" Crisis

The Department of Climate Change and Environment (DCCE) is transitioning from a "Knowledge Consumer" to a "National Information Provider." Our forensic audit identifies three structural risks that threaten the durability of the National Climate Change Adaptation Information Framework (NCAIF) platform.

### 1.1 The Divisional Alignment Gap
*   **The Disconnect**: The **Adaptation Division (2.2)** serves as the primary "Subject Matter Expert" for the NCAIF. However, the **Central Database Group (Section 7)**, responsible for technical infrastructure, is organized under the **Mitigation Division (2.3)**.
*   **The Impact**: This organizational distance creates an "Alignment Gap," where technical infrastructure may be developed without sufficient focus on the mission-critical content required by the Adaptation Division.
*   **The Risk**: The NCAIF could be treated as a secondary technical project rather than a primary institutional mandate.

### 1.2 The Risk of Static Information Decay
*   **Diagnosis**: Previous initiatives struggled because they focused on the website as a container rather than the lifecycle of the information. This led to "Static Information Decay," where documents and data became outdated due to a lack of assigned divisional stewardship.
*   **The Mandate**: The department must move from "Publishing a Website" to "Operating a Data Service."

### 1.3 The Synthesis & Domain Barrier
*   **Capacity & Knowledge Gap**: Staff face a domain expertise gap. Many were transferred from the **Department of Environmental Promotion (DEQP)**, where the focus was on awareness campaigns rather than technical climate adaptation science.
*   **The Result**: While raw data may exist, the capacity to interpret that data into "Policy Insights" is limited. Governance must prioritize **"Source Fidelity"** and **"Validation Rubrics"** so staff can verify content accurately without being climate adaptation specialists.

## 2. Strategic Pivot: Governance via Content Stewardship

To align with leadership priorities, data governance is framed as **"Website Content Ownership."** This ensures the visibility of the platform remains high while establishing the necessary underlying technical discipline.

### 2.1 Reframing Strategy
| Technical Term | Institutional Frame | Value Proposition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Data Governance** | **Website Stewardship Model** | Ensures the information platform remains current and reliable. |
| **Data Steward** | **Node Owner (Sitemap)** | Assigns responsibility for specific sections of the information framework. |
| **Data Quality Rules** | **Fidelity & Citation Standards** | Prevents misinformation by ensuring all content is linked to authoritative sources. |
| **Pillar 2 Use Cases** | **Core Service Definitions** | Directs the technical system to solve the department's actual operational needs. |

## 3. Immediate Roadmap: The "June Coordination" (Phase 1 Milestones)

Phase 1 focuses on the **3 Critical Milestones** identified in the DCCE Engagement Strategy to secure institutional durability.

### Milestone 1: DCCE as the Product Owner (Use Case-Led Design)
*   **Mandate**: DCCE must transition from a passive recipient to an active **Product Owner**. 
*   **Action**: Consolidate functional requirements for the 2027 development phase derived strictly from the **10 Canonical Use Cases (UC-01 to UC-10)**.
*   **Value**: Ensures the future system is designed around validated operational needs, providing a "Procurement Shield" against logic invention by future vendors.

### Milestone 2: Certified Data Ownership (Accountability)
*   **Mandate**: Officially certify **Group Directors** as "Data Owners" and operational staff as "Data Stewards."
*   **Action**: Finalize the **Website Content & Ownership Matrix**, mapping every node of the NCAIF Sitemap to a specific DCCE group.
*   **Value**: Ensures every information product has a designated individual responsible for its accuracy and maintenance.

### Milestone 3: Enforced Interoperability Standards (Fidelity)
*   **Mandate**: Enforce the **Business Glossary** and **Conceptual Data Model (CDM)** as mandatory standards.
*   **Action**: Link entities from the Conceptual Data Model to the corresponding Sitemap nodes.
*   **Value**: Minimizes manual data cleaning and ensures technical consistency across all published products.

---

## 4. Final Strategic Stance
The technical governance of the NCAIF is the foundational component of platform sustainability. By prioritizing **Use Case-Led Functional Specifications** and a **Stewardship Matrix**, the department ensures the platform remains durable, relevant, and authoritative.
