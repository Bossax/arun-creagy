## 3. Thematic Evidence Synthesis
*   **The "Liability Shield" Requirement**: Across NXPO, TBA, and BMA, there is a recurring demand for an automated, DCCE-locked system to remove personal liability for publishing "uncertain" predictive data.
*   **The "Budget Unblocker" Role**: DLA, OTP, and UDDC require DCCE to provide the "Technical Proof of Risk" to overcome the Bureau of the Budget's rejection of adaptation costs.
*   **The "Scale" Mismatch**: NSO, MSDHS, and BMA emphasize that national maps are useless for local action; the service must deliver **Neighborhood/Sub-district (EA-level)** granularity.
*   **Reframing "Reporting"**: M&E is not an administrative task but a **Compliance & Tracking Service** that secures Thailand's standing in international negotiations (NXPO/DCCE).
