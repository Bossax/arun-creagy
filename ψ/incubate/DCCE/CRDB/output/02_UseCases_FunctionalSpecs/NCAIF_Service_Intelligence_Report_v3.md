## 1. Executive Summary: The Intelligence Value Chain
The National Climate Change Adaptation Information Framework (NCAIF) is structured as an **Institutional Intelligence Portfolio**. Unlike traditional "Data Portals," the NCAIF is designed to solve the specific **Operational, Budgetary, and Regulatory bottlenecks** identified by 17+ Thai line agencies. This framework ensures that the DCCE delivers "Transactions of Trust"—authoritative information products that provide legal and technical cover for national decision-making.

## 2. The 7 National Climate Services

### Service 01: Authoritative Discovery & Institutional Certification Service
*   **Primary Agencies**: TBA, NXPO, NSO, ONEP.
*   **Service Definition**: Provision of technically verified and legally recognized metadata and risk baselines, acting as the "Official Seal" of quality and standard compliance.
*   **Tangible Agency Use Cases**:
    1.  **TBA (Banking)**: Providing standardized **Sectoral Damage Functions** to ensure consistency in ICAAP Stress Testing and BoT compliance.
    2.  **ONEP (Environment)**: Issuing **Certified Biodiversity Risk Baselines** to support protected area management and national conservation strategy.
    3.  **NXPO (Policy)**: Establishing the **National SSOT Clearinghouse** to verify climate technology investments.
    4.  **DCCE (Comms)**: Issuing **Official Event Attribution Advisories** to protect staff from liability when communicating climate extremes.

### Service 02: High-Resolution Vulnerability & Social Analytics
*   **Primary Agencies**: MSDHS, NSO, UDDC, BMA.
*   **Service Definition**: Integration of high-resolution hazard projections with socio-economic micro-data to identify sub-district (EA-level) vulnerability.
*   **Tangible Agency Use Cases**:
    1.  **MSDHS (Social)**: Generating **Human Settlement Habitability Projections** to plan 5-year community relocation programs.
    2.  **NSO (Stats)**: Calculating **Disaggregated Population Exposure** (EA-level) for official state compensation claims.
    3.  **UDDC (Architecture)**: Mapping **Neighborhood-Scale "Double Vulnerability"** (Heat + Poverty) to target urban interventions.
    4.  **MSDHS (Health)**: Creating a **Priority Evacuation Registry** that overlays bedridden patient locations with flash-flood hazard zones.

### Service 03: Adaptation ROI & Budgetary Justification Service
*   **Primary Agencies**: DLA, OTP, NESDC, UDDC.
*   **Service Definition**: Provision of techno-economic evidence to justify proactive capital expenditure for climate-resilient infrastructure to the Bureau of the Budget (BoB) and State Audit Office (OAG).
*   **Tangible Agency Use Cases**:
    1.  **DLA (Local Gov)**: Generating **Accumulated Funds (เงินสะสม) Justification Reports** to allow LAOs to spend local budgets on proactive mitigation.
    2.  **OTP (Transport)**: Providing **Climate-Resilience Infrastructure Audits** to justify the 50x cost increase for 100-year flood-proof bridges.
    3.  **NESDC (Economic)**: Issuing **National Infrastructure Loan ROI Certificates** to verify climate-risk assumptions in massive state investments.
    4.  **UDDC (Local)**: Producing **NbS ROI Justification Analytics** to prove the cost-effectiveness of Nature-based Solutions compared to grey infrastructure.

### Service 04: National Loss & Damage Accounting Service
*   **Primary Agencies**: NESDC, DDPM, NSO, Ag-Sector.
*   **Service Definition**: Consolidation of fragmented sectoral loss data into a unified, Sendai-aligned economic valuation of "True Loss" vs. "Relief Payouts."
*   **Tangible Agency Use Cases**:
    1.  **NESDC (Economic)**: Producing **Macro-Economic True Loss Reports** (Direct + Indirect loss) to adjust quarterly national GDP forecasts.
    2.  **DDPM (Disaster)**: Issuing **Disaster Declaration Justification Certificates** to trigger Ministry of Finance relief fund disbursement.
    3.  **NSO (International)**: Consolidating **Official Loss & Damage Registries** for national reporting to the UNFCCC and UNDRR.
    4.  **Agriculture (LDD)**: Generating **Plot-Level Damage Analytics** to support agricultural recovery and carbon credit verification.

### Service 05: Engineering Design Parameters & Technical Advisories
*   **Primary Agencies**: DPT, OTP, MoT, BMA.
*   **Service Definition**: Integration of future-looking climate variables into technical building codes, infrastructure design, and land-use zoning.
*   **Tangible Agency Use Cases**:
    1.  **DPT (Urban)**: Issuing **Climate-Adjusted Rainfall Intensity Tables** for the redesign of urban drainage systems.
    2.  **OTP (Transport)**: Providing **Highway KM-Marker Hazard Projections** to inform the maintenance cycle of critical national routes.
    3.  **DPT (Zoning)**: Generating **Resilient Urban Planning Guidance** to enforce land-use restrictions in high-risk coastal zones.
    4.  **BMA (Engineering)**: Producing **Drainage Tunnel Performance Audits** using climate-adjusted runoff capacity models.

### Service 06: Sectoral Risk Monitoring & Early Warning Advisories
*   **Primary Agencies**: BMA, DOH, FTI, Tourism.
*   **Service Definition**: Real-time monitoring of habitability thresholds and productivity risks related to climate extremes (Heat, Salinity, Scarcity).
*   **Tangible Agency Use Cases**:
    1.  **BMA (Health)**: Activating the **Neighborhood Heat-Risk Intensity Index** to trigger Governor-level health warnings and cooling centers.
    2.  **FTI (Industry)**: Managing the **Industrial Water Security War Room** for the EEC using supply-demand forecasts.
    3.  **DOH (Health)**: Issuing **Heat-Stress Mortality Projections** for healthcare resource allocation.
    4.  **FTI (Business)**: Providing **Business Interruption Cost Guidelines** to help SMEs quantify the financial impact of climate disruptions.

### Service 07: National Adaptation Compliance & Progress Tracking
*   **Primary Agencies**: DCCE, NXPO, NSO, ONEP.
*   **Service Definition**: Automated tracking of national and international adaptation progress (NAP, SDG, GGA) to ensure institutional accountability.
*   **Tangible Agency Use Cases**:
    1.  **DCCE (Internal)**: Automating the **National NAP Progress Dashboard** for annual reporting to the Cabinet.
    2.  **NXPO (Policy)**: Tracking **Climate Innovation Alignment** with the Global Goal on Adaptation (GGA).
    3.  **NSO (International)**: Fulfilling **Consolidated SDG Progress Requests** for UN Voluntary National Reviews (VNR).
    4.  **DLA (Local)**: Integrating **Local Performance Assessment (LPA) Indicators** with national climate targets.

## 3. Institutional Supporting Infrastructure & Governance
To ensure the integrity of these services, the following components are managed as **Governance Standards** (Pillar 7/8):
1.  **Fidelity Guardrail System**: Implements automated data quality labeling (Authoritative, Provisional).
2.  **Stewardship Ledger**: Ensures data provenance and ownership transparency.
3.  **Interoperability Framework (Backlog)**: Managed in collaboration with **DGA** to ensure **Article 15 compliance** and remove personal liability via **Entitlement-Based Access**.
