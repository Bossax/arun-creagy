## 1. Objective
This memo details the methodology used to synthesize the 18+ raw decision moments identified in the `Pillar_02_v3_Intermediate_Extraction_Matrix.md` into the **7 Canonical National Climate Services**. This synthesis ensures the NCAIF is a value-driven service framework, strictly separating "User Services" from "Technical Governance."

---

## 2. Consolidation of National Services

### Service 01: Authoritative Discovery & Institutional Certification Service
*   **Logic**: Addresses the critical "Liability Gap" identified by all agencies. It merges the need for a "Single Source of Truth" (SSOT) with the legal requirement for a "Publishing Seal."
*   **Intelligence Product**: **Authoritative Risk Certificates** and **Validated Metadata Baselines**.

### Service 02: High-Resolution Vulnerability & Social Analytics
*   **Logic**: Responds to the "Scale Mismatch" (National vs. Local). It combines the requirements of NSO, MSDHS, and UDDC for sub-district (EA-level) resolution.
*   **Intelligence Product**: **Neighborhood-Scale Vulnerability Overlays**.

### Service 03: Adaptation ROI & Budgetary Justification Service
*   **Logic**: The primary "Budget Unblocker." It translates climate risk into the techno-economic language of the Budget Bureau and State Audit Office (OAG).
*   **Intelligence Product**: **Adaptation Investment Evidence Packs**.

### Service 04: National Loss & Damage Accounting Service
*   **Logic**: Solves the "Relief vs. True Loss" bottleneck. It consolidates fragmented damage data into a Sendai-aligned economic valuation.
*   **Intelligence Product**: **Consolidated National Loss & Damage Audit**.

### Service 05: Engineering Design Parameters & Technical Advisories
*   **Logic**: Maps the requirement for "Future-Proofed Standards" in the construction and transport sectors (DPT, OTP).
*   **Intelligence Product**: **Climate-Adjusted Technical Parameters**.

### Service 06: Sectoral Risk Monitoring & Early Warning Advisories
*   **Logic**: Groups the threshold-based needs of BMA, DOH, and FTI for real-time habitability and productivity risk tracking.
*   **Intelligence Product**: **Risk Intensity Advisories**.

### Service 07: National Adaptation Compliance & Progress Tracking
*   **Logic**: Reframes "Reporting Chores" into a high-value service. It automates the tracking of NAP, SDG, and GGA indicators.
*   **Intelligence Product**: **National Adaptation Progress Dashboard**.

---

## 3. Re-Classification of Governance & Infrastructure
Requirements previously classified as "Services" but identified as "Technical Pipes" or "Legal Frameworks" have been migrated to the **Governance Backlog (Pillar 7)** or **Technical Fidelity (Pillar 8)**:

1.  **Interoperability & G2G Exchange**: Migrated to **Pillar 7 (Governance)**. This recognizes that the DGA owns the "Transport Pipe" (GDX) while DCCE owns the "Payload Certification."
2.  **Liability Shielding (Article 15)**: Migrated to **Pillar 7 (RACI)**. It is an administrative safeguard, not an information service.
3.  **Data Intake & Quality Gates**: Classified as the **"Fidelity Guardrail System" (Pillar 8)**.

---

## 4. Synthesis Conclusion
The consolidation to 7 services hardens the NCAIF against "Contractor Logic" by ensuring every service is anchored in an agency decision rather than an IT requirement. Technical interoperability is preserved as a mandatory governance standard but is no longer "sold" as a user-facing service.
