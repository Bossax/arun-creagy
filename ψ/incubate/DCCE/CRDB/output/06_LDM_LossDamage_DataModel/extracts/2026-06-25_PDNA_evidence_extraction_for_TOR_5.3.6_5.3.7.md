# PDNA evidence extraction for CRDB loss-and-damage rewrite

## Scope

This note extracts only PDNA-related evidence from [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md) for later rewriting of [`5.3.6`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/CRDB%20-%20TOR.md:190) and [`5.3.7`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/CRDB%20-%20TOR.md:192). It does not rewrite prose and does not compare other standards.

## Core extracted claims

### 1) Full name of PDNA

- PDNA is named **Post-Disaster Needs Assessment** in English and appears in Thai as **การประเมินความต้องการหลังเกิดภัยพิบัติ**. The report places it at **Phase 3** in the opening triad and then describes the operational form as **ระยะที่ 3–4** for the agricultural case. Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:20), [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:94).

**Why this matters for [`5.3.6`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/CRDB%20-%20TOR.md:190) and [`5.3.7`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/CRDB%20-%20TOR.md:192):**
- The TOR rewrite should not describe PDNA as just a generic “loss and damage form.” The source frames it as a named post-disaster assessment method with explicit phase placement.
- The draft MVD should therefore preserve a phase-aware identity: rapid response reporting is not the same layer as PDNA recovery assessment.

### 2) Origin and institutional basis

- The report embeds PDNA inside a broader DDPM-led disaster assessment architecture spanning Phase 0 to Phase 4, where DDPM has a **significant role in Phases 1–2** and a **vital joint role with other agencies in Phases 3–4**. Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:19), [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:41).
- The report recommends a policy and system body called the **Damage and Needs Assessment Working Group (DaNA-WG)** and operational teams at national, provincial, and district/local levels. Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:35), [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:37).
- For PDNA specifically, the report says development of **Phase 3–4 PDNA forms** should be led by **responsible sector agencies**; for agriculture, it explicitly names the **Ministry of Agriculture and Cooperatives** as lead, with **DDPM in a joint role**, especially for human recovery needs. Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:30).
- The report also states that the Phase 1–2 DaNA process uses **MIRA** for rapid humanitarian assessment, which positions PDNA institutionally as the later, recovery-oriented layer rather than the initial emergency layer. Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:70).

**Why this matters for [`5.3.6`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/CRDB%20-%20TOR.md:190) and [`5.3.7`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/CRDB%20-%20TOR.md:192):**
- The rewrite should anchor the proposed MVD in a real institutional workflow, not a free-floating technical schema.
- It supports describing the Loss and Damage Reporting Form as cross-agency by design, with DDPM as a core actor but not the sole owner of sectoral recovery content.
- It also argues for separating rapid-response fields from recovery-planning fields in the test design for past events.

### 3) Objective

- PDNA is described as a **detailed assessment conducted after the emergency response has concluded** in order to **plan long-term recovery and reconstruction** under the **Build Back Better and Safer** principle. It assesses **damages, losses, and recovery needs**. Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:93).

**Why this matters for [`5.3.6`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/CRDB%20-%20TOR.md:190) and [`5.3.7`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/CRDB%20-%20TOR.md:192):**
- The MVD cannot stop at incident description or emergency needs. It must support a triad of **damage**, **loss**, and **recovery need**.
- Any draft form derived from PDNA should be suitable for recovery planning, not only for incident logging.
- Testing against past events should therefore check whether the dataset can reconstruct recovery needs, not merely whether an event occurred.

### 4) Assessment phases / workflow logic

- The report sets a staged workflow across disaster management:
  1. **Phase 0 Preparedness**: build baseline data and readiness data. Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:55).
  2. **Phase 1 Initial Disaster Assessment**: rapid situation reporting in the first 0–72 hours. Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:70), [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:71).
  3. **Phase 2 MIRA / CLA-supported rapid assessment**: more detailed multi-cluster assessment within 14 days. Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:70), [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:78).
  4. **Phases 3–4 PDNA / recovery strategy**: post-emergency assessment for damages, losses, verification, and recovery strategy. Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:21), [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:94).
- The agricultural PDNA form itself follows four steps:
  1. **Create pre-disaster baseline data**. Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:95).
  2. **Assess damages and losses**. Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:101).
  3. **Verify damage and loss data** through meetings with sector teams. Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:105).
  4. **Develop the recovery strategy** for prioritization, budgeting, and measures/programs. Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:106).

**Why this matters for [`5.3.6`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/CRDB%20-%20TOR.md:190) and [`5.3.7`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/CRDB%20-%20TOR.md:192):**
- The MVD should be structured as a workflow-ready model, not a flat list of fields.
- A credible draft form needs at least four logical blocks: **baseline**, **event impact**, **validation**, and **recovery requirement**.
- Testing with three past events should examine whether historical records can populate each block, especially the baseline-to-loss comparison and verification stages.

### 5) Types of data PDNA collects

- The report shows PDNA relies on both **pre-disaster baseline data** and **post-disaster impact/recovery data**. Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:95), [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:101).
- Baseline categories in the agriculture case include:
  - agricultural household structure, income, expenses, debt. Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:96)
  - production statistics by commodity and subsector. Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:97)
  - monthly ready-to-market volumes. Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:98)
  - agricultural water-source characteristics. Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:99)
  - agricultural inputs and asset values. Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:100)
- Impact categories include:
  - direct physical damage to crops, livestock, fisheries, inputs, and water sources. Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:102)
  - indirect economic loss measured by comparing pre- and post-disaster production quantities and prices. Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:103)
  - summary indicators that aggregate damages and losses and relate them to **GPP**, **GRP**, and **GDP**. Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:104)
- Process data also matters: meetings for verification and formulation of budget/program responses are integral parts of the assessment logic. Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:105), [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:106).

**Why this matters for [`5.3.6`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/CRDB%20-%20TOR.md:190) and [`5.3.7`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/CRDB%20-%20TOR.md:192):**
- The draft MVD should include data classes for **baseline**, **exposure/asset inventory**, **damage**, **loss**, **verification status**, and **recovery need/programming**.
- A test against past events should not be counted as successful if it captures only damaged area or death counts but cannot estimate foregone production value or recovery requirements.
- The source also supports keeping macro-linkage fields because the method explicitly rolls up impacts to provincial, regional, and national accounting frames.

## DDPM agricultural forms, data sheets, and field groups relevant to MVD design

### A. Form identity

- The report presents a draft agricultural PDNA form titled **Post Disaster Needs Assessment: PDNA (ภาคเกษตรกรรม)** / **แบบประเมินความต้องการหลังเกิดภัยพิบัติ ระยะที่ 3–4 (ภาคเกษตรกรรม)**. It covers **crops, livestock, and fisheries** as a case study. Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:94).

**Why this matters:**
- For [`5.3.6`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/CRDB%20-%20TOR.md:190), this is direct evidence that a DDPM-linked draft form already exists for one sector and can be mined for the MVD structure.
- For [`5.3.7`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/CRDB%20-%20TOR.md:192), agriculture is a viable first test domain because the source already operationalizes field logic there.

### B. Baseline data sheets / field groups

1. **Agricultural household baseline**
   - number of farmers and households
   - household size
   - agricultural landholdings
   - income from agriculture and non-agriculture
   - agricultural and non-agricultural expenses
   - debt
   - Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:96)

   **MVD implication:** Needed to connect event impacts to livelihood sensitivity and household recovery burden rather than only to physical asset damage.

2. **Agricultural production baseline**
   - cultivated area / harvested area
   - yield per rai
   - total yield
   - farm-gate / market prices
   - number of farmers
   - commodity groups explicitly named: rice, rubber, oil palm, durian, mangosteen, chicken, cattle, farmed shrimp, farmed fish
   - Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:97)

   **MVD implication:** These are core baseline denominators for converting hazard impact into production loss and monetary loss.

3. **Monthly production readiness-to-market sheet**
   - ready-to-market quantity by month for each agricultural type
   - Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:98)

   **MVD implication:** This is one of the strongest fields in the source because it enables timing-sensitive loss estimation; losses depend on when the event strikes relative to the production calendar.

4. **Agricultural water source sheet**
   - water-source type
   - capacity
   - benefited area
   - number of beneficiary households
   - managing authority
   - ownership/management distinction across private/public and government/local organizations
   - Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:99)

   **MVD implication:** Water infrastructure is both an agricultural input and a recoverable asset class, so the form should not reduce agriculture to crop area alone.

5. **Agricultural inputs and assets sheet**
   - number and estimated value of cultivated areas
   - livestock/fish farms
   - machinery
   - farm tools
   - seeds
   - fertilizers
   - pesticides
   - animal feed
   - veterinary medicine
   - Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:100)

   **MVD implication:** These fields support direct asset/input damage accounting and later recovery-cost estimation.

### C. Damage and loss sheets / field groups

6. **Damage sheet**
   - number and cost of crop damage
   - livestock deaths/missing
   - fisheries deaths/missing
   - agricultural inputs partially/totally damaged
   - agricultural water-source repair/replacement costs
   - Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:102)

   **MVD implication:** The form needs explicit severity/state fields such as partial vs total damage and missing vs dead stock, because these affect valuation and recovery pathways.

7. **Loss sheet**
   - pre-disaster production quantity
   - post-disaster production quantity
   - price comparison
   - estimated production value loss
   - inferred farmer income change
   - Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:103)

   **MVD implication:** This is the strongest evidence that “loss” in the DDPM/PDNA logic is not just counted damage; it is a comparative economic calculation.

8. **Summary / aggregation sheet**
   - total damages
   - total losses
   - ratios or shares against GPP, GRP, GDP
   - Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:104)

   **MVD implication:** The MVD should preserve aggregation-ready fields and geographic/economic reference keys so local records can roll upward.

### D. Validation and recovery-planning field groups

9. **Verification block**
   - meetings with sectoral assessment teams
   - discussion of results
   - identification of data problems
   - proposed fixes
   - Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:105)

   **MVD implication:** The draft form should include provenance/verification metadata, not only substantive loss fields.

10. **Recovery strategy block**
   - recovery and reconstruction needs
   - prioritization
   - budget allocation
   - policy measures and programs
   - Build Back Better and Safer orientation
   - Evidence: [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:106), [`Post Disaster Needs Assessment report by DDPM.md`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/Post%20Disaster%20Needs%20Assessment%20report%20by%20DDPM.md:93)

   **MVD implication:** A loss-and-damage reporting form aligned to PDNA should include a recovery-needs section or linked module; otherwise it stops short of the method’s stated purpose.

## Most defensible takeaways for the later rewrite

1. PDNA in the DDPM report is not a generic narrative concept; it is a **phase-specific assessment method** positioned after emergency response and oriented to **damage, loss, and recovery need**.
2. The agricultural PDNA case demonstrates that a viable MVD must contain **baseline denominators**, not only event impacts.
3. The report’s logic separates **damage** from **loss**: damage is direct physical harm/cost, while loss is estimated through **pre/post production and price comparison**.
4. The method also requires **verification** and **recovery-planning** blocks, which means a credible reporting form cannot be only an intake form.
5. The agricultural form already provides sector-specific field groups that can seed MVD design for [`5.3.6`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/CRDB%20-%20TOR.md:190), and those same groups define what should be stress-tested on at least three past events under [`5.3.7`](ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/inbox_source/CRDB%20-%20TOR.md:192).
