# Verified Facts: ART06_Species_Protected_Areas
- **Source**: `6-Vulnerability to climate change of species in protected areas in Thailand`
- **Date Verified**: 2026-06-25

## Verified Numerical Claims and Targets
- **Threatened Species Percentage Increase**: The overall percentage of modeled species assessed as threatened is projected to increase from 11% currently to 35% by 2070 under the best-case (RCP2.6) scenario, and to 54% by 2070 under the worst-case (RCP8.5) scenario.
- **Extinct Species Count under RCP8.5 (by 2070)**: 11 modeled species total (1 primate, 6 birds, 4 plants). Under RCP2.6, no complete extinctions are projected, but threat levels still rise significantly.
