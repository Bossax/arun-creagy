# Verified Facts: ART09_BMR_UrbanSprawl_Floods

## 1. The 6 Dynamic Urban Flood Indicators Mapping
The study categorizes flood risk into three core components, driven by 6 dynamic indicators:
- **Hazard Component:** 
  - UFHI1: Average monthly rainfall
  - UFHI2: Average monthly wet days
- **Exposure Component:** 
  - UFEI1: Vegetation cover (measured by NDVI)
- **Vulnerability Component:** 
  - *Sensitivity:* UFSI1 (Population density) and UFSI2 (Flood waste)
  - *Adaptive Capacity:* UFACI1 (Anti-flood infrastructure, measured by NDBI)

## 2. Detailed Three-Timescale Mitigation Strategies
- **10-Year Strategies (2024–2033):** Streamline drainage networks in high-risk districts like Chatuchak and Khlong Toei. Promote green infrastructure (rain gardens, bioswales, pervious surfaces) in city center districts. Implement early warning systems and community collaboration in densely populated areas.
- **20-Year Strategies (2024–2043):** Increase green spaces in outskirt districts to manage stormwater. Upgrade structural flood defenses in flood-prone districts. Use AI to forecast future floods. Incentivize flood-resistant construction materials.
- **30-Year Strategies (2024–2053):** Enhance water retention capacity specifically along the Chao Phraya River and its canal networks. Implement adaptive measures for residents and engage in international cooperation.

## 3. Implementation Challenges and Roadblocks
- **Operational Barriers:** Existing drainage systems are already burdened by insufficient capacity. Additionally, ineffective waste management (exacerbated by high population density) causes garbage to physically impede drainage networks.
- **Financial Barriers:** Sustained and dedicated financing methods are crucial. Without policy incentives, adopting flood-resistant construction techniques remains financially unviable for private property owners.
- **Policy/Institutional Barriers:** Execution requires strict inter-agency cooperation between municipal authorities and environmental agencies to balance urgent short-term drainage enhancements with long-term urban development.
