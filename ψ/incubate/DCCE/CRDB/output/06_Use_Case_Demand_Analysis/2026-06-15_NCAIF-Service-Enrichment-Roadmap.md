# NCAIF Service Enrichment & Strategic Roadmap

## 🎯 Strategic Objective
Transition the National Climate Change Adaptation Information Framework (NCAIF) from a collection of narrative descriptions into a suite of **"Decision-Ready" Institutional Services**. 

This roadmap maintains the **Human-Centric Blueprint (v6)** while enriching it with technical "atoms" (mathematical models, resolution standards, and compliance logic) extracted from global and local benchmarks. The goal is to empower DCCE as a **Product Owner and Data Custodian** that delivers defensible evidence to high-power government agencies.

---

## 🏗️ Part 1: The 8 Enriched Service Dossiers

### Service 1: Certified Climate Data Repository & Official Endorsement
**"The Single Source of Truth"**

*   **Urgent Use Case**: Line agencies (TMD, HII, GISTDA) and private firms cannot trust fragmented climate data for legal or financial reporting.
*   **Enriched Features**:
    *   **Architecture**: Adopt a **Decoupled "Data Broker" Model** (Copernicus CDS pattern). DCCE does not host all petabytes; it hosts the *Metadata and Certification API* that routes users to original source nodes.
    *   **Standards**: Use **STAC (SpatioTemporal Asset Catalog)** and ISO ontologies for metadata discovery.
*   **Measurable Outcome**: One "Official Data Badge" issued for a core hazard dataset (e.g., rainfall) that includes an endorsement of the source, update cycle, and usage conditions.
*   **Service Package**: Metadata catalog, data-quality scorecard, and a "Broker API" endpoint.
*   **Adoption Test**: Will the SEC or Bank of Thailand accept data from this repository as "Audit-Ready" for corporate disclosure?

### Service 2: High-Resolution Spatial Risk Analytics
**"Localizing the Crisis"**

*   **Urgent Use Case**: Provincial governors and local municipalities need to identify vulnerable households and critical infrastructure at a sub-provincial grain (Subdistrict/EA).
*   **Enriched Features**:
    *   **Resolution Standard**: Mandate a **90m resolution benchmark for flood** and 1km for other physical hazards (Global Industry Standard).
    *   **Social Overlay**: Integrate the **Thai People Map and Analytics Platform (TPMAP)** social deprivation layers to identify the intersection of hazard and poverty.
*   **Measurable Outcome**: A "Subdistrict Risk Profile" for one pilot province (e.g., Ayutthaya) that identifies specific enumeration areas (EA) at high risk of seasonal flooding.
*   **Service Package**: Geo-spatial risk layer, vulnerable population heatmap, and a local exposure dashboard.
*   **Adoption Test**: Can a local health officer use this map to pre-deploy resources to bedridden patients (TPMAP-linked) before a flood?

### Service 3: Finance & Budget Decision Support
**"The Economic Shield"**

*   **Urgent Use Case**: Agencies struggle to justify the higher costs of "climate-resilient" infrastructure to the Budget Bureau.
*   **Enriched Features**:
    *   **Mathematical Core**: Incorporate **Localized Empirical Damage Functions**.
    *   **Adaptation ROI**: Quantify the **Return on Adaptation (ROI)** for three specific measures: irrigation facilities, climate-adapted varieties, and planting time adjustments.
*   **Measurable Outcome**: A "Cost-Benefit Evidence Brief" for one infrastructure project (e.g., Nature-based drainage) that shows the **Avoided Losses** compared to grey infrastructure.
*   **Service Package**: Avoided-loss calculation template, CBA summary, and a methodology certification note.
*   **Adoption Test**: Does the Budget Bureau accept the "Avoided Loss" logic as a justification for a 15% project cost increase?

### Service 4: Historical Loss & Damage Assessment
**"Beyond Relief Spending"**

*   **Urgent Use Case**: NESDC needs to separate one-time relief payments from the long-term "True Economic Loss" for macroeconomic reporting.
*   **Enriched Features**:
    *   **Category Mapping**: Separate impacts into **Economic Loss** (OpEx/CapEx) and **Non-Economic Loss (NEL)** using Sendai-aligned categories.
    *   **Vulnerability Curves**: Apply the **Komolafe (2019)** logarithmic curves for structural damage ($D_f$) and the **Copernicus NHESS (2026)** rice crop formulas.
*   **Measurable Outcome**: A "Historical Impact Dossier" for a major event (e.g., the 2011 floods) that cross-walks DDPM damage records into currency-based economic impact categories.
*   **Service Package**: Loss-category crosswalk, empirical damage estimate, and uncertainty/limitation report.
*   **Adoption Test**: Does NESDC confirm this dossier reduces ambiguity in their macro-economic sectoral reporting?

### Service 5: Engineering Design Variables
**"Future-Proofing Infrastructure"**

*   **Urgent Use Case**: Engineers are using 30-year historical averages for Rainfall Intensity, which fail to capture future flood peaks.
*   **Enriched Features**:
    *   **Design Codes**: Convert CMIP6 projections into **Design Runoff** and **Peak Flow** coefficients.
    *   **Hazard specific**: Focus on localized **Intensity-Duration-Frequency (IDF) curves**.
*   **Measurable Outcome**: A "Design Code Update Brief" for one system (e.g., urban drainage in Bangkok) that provides the new recommended safety factors for 2050 scenarios.
*   **Service Package**: IDF update tables, peak-flow projections, and an engineering application guide.
*   **Adoption Test**: Will the Department of Highways adopt these "Future-Ready" safety factors in their next procurement round?

### Service 6: Multi-Hazard Impact-Based Early Warning
**"Acting on Impact, Not Just Weather"**

*   **Urgent Use Case**: Farmers and workers need to know the *impact* of heat/rain (e.g., "crop loss likely"), not just the temperature/mm.
*   **Enriched Features**:
    *   **Threshold Governance**: Set **Localized Flood Thresholds** based on soil absorption capacity per subdistrict.
    *   **Health Overlay**: Use localized correlations between flood depth and waterborne disease (e.g., Leptospirosis) to trigger health warnings.
*   **Measurable Outcome**: An "Impact-Based Alert" for one hazard (e.g., Extreme Heat) that provides a specific operational instruction for outdoor workers.
*   **Service Package**: Localized threshold maps, health-risk overlays, and an operational action protocol.
*   **Adoption Test**: Do local industrial estates trigger their "Business Interruption" protocols based on these impact-based alerts?

### Service 7: Adaptation Policy M&E
**"Tracking National Progress"**

*   **Urgent Use Case**: DCCE must report Thailand's adaptation progress to the Global Goal on Adaptation (GGA) with concrete indicators.
*   **Enriched Features**:
    *   **Compliance Bridge**: Align indicators with the **SEC (56-1)** timelines (SET50/Dec 2026) and the **Bank of Thailand Taxonomy (Phase 2)**.
    *   **Maturity Tracking**: Monitor **Technology Readiness Levels (TRL)** of local adaptation technologies.
*   **Measurable Outcome**: A "National Adaptation Progress Card" that aggregates provincial results into a unified reporting template for international disclosure.
*   **Service Package**: Indicator registry, institutional report templates, and a TRL tracker.
*   **Adoption Test**: Does the NCCPC (National Climate Change Policy Committee) use this scorecard to re-allocate the National Climate Change Fund?

### Service 8: Uncertainty Governance
**"The Institutional Shield"**

*   **Urgent Use Case**: Decision-makers are afraid to act on climate data because of "high uncertainty" and fear of audit failure.
*   **Enriched Features**:
    *   **Uncertainty Labels**: Implement a **"Suitability for Use" Labeling System** (IPCC/Copernicus-style) that matches data quality to administrative action.
    *   **Model logic**: Adopt the **FICE (Foundational Intelligence for Climate and Economics)** logic to "pre-test" assumptions under multiple scenarios.
*   **Measurable Outcome**: A "Decision Confidence Note" that accompanies every high-stakes climate service output, defining the "Confidence Level" of the methodology.
*   **Service Package**: Uncertainty guidance manual, confidence rating rubric, and an institutional shield protocol.
*   **Adoption Test**: Does an agency head feel comfortable signing off on a project budget because DCCE has "Governed" the uncertainty of the underlying data?

## 🧩 Part 3: Alpha Wedge Detail (Service 4: Historical Impact Dossier)

The Alpha Wedge serves as the "Bridge" between Phase 1 (Normalization of Line Agency Data) and Phase 2 (Analytical Enrichment). It transforms raw fiscal records into decision-ready economic evidence.

### 1. The Terminology Bridge
*   **Input (Raw Source)**: **Government Advance Payments** (เงินทดรองราชการ). 
    *   *Semantic*: Represents public fiscal outlays for emergency relief.
    *   *Status*: 2560–2567 Province-level series normalized to Silver.
*   **Output (Service Goal)**: **Estimated Economic Damage**.
    *   *Semantic*: Represents the total value of assets destroyed/impaired (OpEx + CapEx).
    *   *Requirement*: Essential for NESDC macroeconomic sectoral reporting.

### 2. Functional Requirements (The "Hard Atoms")
*   **Weighting Logic**: Adopt the **Pilot Methodology (2024)** weights:
    *   Deaths per 100k (22.5%)
    *   Affected per 100k (15.0%)
    *   Government Advance Payment per GPP (37.5%)
*   **Normalization Standard**: Use **Min-Max scaling** for indicator components, followed by a **Final Re-normalization** (0.0 to 1.0) of the aggregate score to ensure cross-provincial comparability.
*   **Spatial Granularity**: Initial pilot at **Province level** (using Gold DDPM tambon facts aggregated up), with a roadmap to **Tambon-level damage estimation** for Services 2 and 3.

### 3. Methodology Roadmap (Service 4 Pipeline)
1.  **Normalization**: Complete the terminology refactor from "Eco-Loss" to "Government Advance Payment" (DONE).
2.  **Harmonization**: Cross-walk Government Advance Payment categories (Flood, Drought, Wind) against sectoral GPP.
3.  **Estimation**: Apply localized **Damage-to-Relief Ratios** (derived from the Komolafe/Sendai benchmarks) to estimate the total "Alpha" economic wedge.
4.  **Validation**: Compare estimated totals against historical World Bank/NESDC flood damage reports (e.g., 2011 benchmarks).
