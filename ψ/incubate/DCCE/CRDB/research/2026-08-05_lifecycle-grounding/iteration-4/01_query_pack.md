## Notebook: Business requirement for SW development (`5133ef48-564c-40df-bdd1-142bb7e5bdf3`)

1. What are the standard components of a complete, well-formed business-requirements package for
   enterprise-data-platform software development specifically, as distinct from generic application
   software? For each component, what distinguishes a well-executed version from a weak one?
2. When a data-platform initiative is split across two separate engagements — a pre-build
   requirements/blueprint phase followed by a downstream development phase, delivered by a different
   team or vendor — how should the first engagement's output be organized so it is directly usable as
   the second engagement's input, without requiring the second team to rediscover requirements during
   the build?
3. What distinguishes a requirements package that is ready to support a fixed-price vendor build from
   one that is not? What specific gaps or ambiguities most commonly cause fixed-price data-platform
   contracts to fail or blow their budget?
4. At a high level, how do use-case prioritization, service/content design, and data-governance-
   framework sign-off typically fit into a business-requirements package for a data platform — as
   distinct deliverables, or components of one?
