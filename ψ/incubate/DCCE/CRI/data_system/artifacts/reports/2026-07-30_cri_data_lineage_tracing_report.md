## 3. Step-by-Step Backward Lineage Walkthroughs

### 3.1 Disaster Statistics Pipeline (`ddpm_yearly` & `ddpm_raw`)
1. **JSON Output Stage**: `tmp_stage1_export.py` exports tambon and province aggregate metrics (`deaths.json`, `injured.json`, `affected_people.json`, `affected_households.json`).
2. **Exporter Execution**: Lines 375 & 381 call `rebuild_yearly_ddpm_fact()` and `load_csv()` from `data/2_gold/ddpm/`. `rebuild_yearly_ddpm_fact()` dynamically combines `fact_ddpm_tambon_impact_climate_yearly_<hazard>_2560_2567.csv`.
3. **Gold Aggregation**: `script/ELT/build_gold_ddpm_tambon_impact_climate_2560_2567.py` and `build_gold_ddpm_tambon_impact_climate_by_hazard_2560_2567.py` filter climate hazard types, join 6-digit `subdistrict_code` to `dim_location_master.csv`, calculate YoY deltas, and compute national percentile ranks.
4. **Silver Consolidation**: `script/ELT/consolidate_ddpm_master_silver.py` ingests all yearly village disaster CSVs, applies mandatory `parse_clean_numeric()` comma sanitization on impact columns, normalizes 6-digit tambon codes, and outputs `data/1_silver/ddpm/master_village_disaster_stat_2557_2567.csv`.
5. **Raw Bronze Intake**:
   - Disaster statistics (2557–2567): 11 raw CSV files in `data/0_bronze/ddpm/` (e.g. `2567 - สถิติการเกิดสาธารณภัยรายหมู่บ้าน.csv`).
   - Wildfire statistics: `data/0_bronze/2026-07-16-cri-proj-data/Wildfire_hh_data.csv`.

### 3.2 Population & Household Pipeline (`pop` & `hh`)
1. **JSON Output Stage**: Used as denominators for per 100,000 population rates and in `apply_conversion_and_clean_keys` to convert affected households to estimated affected people.
2. **Exporter Execution**: Lines 383 & 384 load `silver_population_annual.csv` and `silver_household_annual.csv`.
3. **Silver Normalization**: `script/normalize_population_to_silver.py` validates headers, parses annual counts (2560–2567), standardizes DOPA 6-digit tambon and 2-digit province codes, and outputs Silver annual CSVs.
4. **Bronze Extraction**: `script/extract_population_tables.py` uses `openpyxl` to extract worksheets `pop67` and `pop60-67` from workbook `data/0_bronze/2026-06-12_cri_proj_data/CRI Data - Population.xlsx` into `population_extracts/*.raw.csv`.

### 3.3 Financial Relief Loss Pipeline (`loss`)
1. **JSON Output Stage**: Exports financial relief values (`loss_abs.json`) and economic loss percentages against GPP (`loss_per_gpp.json`).
2. **Exporter Execution**: Line 385 loads `silver_govt_adv_payment_annual_long.csv`.
3. **Silver Normalization**: `script/normalize_govt_adv_payment_to_silver.py` maps hazard labels to canonical hazard codes (`FLOOD`, `DROUGHT`, `WINDSTORM`), validates province codes against `province_code_lookup.csv`, and unpivots annual relief amounts into long form.
4. **Bronze Extraction**: `script/extract_govt_adv_payment_hazard_sheets.py` reads sheets `Eco loss อุทกภัย`, `Eco loss ภัยแล้ง`, and `Eco loss วาตภัย` from `data/0_bronze/2026-06-12_cri_proj_data/CRI Data - Government_Advanced_Payment.xlsx`.

### 3.4 Gross Provincial Product Pipeline (`gpp`)
1. **JSON Output Stage**: Computes province-level loss as % of GPP (`loss_per_gpp.json`).
2. **Exporter Execution**: Line 386 loads `silver_gpp_annual_long.csv`.
3. **Silver Normalization**: `script/normalize_gpp_to_silver.py` extracts current market price GPP, maps province names to DOPA codes, and produces `silver_gpp_annual_long.csv`.
4. **Bronze Extraction**: `script/extract_gpp_tables.py` parses sheets `GPP 67` and `GPP 60-67` from `data/0_bronze/2026-06-12_cri_proj_data/CRI Data - GPP.xlsx`.

### 3.5 Heatwave Impact Pipeline (`heat`)
1. **JSON Output Stage**: Exports heatwave mortality and injury metrics (`heat_deaths.json`, `heat_injured.json`).
2. **Exporter Execution**: Line 387 loads `silver_heatwave_impact_long.csv`.
3. **Silver Normalization**: `script/normalize_heatwave_to_silver.py` normalizes heatwave casualty figures by province and year.
4. **Bronze Extraction**: `script/extract_heatwave_table.py` parses sheet `Heatwave` from `data/0_bronze/2026-06-12_cri_proj_data/CRI Data - Heatwave.xlsx`.

### 3.6 Administrative & Spatial Lineage (`prov_code_lookup` & `location_spine`)
1. **JSON Output Stage**: Generates GeoJSON and TopoJSON spatial boundary files (`province_boundaries` and `tambon_boundaries`).
2. **Exporter Execution**: Lines 388, 402, 440, and 441 load `province_code_lookup.csv`, `dim_location_master.csv`, `province_boundaries_enriched.shp`, and `tambon_boundaries_enriched.shp`.
3. **Gold / Silver Processing**:
   - `script/ELT/etl_dopa_master.py` ingests DOPA CCAATT codes (`data/0_bronze/dopa/ccaatt.xlsx`) and village master lists (`code_village_dopa_2019.xls`) to produce the single source of truth `dim_location_master.csv`.
   - `script/ELT/prep_dopa_boundaries.py` joins `dim_location_master.csv` with DOPA shapefiles (`THA_Tambon.shp`, `THA_Province.shp`) and replaces Bangkok subdistricts with official BMA geometries (`ADMIN_BND.shp`).
   - `script/build_province_code_lookup.py` creates `province_code_lookup.csv` for crosswalking province names to 2-digit DOPA codes.
