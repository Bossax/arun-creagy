## 🔥 ISSUE #4 (NEW FEATURE): Heatwave Standalone Normalized Score (`heat_score`)

### Background & Agency Lineage
Heatwave casualties (`heat_deaths.json`, `heat_injured.json`) are officially owned and recorded by the **Department of Health (DOH / กรมอนามัย, Ministry of Public Health)** across 2561–2567.

Under **v4.3**, a dedicated **Heatwave Normalized Score (`heat_score`)** is calculated specifically for heatwave impact layers (combining heat deaths and heat injuries with equal 50%-50% weighting). The composite CRI Index formula remains unchanged.

### Functional Requirements

1. **REQ-4.1: Standalone Heatwave Scoring Formula**
   * Apply MinMax normalization ($0.0 \le s \le 1.0$) to heat deaths and heat injuries per province:
     $$s_{\text{heat\_deaths}} = \text{MinMax}(\text{heat\_deaths})$$
     $$s_{\text{heat\_injured}} = \text{MinMax}(\text{heat\_injured})$$
   * Compute standalone Heatwave Score with equal (50%-50%) weighting:
     $$\text{heat\_score} = 0.5 \times s_{\text{heat\_deaths}} + 0.5 \times s_{\text{heat\_injured}}$$

2. **REQ-4.2: Export `heat_score.json` Assets**
   * Export `heat_score.json` implementing the v4.3 unified schema:
     ```json
     {
       "metric_key": "heat_score",
       "metric_label": "Heatwave Impact Score",
       "period_key": "period_2561_2567",
       "unit_label": "Score [0-1]",
       "records": [
         {
           "province_code": "80",
           "province_name_th": "นครศรีธรรมราช",
           "raw_value": 0.4250,
           "normalized_score": 0.4250,
           "rank_desc": 5,
           "display_value": "0.4250",
           "value": 0.4250
         }
       ]
     }
     ```
   * Export under `build_exports/stage1/period_2561_2567/all/` and `build_exports/stage1/period_2567/all/`.
