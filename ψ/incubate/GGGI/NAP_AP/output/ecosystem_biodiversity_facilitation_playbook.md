## 🛠️ The 4-Step Facilitation Process

1. **Step 1: Set the Stance (The "Translation" Framing)**
   - Reframe high-level GGA outcome indicators into practical agency output actions. Explain that literal "resilience" databases are not required, but operational activities that contribute to them are.
2. **Step 2: The In-Session Translation Cheat Sheet**
   - Translate target rows (9(d)-1 to 9(d)-6) into specific questions about forest coverage, community forestry, coral bleaching, endangered species censuses (Thailand Red Data Book), and Soil/Water EbA actions.
3. **Step 3: Capture Metadata "Breadcrumbs"**
   - Probe for format (Shapefiles, CSV, PDF), frequency (annual, decadal), and specific division custodians.
4. **Step 4: Map Gaps & Proxies (Handling Friction)**
   - Reconcile PDPA/security blocks using access-permission flags and register satellite proxies from GISTDA where ground data is sparse.
