# GGGI NAP AP Evidence Registry

## Purpose

## Usage and maintenance

- Append new rows; do **not** delete or overwrite old entries.
- Use **`E-NAP-XXX`** for Evidence IDs.
- Keep paths absolute or root-relative to the workspace.
