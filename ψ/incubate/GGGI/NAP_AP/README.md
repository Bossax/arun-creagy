# Enhancing Thailand's Adaptation Action Plan 2025 - 2030

## Project Background

The Department of Climate Change and Environment (DCCE) in Thailand is working to roll out its Adaptation Action Plan (AAP) for 2025-2030 to create real results for climate change adaptation. To support this effort, the Global Green Growth Institute (GGGI) is hiring a consulting firm to help the DCCE update and improve the current plan.

## Overall Goal and Objectives

The main goal of this project is to enhance Thailand's Adaptation Action Plan so it aligns with the 59 indicators of the Global Goal on Adaptation (GGA) and to outline the resources needed to make the plan work.

The key objectives are to:

- Review the current AAP, looking closely at its goals, timeline, and budget information. %% This refers to NAP Action Plan authored by TDRI researcher . - Provide clear advice on how to use the updated plan to check progress and assign resources in the future.
    

## Project Structure and Scope of Work

The consultant will work closely with the DCCE and GGGI Thailand to complete five main activities:

- **Activity 1: Review and Analysis** Review Thailand's current National Adaptation Plan (NAP) and AAP to find gaps, especially looking at how clear the goals are and if the budget framework is complete.
    
- **Activity 2: Update Performance Targets** Check the current indicators to see if they are clear, easy to collect data for, and match the Global Goal on Adaptation. Suggest new ways to organize these targets for better reporting.
    
- **Activity 3: Improve Indicators** Suggest new indicators and create a clear table that links the goals to their baseline values, target numbers, data sources, and the agencies in charge.
    
- **Activity 4: Budget Estimation** Figure out an estimated budget for the plan's activities using available data. Group these costs into useful categories, like operational costs or capital investments, to help leaders make funding choices.
    
- **Activity 5: Stakeholder Consultation** Host an initial meeting to get feedback from relevant groups, hold at least one stakeholder consultation, and use that feedback to finalize the project.

## Team Requirements

To complete this project, the consulting team must meet specific qualifications:

- Hold an advanced degree (Master's or PhD) in a field like environmental science, climate policy, or economics.
    
- Have proven experience with climate change adaptation policy and a strong grasp of international frameworks like the Paris Agreement.
    
- Be familiar with Thailand's national climate plans.
    
- Have strong written communication skills in both Thai and English.
