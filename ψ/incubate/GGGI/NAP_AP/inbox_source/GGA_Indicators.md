# Thematic targets
## Target 9(a)-Water supply and sanitation
1. **Level of water stress**, which accounts for the intensity and/or frequency of relevant climate hazards and can include the outcomes of adaptation actions.
2. **Level of water use efficiency**, including measurements that reflect the outcomes of adaptation actions.
3. **Proportion of critical water and sanitation infrastructure systems that are resilient** to climate-related hazards across various warming scenarios, specific to different regions and contexts.
4. **Proportion of the total area of basins and the cryosphere** for which a climate adaptation plan has been developed and implemented based on different warming scenarios.
5. **Proportion of the population using safe and affordable potable water services** that are climate-resilient.
6. **Proportion of the population using sanitation services** that are safely managed and climate-resilient.
7. **Extent of measures taken to improve and extend water, sanitation, and hygiene services** specifically tailored to the needs of vulnerable groups and populations disproportionately affected by climate change.
8. **Proportion of bodies of water with good ambient water quality** suitable for drinking water supply.
9. **Number of people per 100,000 supported in planned relocation processes** in response to water-related hazards, ensuring that adaptation measures were taken to guarantee population safety.

## Target 9(b)-Food and agriculture
1. **Proportion of area under management** for food and agricultural production that utilizes practices and technologies relevant to climate change adaptation.
2. **Extent of implementation of institutional frameworks** for knowledge transfer, research and development, and extension services that support climate change adaptation in food and agriculture, relative to needs.
3. **Level of degraded areas** that are currently under management for food and agricultural production, including as an outcome of adaptation actions where applicable.
4. **Level of food and agricultural yield** in areas managed for food and agricultural production, including as an outcome of adaptation actions where applicable.
5. **Proportion of the population with equitable access** to adequate food and nutrition, including as an outcome of adaptation actions where applicable.

## Target 9(c)-Health impacts and health services
1. **Rate of mortality** associated with climate impacts compared to counterfactual rates, which can include the outcomes of adaptation actions or the coverage of early warning systems.
2. **Level of incidence of climate-sensitive infectious diseases**, including as an outcome of adaptation actions.
3. **Rate of morbidity** associated with climate impacts compared to counterfactual rates, including as an outcome of adaptation actions.
4. **Proportion of the population vulnerable to climate change** that has access to mental health and psychosocial support.
5. **Capacity of climate health services**, measured by the extent to which they remain at full capacity during and following climate-related events relative to their pre-event capacity.
6. **Percentage of health facilities that are resilient** to climate-related hazards under various warming scenarios (as appropriate for different regions and contexts).
7. **Coverage of essential health services** that are supported by adaptation measures to ensure they remain continuous during and following climate-related events.
8. **Proportion of health practitioners** who have received capacity-building support pertaining to climate change adaptation and health.

## Target 9(d) - Ecosystems and biodiversity
1. **Proportion of climate-resilient ecosystems** that continue to provide services to the populations that depend on them.
2. **Proportion of ecosystem areas** where adaptation actions have been implemented to enhance their resilience and the services they provide.
3. **Level of resilience of ecosystems**, which can include the outcomes of adaptation actions where applicable.
4. **Level of threat status of ecosystems**, including as an outcome of adaptation actions.
5. **Level of threat status of species**, including as an outcome of adaptation actions.
6. **Level of adaptive capacity, resilience, and vulnerability** to climate impacts that result from implementing ecosystem-based adaptation and nature-based solutions, as reported by Parties.

## Target 9(e) infrastructure and human settlements  
1. **Proportion of settlement upgrading programmes** implemented that incorporate climate change adaptation measures and maintain sustained engagement at the local level.
2. **Proportion of infrastructure and human settlements** that are vulnerable to climate-related hazards and other extreme events that have been relocated to a safer location.

Additionally, the sources note that to capture contextual information, the indicators for target 9(e) can be disaggregated by the **type of infrastructure** and **type of settlement**.


## Target 9(f)  poverty eradication and livelihoods
1. **Level of the population living in poverty**, which can include the outcomes of adaptation actions where applicable.
2. **Proportion of the population in climate-vulnerable areas** that has access to social protection services.
3. **Level of social protection systems** that incorporate climate risk management aspects and are capable of responding to the impacts of climate change.

# Dimensional targets
## Target 10(a)-Impact, vulnerability, and risk assessment

1. **Level of establishment of multi-hazard early warning systems**.
2. **Level of conduct of assessments of climate hazards, climate change impaป_cts, and exposure to risks and vulnerabilities** based on different global warming scenarios, as appropriate for regions and contexts.
3. **Level of establishment of multi-hazard monitoring and impact-based forecasting systems**, which includes monitoring stations.
4. **Number of people per 100,000 that are covered by early warning information** distributed through local governments or national dissemination mechanisms.
5. **Percentage of the population exposed to or at risk from climate-related disasters that are protected** through pre-emptive evacuation measures following an early warning.
6. **Level of establishment of climate information services** for risk reduction and systematic observation to support improved climate-related data, information, and services.
7. **Extent of usage of climate risk information and comprehensive risk assessment** (based on different global warming scenarios) to inform the formulation of national adaptation plans, policy instruments, and planning processes or strategies.

## Target 10(b) - planning
1. **Status of having national adaptation plans**, policy instruments, and planning processes and/or strategies in place.
2. **Status of having gender-responsive adaptation plans**, policy instruments, and planning processes and/or strategies in place.
3. **Existence of national adaptation plans**, policy instruments, planning processes, and strategies that have been informed by traditional knowledge, knowledge of Indigenous Peoples, and local knowledge systems.


## Target 10(c)-implementation

1. **Extent of implementation of national adaptation plans, policies, and strategies** relative to their planned implementation.
2. **Number of deaths and missing persons associated with climate-related hazards**, per 100,000 people, including as an outcome of adaptation actions where applicable.
3. **Net savings as a percentage of gross domestic product** resulting from avoided losses, including as an outcome of adaptation actions where applicable.
4. **Amount of finance for climate adaptation** reported in accordance with the relevant chapters of decision 18/CMA.1. This includes the amount of international public finance for climate adaptation provided by developed countries and received by developing countries to implement their adaptation plans, policy instruments, and strategies.
5. **Technology development and transfer for climate adaptation** reported in line with the relevant chapters of decision 18/CMA.1. This includes technology development and transfer support provided by developed countries, as well as the support needed and received by developing countries for implementing their adaptation plans and strategies.
6. **Capacity-building for climate adaptation** reported in accordance with the relevant chapters of decision 18/CMA.1. This includes capacity-building support provided by developed countries, alongside what is needed and received by developing countries for implementing their adaptation plans, policy instruments, and strategies.

## Target 10(d)-monitoring, evaluation and learning
1. **Extent of design of a system** for monitoring, evaluation, and learning for national adaptation efforts relative to needs.
2. **Level of operationalization of a system** for monitoring, evaluation, and learning for national adaptation efforts.
3. **Level of periodic publication** of monitoring, evaluation, and learning findings regarding the implementation of national adaptation efforts.
4. **Level of integration** of monitoring, evaluation, and learning system findings into national adaptation efforts.
5. **Level of institutional capacity** to fully operate systems for monitoring, evaluation, and learning for national adaptation efforts.
