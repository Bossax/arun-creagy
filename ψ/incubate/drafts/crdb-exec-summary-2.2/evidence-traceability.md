## Editorial cautions

- Section 2.2 explains the evidence-to-service logic; Section 2.3 reports the eight services and their distinct statuses.
- The national reporting pipeline is an enabling use case associated with Adaptation Monitoring & Evaluation, not a ninth service.
- Do not elevate Global Goal on Adaptation reporting into a broad UNFCCC claim without a source that states that relationship.
