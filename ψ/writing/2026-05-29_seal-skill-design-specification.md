## 1. Mission: The Sovereignty of Causality
The `/seal` skill bridges the session gap by forensically reconstructing the **Audit-to-Asset Chain** (Evidence -> Trigger -> Change -> Asset). It ensures that every technical artifact is logically anchored to the research intent recorded in previous sessions, preserving the project's structural integrity across time.

---

## 2. The Canonical Architecture
The skill mandates four canonical ledgers in the project's incubation root (`ψ/incubate/<PROJECT>/`):

1.  **`Evidence-Registry.md`**: Logs source notes, audits, and external benchmarks.
2.  **`Trigger-Log.md`**: Logs gaps or friction points identified in evidence.
3.  **`Change-Log.md`**: Logs strategic decisions and design commitments.
4.  **`Deliverable-Map.md`**: Logs hardened assets and their \"Sealed\" status.

---

## 3. The Audit Engine: Memory & Forensic Archaeology
The skill reconstructs the timeline using a dual-layer approach:

*   **Primary (Memory Archaeology)**: Crawls `ψ/` for recent retrospectives and handoffs. It extracts intent from the `AI Diary`, `Lessons Learned`, and `Next Steps` sections.
*   **Secondary (Active Forensic Dig)**: If prose-based memory is insufficient, the skill pivots to the **Trace Skill (`arra_trace`)** to perform a deep dig of the filesystem, git history, and technical ancestry.

---

## 4. The `/seal` Workflow

### Phase 1: Discovery & Triage
- Identifies the project slug and the target asset.
- Performs \"Memory Archaeology\" to find the Evidence and Trigger in past text.
- Reconciles narrative intent with `git log --stat` reality.

### Phase 2: The Traceability Verdict (Proposal)
Presents the reconstructed chain for approval:
- **E (Evidence)**: The grounding proof.
- **T (Trigger)**: The reason for change.
- **CH (Change)**: The design pivot.
- **D (Asset)**: The final deliverable.

**Options**: Seal, Manual Correction, or **Trace Escalation** (triggering a deep forensic dig).

### Phase 2.5: Trace Escalation (The Forensic Dig)
Triggered when the proposal misses the \"essence.\" The auditor runs `arra_trace` on the asset to discover technical ancestors (files, commits, history) and re-proposes the chain based on this discovery.

### Phase 3: Sealing (The Hardening)
Upon \"Green Light\":
1.  **Sequential Write**: Updates the 4 ledgers in T-E-CH-D order.
2.  **Hardening Trace**: Runs `arra_trace` to log the sealing event.
3.  **Trace Linking**: Uses `arra_trace_link` to physically bond discovered ancestors to the Sealing Trace.
4.  **Finalization**: Creates an outbox shipment reflecting on the strategy.
