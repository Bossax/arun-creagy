## 5. Epistemic Demarcation & Legal Qualifications

To prevent over-generalization, all key claims are strictly bounded by their actual statutory scope versus consultant design recommendations:

### 1. Qualification of the "Disaster Relief Decree Requirement"
- **De Jure Statutory Scope**:  
  Under the **Ministry of Finance Regulation on Advance Payments for Disaster Relief B.E. 2562 (Clauses 18 and 21)**, the Provincial Advance Payment (*เงินทดรองราชการในอำนาจ ผวจ. 20 ล้านบาท*) and Ministry Advance Payments (*เงินทดรองราชการระดับกระทรวง*) strictly require the official administrative instrument: *"ประกาศเขตการให้ความช่วยเหลือผู้ประสบภัยพิบัติกรณีฉุกเฉิน"* issued by the Governor. Furthermore, **Cabinet Central Emergency Reserve (*งบกลาง*)** allocations disbursed through MOI/DDPM reference these official decrees to verify beneficiary lists.
- **Critical Legal Qualification (Not an Absolute Blanket)**:  
  This requirement does **NOT** mean that no public baht can ever be disbursed during a disaster without a provincial decree. Under the **Disaster Prevention and Mitigation Act B.E. 2550 (§21)** and the **Ministry of Interior Regulation on Expenses for Assisting People According to Duties of Local Administrative Organizations B.E. 2566**, autonomous Local Administrative Organizations (อปท. — เทศบาล/อบต.) have statutory authority to deploy their own local emergency contingency funds immediately for life-saving operations, evacuation, temporary shelters, and emergency food/water supplies before a provincial decree is signed.
- **Drafting Guidance for Samart**: State that the decree is the legal prerequisite for **state disaster advance payments under the MOF 2562 Regulation and Cabinet central allocations**, rather than asserting an unqualified blanket claim across all tiers of government.

### 2. Qualification of the "SAO Audit Liability" Claim
- **De Jure Statutory Scope**:  
  The State Audit Act B.E. 2561 and the Civil Liability for Wrongful Acts of Officials Act B.E. 2539 impose disciplinary and financial surcharge liability for unauthorized expenditures, gross negligence, or intentional violation of financial regulations. Documenting physical damage on an initial survey form does not constitute a criminal offense or tort per se.
- **De Facto Behavioral Reality**:  
  Local civil technicians operate under acute risk-aversion. When statutory welfare compensation is legally capped at 49,500 THB per house, documenting a market reconstruction appraisal of 2,000,000 THB creates two immediate institutional problems:
  1. Affected residents demand compensation based on the certified valuation;
  2. State Audit Office (สตง.) auditors scrutinize why official engineering personnel spent municipal resources certifying valuations that have no legal basis for reimbursement under MOF regulations, raising suspicions of irregular documentation.
- **Epistemic Classification**:  
  The **"Zero-Monetary-Valuation at Local Level"** is **NOT a statutory legal mandate**; it is a **normative system design rule proposed by the consultant** to eliminate this behavioral risk, protect local staff, and ensure methodological consistency by centralizing valuation.

### 3. Classification of Other Key Operational Elements
- **Zero-New-Survey Rule for Tier 1**: A **consultant operational design principle** (not a statute) ensuring CRDB can function immediately using existing administrative data without imposing new survey burdens on local staff.
- **72-Hour End-Date Imputation**: An **automated algorithmic heuristic proposed for CRDB** (not a statutory definition under the Disaster Act) to resolve orphan records when remote sensing detects no active floodwaters for 72 consecutive hours.
- **Population-Duration Allocation Formula ($M:N$)**: A **mathematical attribution model proposed by the consultant** to attribute lump-sum decree expenditures across multiple concurrent events, not an accounting standard currently mandated by CGD.
