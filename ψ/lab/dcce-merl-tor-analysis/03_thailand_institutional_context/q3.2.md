# Ditto's Dimension 4 design is a measurement scaffold, not an institutional design

## Question
Is Ditto's "Dimension 4: MEL" design in the 2025 M&E Platform an adequate, Thailand-specific MEL framework — and does DCCE's acceptance of it count as evidence that it is?

## Evidence
NotebookLM query against the DCCE-specific notebook (`ψ/inbox/notebooklm_runs/2026-08-21_1600.../q1_targets.json`). What Dimension 4 actually contains: a 0–5 weighted scoring formula, percent-of-target achievement bands, a composite index, a traffic-light dashboard, and one paragraph of philosophy (shift from "Accountability/Audit" M&E to "Learning-oriented MEL," organized around "are we becoming more resilient"). What it does **not** contain, checked directly: no RACI, no named institutional owner for any function, no convening mechanism, no routing logic for how a red score reaches the right agency, no legal or budget hook, nothing that encodes DCCE's actual coordination-only relationship with 18 independently-mandated agencies (see [[q3.1]], open). Boss's direct challenge in conversation: this makes the design "generic" — the same scoring logic could sit on any country's indicator set unmodified.

## Judgement
Closed, and it corrects an earlier, too-generous claim in this session's own prior writing (`ψ/writing/2026-08-22_MERL-concept-note-and-tor-direction.md`, since flagged for revision), which said DCCE "already has a MEL design, it just needs activating." That's true only for the measurement half. Ditto's acceptance by DCCE is a **procurement fact** (DCCE commissioned and signed off on it); it is not evidence of **institutional adequacy** — these are separate axes and were wrongly conflated. Activating Dimension 4 exactly as written would produce a working scoreboard nobody is institutionally obligated to act on. The institutional-arrangement layer this note finds missing is exactly what [[q1.2]]/[[q2.6]]'s distributed model and [[q2.7]]'s forcing-mechanism finding have to supply — new design work, not activation of something dormant. Feeds directly into [[q4.1]]'s TOR-framing question.
