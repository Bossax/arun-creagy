# Current operational status of the 2025 M&E platform

## Question
What is the actual, current operational status of the 2025 M&E platform and its indicator set?

## Evidence
Primary sources via NotebookLM (`ψ/writing/2026-08-21_MERL-TOR-prior-project-context.md`, `ψ/inbox/notebooklm_runs/2026-08-21_1539.../`): indicator count moved from ~150–160 (original Chulalongkorn study) to 233 (March 2026 Phase 2 report) to **137 by April 2026, declared closed for at least two years** — direct quote, Asst. Prof. Dr. Pawitr Chuenchoom: *"เราไม่มีโอกาสแก้ตัวชี้วัดแล้ว"* (we no longer have the opportunity to revise the indicators). Same source, on record: the platform is still "phase one," not yet API-connected (*"ระบบตรงนี้ยังไม่ได้เชื่อมโยง API ตอนนี้มันเป็นเฟสแรก"*), and data is described as scattered (*"การขาดแคลนข้อมูล... หรือมีมันก็กระจัดกระจายมาก"*). Most indicators remain monitoring-only in practice; the evaluation-grade layer is aspirational, revisited "in two years."

## Judgement
Closed. This is the ground truth against which [[q3.2]]'s design/reality gap is measured, and it directly explains why [[q1.4]]'s reframed site-visit objective (tracing where indicator data would actually originate locally) is a genuine open question rather than something answerable from Bangkok. Also directly informs [[q4.5]]'s recommendation to measure operationalization (targets entered, cycles completed, data-completeness rate) rather than audience awareness.
