# Does Thailand already have an extendable budget-tagging mechanism

## Question
Does Thailand already have a climate-related budget tagging or reporting mechanism (via the Bureau of the Budget, NESDC, or its BTR preparation process) that could plausibly be extended to adaptation, the way the Philippines extended Climate Change Expenditure Tagging (CCET) from mitigation to a general climate framework?

## Evidence
Not yet researched. [[q2.7]] establishes that budget-precondition tagging is one of the two forcing mechanisms that actually worked elsewhere (the other being statutory duty). This workspace already has a live UNDP-BTR thread (`ψ/incubate/UNDP/BTR/`) suggesting Thailand has active BTR-preparation infrastructure that may already tag climate spending for mitigation reporting.

## Judgement
**Open — needs Boss, or a follow-up research pass Boss explicitly approves** (per Boss's stated preference this session: simple search first, deep research only with approval). If Thailand already tags climate spending anywhere in its budget process — even mitigation-only — that is a faster, cheaper lever to extend than building a wholly new forcing mechanism from zero, and would materially change the "what's realistic" answer in [[q3.1]] and the TOR's ambition in [[q4.6]]/[[q4.8]].
