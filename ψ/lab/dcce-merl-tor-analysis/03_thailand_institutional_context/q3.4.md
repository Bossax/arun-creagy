# Does Thailand already have an extendable budget-tagging mechanism

## Question
Does Thailand already have a climate-related budget tagging or reporting mechanism (via the Bureau of the Budget, NESDC, or its BTR preparation process) that could plausibly be extended to adaptation, the way the Philippines extended Climate Change Expenditure Tagging (CCET) from mitigation to a general climate framework?

## Evidence
[[q2.7]] establishes that budget-precondition tagging is one of the two forcing mechanisms that actually worked elsewhere (the other being statutory duty). This workspace already has a live UNDP-BTR thread (`ψ/incubate/UNDP/BTR/`) suggesting Thailand has active BTR-preparation infrastructure that may already tag climate spending for mitigation reporting. Boss, per conversation: "Probably Budget Bureau. UNDP is working on climate and gender tagging." Boss later sharpened this: "they have tagging mechanisms but no climate change agenda included" — i.e. Thailand's PFM system already has functioning budget-tagging categories, but climate/adaptation is not currently one of them.

## Judgement
Closed. The mechanism itself exists — this is not a "build tagging infrastructure from zero" problem. What's missing is a climate/adaptation tag *category* within an already-functioning tagging system. That's a materially lighter lift than the original "probably Budget Bureau" framing suggested, and reframes [[q3.8]]/[[q4.9]]'s ask from "invent a mechanism" to "get climate adaptation added as a recognized budget-tag category." UNDP's climate + gender tagging work ([[q3.7]]) is a plausible template or entry point for that addition. Any TOR recommendation should name "add an adaptation tag category to the existing PFM tagging system" as the concrete ask, not a vaguer "extend budget tagging."
