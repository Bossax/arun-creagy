# Does a real convening authority already exist at DCCE

## Question
Does DCCE's org chart already have a convening authority (e.g. an "adaptation division head") with real institutional weight to resolve competing cross-divisional recommendations, as the distributed root-cause model in [[q2.6]] requires?

## Evidence
Boss's model names this role generically ("the head of, um, adaptation division") without confirming it exists with real authority in DCCE's actual structure. TOR §12 (`ψ/inbox/TOR-DCCE-MERL-2026.md`) names the responsible unit as "กลุ่มติดตามประเมินผลการปรับตัว" (Adaptation M&E Group) under "กองขับเคลื่อนการปรับตัวต่อการเปลี่ยนแปลงสภาพภูมิอากาศ" (Adaptation Driving Division) — but this is the M&E function's own home, not necessarily a convening authority with power over the three other adaptation-cycle functions.

## Judgement
**Open — needs Boss.** Without this, [[q2.6]]'s model (validated against Germany/UK/Philippines precedent as the right *shape*) has no institutional home to actually execute the "pool recommendations, resolve politically" step within DCCE. If no such role exists with real weight, creating or designating one becomes a prerequisite deliverable for the next TOR, not an assumption it can build on.
