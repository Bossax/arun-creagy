# DCCE's actual authority over the 18 line agencies

## Question
What authority does DCCE actually have over the 18 focal-point agencies across 6 sectors — coordination only, or some real lever (budget, legal, political)?

## Evidence
Boss's own framing in conversation: "the DCCE is just a coordinating organization" — for planning, DCCE can provide tools/data/guidelines that agencies "have to adopt... to apply to their routine"; for implementation, agencies "have to incorporate maybe principles" into their own projects; even risk/impact assessment "could be done entirely by [other] agencies who have specific datasets." No document has yet established what formal levers, if any, DCCE holds (MOUs, cabinet mandates, budget-review input, legal reporting requirements it can invoke).

## Judgement
Closed, per Boss: "mostly MOUs and momentum DCCE has built on over the years. No legal piggyback." Confirms the consequential fact this whole analysis was blocked on — DCCE holds no budget-review role and no legal reporting duty it can invoke; its authority over the 18 agencies is soft (accumulated MOUs and institutional standing), not hard. This means [[q2.7]]'s finding (compliance requires a budget or legal forcing mechanism, never persuasion alone) has no existing lever to build on at DCCE today — any compliance mechanism the next TOR proposes has to work through voluntary/soft channels or explicitly recommend building a new lever, not assume one exists. Directly unblocks [[q3.4]] and [[q4.7]].
