# DCCE's actual authority over the 18 line agencies

## Question
What authority does DCCE actually have over the 18 focal-point agencies across 6 sectors — coordination only, or some real lever (budget, legal, political)?

## Evidence
Boss's own framing in conversation: "the CCE is just a coordinating organization" — for planning, DCCE can provide tools/data/guidelines that agencies "have to adopt... to apply to their routine"; for implementation, agencies "have to incorporate maybe principles" into their own projects; even risk/impact assessment "could be done entirely by [other] agencies who have specific datasets." No document has yet established what formal levers, if any, DCCE holds (MOUs, cabinet mandates, budget-review input, legal reporting requirements it can invoke).

## Judgement
**Open — needs Boss.** This is the single most consequential unresolved fact in the whole analysis: [[q2.7]]'s finding (compliance requires a budget or legal forcing mechanism, never persuasion alone) is only actionable for DCCE if some lever, however partial, already exists to build on. Specifically need: (1) any existing MOU or cabinet-level mandate DCCE holds over the 18 agencies, (2) any role DCCE has in those agencies' budget review or approval process, (3) any legal reporting duty DCCE can currently invoke. Directly blocks [[q3.4]] and [[q4.7]].
