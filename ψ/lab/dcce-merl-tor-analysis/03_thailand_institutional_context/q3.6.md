# Current Ditto relationship status and technical-asset access

## Question
What is DCCE's current contractual relationship with Ditto (Thailand) PCL, and does DCCE hold the technical documentation/data access needed to hand Dimension 4 (see [[q3.2]]) to a new contractor for activation?

## Evidence
Not established in conversation or in any document reviewed this session. The Phase 2 Progress Report (dated March 2026) and the April 2026 launch-event transcript are the most recent primary sources touching the platform; neither confirms whether Ditto's contract has since closed, is under maintenance, or is expected to continue in parallel with a new MERL contractor.

## Judgement
**Open — needs Boss.** This determines whether "activate Dimension 4" ([[q4.1]]) is something a new MERL contractor can do independently, or whether it requires DCCE to first secure continued Ditto involvement or a formal technical handover. A TOR that assumes activation is possible without checking this could be asking a bidder to reverse-engineer a system they have no contractual access to modify.
