# Current Ditto relationship status and technical-asset access

## Question
What is DCCE's current contractual relationship with Ditto (Thailand) PCL, and does DCCE hold the technical documentation/data access needed to hand Dimension 4 (see [[q3.2]]) to a new contractor for activation?

## Evidence
The Phase 2 Progress Report (dated March 2026) and the April 2026 launch-event transcript are the most recent primary sources touching the platform; neither confirmed whether Ditto's contract had since closed. Boss, per conversation: "the contract is closed. the platform was handed to DCCE."

## Judgement
Closed. Ditto's contract has ended and the M&E platform asset has already been formally handed to DCCE — meaning "activate Dimension 4" ([[q4.1]]) is something a new MERL contractor can plausibly do independently, provided DCCE actually holds the technical documentation and data access that should have come with the handover (not yet separately verified, but no longer blocked on Ditto's continued involvement). This removes one of the operationalization risks flagged in [[q3.2]]/[[q4.1]] — the next TOR can assume DCCE, not Ditto, is the counterparty for technical access.
