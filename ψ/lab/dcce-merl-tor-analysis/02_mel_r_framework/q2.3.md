# MEL vs MERL as the recognized standard

## Question
Is MEL or MERL the actual global/institutional standard for adaptation M&E?

## Evidence
NotebookLM query against the general adaptation-M&E-literature notebook (`ψ/inbox/notebooklm_runs/2026-08-21_1615.../q2_learning.json`, source `0357bd94`): COP28's UAE Framework for Global Climate Resilience, target 10(d), requires every Party to have "designed, established and operationalized a system for monitoring, evaluation and learning" by 2030 — three letters, MEL. A direct check of the term "MERL" against the same literature set came back clean: not used as a standard construct anywhere queried (`.../q3_research_component.json`). Simple-search follow-up this turn (`~/.pplx-cli/history/2026-08-22_03-05-22...md`) confirms and sharpens this: the field's actual formal elements are monitoring, evaluation, learning; where a four-letter form does appear, it is sometimes "MERL" with **R standing for Reporting**, not Research.

## Judgement
Closed. MEL is the real, treaty-referenced standard Thailand's obligations are measured against. "MERL" (with R = Research) is not a recognized construct in the literature checked — DCCE's own instinct to wonder what the fourth letter does was correct.
