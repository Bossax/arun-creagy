# What Learning means, distinct from watching data

## Question
Is Learning just "look at the data," or something structurally different?

## Evidence
Web search grounding (WebSearch, this turn's sources: IISD, NAP Global Network, WRI "Reshaping MEL for Locally Led Adaptation," learningforsustainability.net). Learning is a reflexive process that changes goals and instruments themselves, not just effort applied to them — "recalibration of goal/instrument configurations." Distinguished as single-loop (missed target → push harder, same strategy) vs. double-loop (missed target → target, strategy, or underlying belief about what drives resilience gets revisited) — a distinction traced to Argyris & Schön and echoed directly in Ditto's own Dimension 4 language (`ψ/inbox/notebooklm_runs/2026-08-21_1600.../q1_targets.json`, citation 8): shifting from "Accountability/Audit" M&E to "Learning-oriented MEL" organized around *are we becoming more resilient*, not how many projects finished. Boss's own execution model in [[q1.2]]/[[q2.6]] describes how this actually gets executed institutionally: a scheduled, structured review (what happened vs. expected, why, what changes) convened by people who can actually revise the plan, with the finding written back into the governing document.

## Judgement
Closed. A scoring dashboard, even fully operational, only ever delivers single-loop learning. Double-loop learning requires the convening + authority-to-revise mechanism described in [[q1.2]] — which is exactly what Ditto's Dimension 4 design never specified (see [[q3.2]]), even though its own rhetoric names the right goal.
