# Formal stages of a generic adaptation cycle

## Question
What are the formal stages of a generic climate adaptation cycle, and how do they relate structurally?

## Evidence
Synthesized from this session's own reasoning, developed jointly with Boss across several turns — not drawn from a single named framework document. The working model that emerged: (1) impact/vulnerability/risk assessment, (2) adaptation planning, (3) implementation, (4) monitoring & evaluation. Each stage feeds the next; M&E's outputs are meant to loop back into (1)–(3) as revised risk pictures, plans, and implementation adjustments.

## Judgement
Four stages, not three — M&E is a distinct fourth function, not folded into implementation. The load-bearing insight (see [[q1.2]]) is that M&E's job in this cycle is narrower than commonly assumed: it flags deviation between expected and actual results, but the burden of explaining *why* belongs to whichever of the other three stages has the domain standing to investigate. This four-stage structure is the frame the rest of this analysis's Part 1 (adaptation-cycle) notes build on.
