# Is this analysis for redirecting DCCE or guiding a bidder

## Question
Is this whole body of analysis meant to redirect DCCE's TOR text before it's issued, or to guide how a bidder should interpret and execute a TOR that's already fixed?

## Evidence
Not stated by Boss yet. The two audiences produce genuinely different deliverables: a redline memo addressed to DCCE (which can propose changing TOR clauses, budget, qualifications) vs. a bidder's execution strategy (which has to extract the most real value from an unchangeable text — e.g., how to interpret "meeting or dialogue forum" generously, how to use the already-funded forums for target-entry even if the TOR doesn't say to).
