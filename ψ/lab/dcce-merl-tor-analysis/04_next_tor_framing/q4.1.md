# Activation vs institutional design as the TOR's target

## Question
Should the next TOR be framed as pure "activation" of the existing platform, or does it need to also commission new institutional-arrangement design?

## Evidence
[[q3.2]] establishes that Ditto's Dimension 4 is a measurement scaffold with zero institutional-arrangement content — no RACI, no routing logic, no forcing mechanism. [[q1.2]]/[[q2.6]] establish what a working institutional arrangement actually requires (distributed root-cause ownership, a convening authority, a forcing mechanism per [[q2.7]]). None of that exists in Ditto's design or anywhere else checked. `ψ/writing/2026-08-22_MERL-concept-note-and-tor-direction.md` originally framed this as pure "activation" ("find out why Dimension 4 never turned on, and turn it on") — a framing this note revises.

## Judgement
Closed, revised. "Activation, not invention" was too generous. The accurate framing is two-part: activate the measurement layer that already exists (targets, scoring, dashboard), **and** design the institutional-arrangement layer that never existed — root-cause routing, convening authority, and at least the first step toward a forcing mechanism. The second half is new design work, not a switch to flip. Depends on [[q3.6]] (Ditto access) for feasibility and [[q4.4]] for what qualifications this work requires of a contractor.
