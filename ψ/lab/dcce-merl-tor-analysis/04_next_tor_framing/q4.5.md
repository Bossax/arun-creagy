# What the evaluation clause should actually measure

## Question
Should TOR §5.6 (evaluation) measure audience awareness, or system operationalization?

## Evidence
Current TOR §5.6 (`ψ/inbox/TOR-DCCE-MERL-2026.md`) asks for "% of target groups who used the knowledge" — an awareness proxy, not a system-function measure. [[q3.3]] establishes the actual operational facts worth tracking: how many of the 137 indicators have a real target entered, how many sectors completed one full monitor-evaluate-adjust cycle, what the data-completeness rate is. [[q2.5]] establishes that a stated basis for judgment (target or recorded assumption) is the real evaluative requirement — the current metric has neither.

## Judgement
Closed. Replace the awareness percentage with observable operational measures: indicators with targets entered, sectors completing a full cycle, data-completeness rate at contract close. These are the only measures that actually answer whether this project did what its own name claims.
