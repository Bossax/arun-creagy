# National-to-local sequencing: trigger and precondition

## Question
What should trigger DCCE extending MEL from national to local/area level, and is it premature for this TOR to address at all, given DCCE lacks even a working national-level Phase 3 cycle?

## Evidence
Boss's framing this session was explicit: national level first, local level only after. [[q1.4]] (site visit as area-based diagnostic, not a fix for the national reporting mechanism) and [[q3.5]] (no real convening authority yet exists within DCCE) both point the same direction — local-level MEL work depends on national-level plumbing existing first.

## Judgement
**Open — needs Boss's confirmation of the sequencing logic**, and specifically whether the next TOR should explicitly exclude local-level MEL construction (leaving only the area-diagnostic site-visit use already scoped in [[q1.4]]), or whether some minimal local-level hook still belongs in this TOR cycle.
