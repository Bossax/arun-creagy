# Consultant qualification requirements for the next TOR

## Question
What should §4 (consultant qualifications) require, given the domain-light-contractor risk already flagged once in DCCE's own procurement history?

## Evidence
`ψ/incubate/DCCE/CRDB/output/2026-05-18_TOR-Review/Contractor-Fit-Analysis-Ditto.md` — the CRDB TOR review already found Ditto to be an "Engineering-First Integrator," domain-light on climate adaptation science, dependent on partner TEAMG for domain logic, and recommended the TOR mandate specific expertise (a "Lead Climate Knowledge Broker" with published research background) that Ditto lacked in-house. Current MERL TOR §4 (`ψ/inbox/TOR-DCCE-MERL-2026.md`) is entirely procurement boilerplate — legal capacity, no bankruptcy, Ministry of Finance consultant registry — with no requirement for climate-adaptation M&E research experience.

## Judgement
Closed. Given [[q4.1]]'s finding that this contract now needs to do real institutional-arrangement design (not just activation), the qualification gap is more consequential than it would be for a pure activation task. Reuse the CRDB review's redline rather than rediscovering it: require demonstrated published or applied experience in climate adaptation evaluation methodology, not just legal capacity to hold a government contract.
