# RQ08: accountability, performance, and learning

## Lead question

How can Thailand distinguish forecast error, justified precaution, operational delay, capacity failure, and negligent non-action—and use that distinction for learning and accountability?

## Decision informed

The scorecard, review process, disclosure rules, and consequences needed to improve performance without recreating incentives for late, overly cautious warnings.

## Related atoms

ART-C02, ART-C08, ART-C09, ART-N02, ART-P06, LAW-C07, LAW-P11, LAW-A08, REL-11, REL-17.

## Assumptions to test

- End-to-end performance can be measured without attributing all failure to the forecaster.
- Transparent review can improve trust and practice rather than intensify blame avoidance.
- Avoidable loss can be estimated credibly enough to inform policy.
- Consequences for non-performance can coexist with protected professional judgment under uncertainty.

## Subquestions

1. Which indicators measure forecast skill, impact accuracy, warning timeliness, trigger compliance, resource release, reach, assisted action, and outcomes?
2. What is the unit of evaluation: event, warning, agency, location, season, or action protocol?
3. How should justified false alarms and near misses be recorded?
4. What evidence is necessary to label loss preventable rather than unavoidable?
5. Who conducts event review, and how independent must that body be?
6. Which findings should be public, and what security or privacy limits apply?
7. When should a finding lead to recalibration, training, resource investment, administrative action, political accountability, compensation, or legal liability?
8. How can public scorecards include unofficial forecasts without granting them institutional authority?

## Evidence and method

- Review forecast-verification, incident-review, audit, safety-investigation, and anticipatory-action evaluation models.
- Build a scorecard spanning the entire chain rather than counting only forecasts or messages.
- Test the scorecard on historical events to identify unavailable data and perverse incentives.
- Separate confidential learning review from public accountability while defining how serious findings cross between them.

## Completion criteria

- Metrics identify the stage and owner of performance rather than collapsing results into “government failure.”
- False alarms, misses, delayed actions, and successful prevention are treated consistently.
- Review outputs have defined routes into trigger revision, plans, budgets, training, and accountability.
- Disclosure supports public understanding without exposing personal data or operationally sensitive information.

## Boundaries

This brief designs an accountability system; it does not pre-judge liability for the September 2026 event.
