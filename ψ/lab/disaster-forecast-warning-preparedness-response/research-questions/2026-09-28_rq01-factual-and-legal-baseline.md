# RQ01: factual and legal baseline

## Lead question

Which event, expenditure, institutional, legal, and comparative claims in the two source documents are supported by primary evidence, and which must be corrected, qualified, or removed?

## Decision informed

Whether the source narratives are reliable enough to support further diagnosis and legislative design.

## Related atoms

ART-F01–ART-F10, ART-A04, ART-A08, LAW-F01–LAW-F04, LAW-A01, LAW-A02, REL-18.

## Assumptions to test

- The September 2026 Bangkok event occurred in the sequence described.
- The THB 949 million figure is correctly scoped and comparable across agencies.
- Existing disaster and financial law lacks sufficient pre-impact authority.
- The cited regional commitments have relevant domestic implications.
- Comparative anticipatory-action cases support the claimed benefits.

## Subquestions

1. What were the timestamped forecasts, rainfall observations, water levels, official warnings, Cell Broadcast messages, orders, and field actions?
2. Which FY2026 appropriations count as forecasting or monitoring, and what does the article exclude?
3. What powers and duties already exist under the Disaster Prevention and Mitigation Act, national plan, governor/local-authority laws, budget rules, emergency-advance regulations, and related instruments?
4. What is the status and relevant content of the proposed Climate Change Act and proposed disaster-law amendment?
5. What exactly did Thailand endorse under ASEAN, Sendai, Paris, and related frameworks?
6. What do the Nan and international cases actually demonstrate about cost, timing, and outcomes?
7. Which climate-attribution and infrastructure-capacity statements are supported by event-specific analysis?

## Evidence and method

- Build a claim-verification table keyed to every `ART-F` and `LAW-F` atom.
- Prefer official timestamps, archived alerts, rainfall observations, meeting records, appropriation documents, enacted laws, regulations, cabinet decisions, and primary evaluations.
- Use media reports only to locate primary material or document public statements.
- Ask legal specialists to distinguish absent authority from unused or ambiguously interpreted authority.

## Completion criteria

- Every factual atom is marked confirmed, qualified, contradicted, or unresolved with a citation.
- The legal baseline distinguishes statute, subordinate rule, plan, formal mandate, and actual practice.
- The THB 949 million figure is reproducible from a documented inclusion rule.
- No later brief relies on a contradicted claim without explicitly treating it as a disputed hypothesis.

## Boundaries

This brief verifies the baseline. It does not yet design new statutory language or calculate a full economic appraisal.
