# RQ02: warning workflow and decision rights

## Lead question

What is the real end-to-end workflow—from forecast production to local protective action—and where do information, authority, responsibility, or time get lost?

## Decision informed

Which institutional link needs clarification, redesign, or legal change, rather than assuming “fragmentation” is one undifferentiated problem.

## Related atoms

ART-F02, ART-F04, ART-C01, ART-C04, ART-C05, LAW-P02–LAW-P05, LAW-A02, REL-01, REL-02.

## Assumptions to test

- No actor currently owns the whole conversion from hazard forecast to local impact and action.
- Formal mandates differ from actual operating practice.
- Approval and escalation delays materially reduce useful lead time.
- Local observations are not integrated quickly into national warning decisions.

## Subquestions

1. Who produces each forecast, impact interpretation, alert recommendation, official warning, public message, operational order, and field action?
2. Which actor can initiate Cell Broadcast, through what approval path, and using which evidence?
3. How do Bangkok, provinces, DDPM/NDWC, TMD, water agencies, and local authorities exchange information during an event?
4. At each handoff, what format, system, service standard, sign-off, and expected time applies?
5. Who can escalate when local observations conflict with a national forecast?
6. Where do actors perceive personal, legal, political, or audit risk?
7. Which failure points are mandate gaps, coordination gaps, capacity gaps, or deliberate risk decisions?

## Evidence and method

- Reconstruct at least two events as timestamped process traces, including one failure and one comparatively successful warning.
- Compare formal SOPs and organizational mandates with interviews and system logs.
- Produce a swimlane showing actor, input, decision, output, authority, elapsed time, and downstream dependency.
- Validate the reconstructed workflow with representatives from each participating institution rather than accepting a single-agency account.

## Completion criteria

- One verified current-state workflow covers forecast to field action.
- Every decision point has a named formal and practical owner.
- Delay and failure modes are localized to specific handoffs.
- The research can state which changes need law, SOP, system integration, staffing, or no structural change.

## Boundaries

This brief maps the current workflow. It does not select trigger values or prescribe a new organization chart.
