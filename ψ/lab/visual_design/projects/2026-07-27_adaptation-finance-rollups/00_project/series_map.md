# Series map

## Series thesis

Help adaptation project teams move from project definition to an appropriate funding route and a submission-ready proposal.

## Roll-up sequence

### Roll-up 01 — Adaptation Finance Quick Start

Universal orientation: readiness, screening, access-route selection, and the funding lifecycle.

### Roll-up 02 — GCF and AF Application Playbook

International route execution: GCF’s documented pathway, plus the source-supported shared route for AF.

## Reading sequence

1. Use Roll-up 01 to understand the lifecycle and prepare the project inputs.
2. Use Roll-up 02 when considering GCF or AF and assigning institutional roles.

## Banner architecture

- Format: 60 × 160 cm portrait.
- Each roll-up: four topics maximum.
- Shared composition: title zone → orientation zone → central key visual → four-topic content stack → action/footer zone.
- The central visual is the climate-finance collage, adapted with DCCE–Creagy colors on white.

## Boundary

The source gives detailed GCF information but does not provide AF-specific thresholds, program details, or timelines. Roll-up 02 must visibly preserve that boundary.
