# Project brief

## Working title

Adaptation Finance: Two Practical Roll-ups

## Audience

- Roll-up 1: project owners, local authorities, and adaptation teams preparing a first funding submission.
- Roll-up 2: proposal developers and implementing partners considering GCF or AF routes.

## Intended use

Two practical orientation panels that help readers move from an adaptation project idea to a funding route and submission pathway.

## Required structure

1. **Adaptation Finance Quick Start** — readiness and the funding lifecycle.
2. **GCF and AF Application Playbook** — GCF-led preparation with an AF routing lane.

## Source boundary

Use only the supplied Thai guide: `ψ/inbox/คู่มือและข้อเสนอแนะการเข้าถึงแหล่งทุนด้านการปรับตัวต่อสภาพภูมิอากาศ.md`.

The source gives detailed GCF information. It names AF as a multilateral climate fund and includes AF in the shared accredited-entity / endorsement pathway, but does not provide detailed AF-specific thresholds or procedures. Do not infer them.

## Reader outcome

Readers should leave with an actionable next step, not a general description of climate finance.

## Format

Two vertical banners, each **60 × 160 cm**. The content must occupy the full height with generous breathing room, while staying scannable at standing distance.

## Composition constraint

Each banner has no more than four content topics. The climate-finance collage remains the central key visual; text and diagrams frame it above and below rather than replacing it.
