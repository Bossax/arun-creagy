# Roll-up 01 — Visual element register

| Topic | Small visual cue | Reader job | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prepare the project | Project team, plan, and map fragments | Make capacity building concrete | Upper-right and opposite edge crops |
| Match the project to funding | Generic banknote, calculator, and funding document | Signal financial fit | Medium right-edge cluster |
| Choose the access route | Editable branching diagram | Show the actual decision | No photographic cue |
| Complete the lifecycle | Agreement folder plus project-delivery/infrastructure fragment | Signal formalization and delivery | Opposing lower-edge crops |

## Composition rule

All photographic fragments combined occupy 25–35% of the banner. The workflow, decision fields, and route diagram remain the primary visual content.
