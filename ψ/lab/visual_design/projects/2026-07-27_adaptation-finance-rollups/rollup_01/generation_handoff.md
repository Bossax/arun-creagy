# Roll-up 01 — Generation handoff

## Image-generation brief — no text

Generate a **text-free** 60 × 160 cm portrait visual scaffold on a white canvas. Do not render letters, words, numbers, labels, badges, logos, currency denominations, icons containing text, or pseudo-typography.

Use `00_project/climate finance un theme.jpg` only for editorial texture and collage contrast—never its objects, composition, or crop. Use the palette: teal `#358799`, mint `#1DBF99`, sage `#95C6A6`, rose `#D9788D`, and blush `#EBB8BB`.

Create four clearly separated, empty content zones stacked vertically, each with generous blank space for later editable copy and diagrams. Restore Draft 0-level editorial density through **original distributed imagery**: a large cropped project-team-and-plan fragment at the upper-right; a smaller map/plan fragment near Topic 1; a banknote/calculator/funding-document cluster entering from the right around Topic 2; no photograph in the route zone; and cropped agreement/folder plus small project-delivery/infrastructure fragments at the lower edges around Topic 4. Use diagonal teal, mint, sage, rose, and blush edge fields to connect these fragments. Combined imagery may occupy 25–35% of the banner, but must not cover text zones or form one central hero collage.

## Layout stage — text authority

Do not ask the image generator to render any content. Add all final copy, routes, arrows, numbers, and source notes in the design application using:

- `layout_text_en.md` for English
- `layout_text_th.md` for Thai

These files are the sole authority for display text.

## Source boundary

Use the layout-copy source notes, not generated imagery, for attribution.
