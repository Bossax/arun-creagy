# Review v02 — content versus visual hierarchy

## Finding

The large central worktable was visually dominant but did not explain the funding workflow as well as the decision fields and route choices do.

## Decision

Remove the central visual cluster. Distribute small contextual photographic fragments across the four topic blocks and make the content architecture the primary visual.

## Required hierarchy

1. Four topic blocks and their decisions
2. Five-field funding match and access-route diagram
3. Small topic-linked photographic cues
4. Footer action
