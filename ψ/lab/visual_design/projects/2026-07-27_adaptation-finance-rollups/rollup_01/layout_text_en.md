## Title

From project idea to funding submission

## Subtitle

A quick-start lifecycle for adaptation teams

## Central caption

A fundable project connects need, activity, finance, and delivery.

## Topic 1 — Prepare the project

Define the adaptation purpose. Organize activities. State the funding need.

## Topic 2 — Match the project to funding

Screen: target sector | eligible entity | supported activity | financial instrument | co-financing option.

## Topic 3 — Choose the access route

Direct application | Through an Accredited/Implementing Entity | Accredited Entity-led application.

## Topic 4 — Complete the funding lifecycle

Proposal → review → approval → agreement → implementation.
