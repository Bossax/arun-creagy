# Roll-up 01 — Visual plan

## Format and hierarchy

- **Canvas:** 60 × 160 cm portrait, white background.
- **Topic limit:** four topics only.
- **No hero visual:** remove the large central worktable collage.
- **Content allocation:** approximately 60% workflow and decision content, 30% distributed photographic/collage framing, and 10% whitespace and structural separation.
- **Reading path:** title → Topic 1 → Topic 2 → Topic 3 → Topic 4 → action footer.
- **Breathing room:** use white space and simple rules between topic blocks; no decorative central cluster.

## Four-topic composition

## Theme application

Use Draft 0's editorial density and the climate-finance reference's collage energy, but build an original composition: white canvas, teal structure, mint readiness, sage secondary panels, rose decisions, and blush soft blocks. Use several medium-scale grayscale fragments at the outer edges and between chapter transitions; do not create a copied central cluster or reuse reference objects.

- **Top zone:** title, subtitle, and a short promise, balanced by a large cropped grayscale fragment of a project team reviewing plans at the upper-right edge. Use a teal diagonal field behind the crop.
- **Topic 1 — Prepare the project:** three short input labels, paired with a smaller plan/map fragment at the opposite edge. This cue communicates capacity building and preparation.
- **Topic 2 — Match the project to funding:** five screening fields occupy the main area. Frame them with an unmarked banknote, calculator, and funding-document fragment that enters from the right edge.
- **Topic 3 — Choose the access route:** the three route options are the visual. Use an editable branching diagram; no photographic object is needed.
- **Topic 4 — Complete the lifecycle:** proposal → review → approval → agreement → implementation. Add a cropped agreement/folder fragment and a small original project-delivery/infrastructure fragment at opposing lower corners.
- **Bottom action strip:** “Start here: define the project, fill the five fields, shortlist funds.”

Reader job: establish sequence → show screening → explain routing → prompt action.
