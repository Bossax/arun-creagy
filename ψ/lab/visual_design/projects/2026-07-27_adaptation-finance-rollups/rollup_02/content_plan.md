# Roll-up 02 — Content plan

## Title

GCF and AF: route the proposal before you write it

## Reader outcome

The reader can prepare an international proposal, distinguish the documented GCF path from the source-supported AF route, and identify the final submission checkpoint.

## Four-topic architecture

## Layout thesis

The two fund routes—not a decorative illustration—are the key visual. GCF and AF occupy equal-width, equal-status route panels fed by one shared preparation gate and resolved by one shared submission checkpoint.

### 1. Prepare the evidence package

Bring together the adaptation purpose, activities, feasibility evidence, risk analysis, environmental and social assessment, and economic justification.

### 2. Follow the GCF route

Concept Note → NDA review/NOL → Accredited Entity full proposal → GCF review/approval → FAA.

### 3. Follow the AF route

Project concept → Accredited/Implementing Entity → national endorsement/NOL → fund submission. The source does not provide AF-specific thresholds or program details.

### 4. Pass the submission checkpoint

Confirm the partner, endorsement/NOL pathway, evidence package, and fund-specific requirements before submission.

## Insight Card

1. **Reader question:** How should I route an international adaptation proposal toward GCF or AF?
2. **Source-specific finding:** For international multilateral funds such as GCF and AF, the project developer generally does not submit directly; the proposal moves through an Accredited/Implementing Entity and requires national endorsement or an NOL.
3. **Mechanism:** The Accredited Entity develops/submits the proposal while the national coordinating authority checks policy alignment and issues the endorsement/NOL where required.
4. **Consequence:** Teams must secure the right institutional partner and national clearance before treating the proposal as submission-ready.
5. **Visual proof:** Two equal-status route panels: GCF’s documented five-step route and AF’s source-supported four-step route, both connected to the same preparation and submission gates.
6. **Evidence anchor:** Source 01, lines 30, 34, 38–51, 157–159, and 181–182.

## Action takeaway

Identify the partner → address endorsement/NOL → assemble the evidence package → verify fund-specific requirements.
