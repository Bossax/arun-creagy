# Review v01 — central visual originality

## Finding

The initial central visual direction was too close to the supplied climate-finance reference in both object language and conceptual shorthand. It did not make the roll-up’s institutional access mechanism visible enough.

## Preserve

- Editorial grayscale collage treatment
- White canvas and DCCE–Creagy palette
- Central visual as the main attention anchor
- Four-topic GCF/AF structure

## Change

- Replace generic finance-plus-climate objects with an original proposal-to-partnership table.
- Show project staff and institutional partners reviewing a dossier, map, financial instruments, and endorsement artefact.
- Make the route through partnership and national clearance visually legible.

## Do not change

- 60 × 160 cm portrait format
- GCF-specific sequence and AF verification boundary
- Editable final labels and source notes
