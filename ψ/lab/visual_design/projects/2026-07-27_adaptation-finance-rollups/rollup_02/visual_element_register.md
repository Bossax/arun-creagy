# Roll-up 02 — Visual element register

| Zone | Visual cue | Reader job | Placement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Header | Original project/adaptation-infrastructure vignette | Establish climate-adaptation context | Upper-right edge crop |
| Shared preparation | Small dossier or evidence fragment | Signal project readiness | Outer edge, never central |
| GCF–AF comparison | No image inside panels | Preserve equal comparison hierarchy | Panels remain clear |
| Submission/footer | Project-team or delivery fragment | Close with delivery and accountability | Lower outer corner |

## Composition rule

Use 25–30% distributed edge imagery and color framing. GCF and AF panels retain equal visual weight and remain the dominant proof.
