# Roll-up 02 — Visual plan

## Format and hierarchy

- **Canvas:** 60 × 160 cm portrait, white background.
- **Topic limit:** four topics only.
- **Key visual:** the GCF–AF comparison itself. No large central illustration.
- **Reading path:** title → shared preparation gate → equal GCF and AF panels → shared submission checkpoint → role/footer strip.
- **Breathing room:** use large route cards, clear vertical space, and short labels; do not add a fund directory or decorative central image.

## Theme application

Use teal and sage to distinguish the two route panels without implying that either fund is more important: teal for GCF and sage for AF. Both panels must have identical width, header height, card structure, and visual weight. Use mint only for the shared “ready / endorsed” checkpoints, rose for small warning/verification emphasis, and blush for quiet panel fills. Recover Draft 0's editorial density with original grayscale edge vignettes, diagonal color wedges, and cropped project/finance/environment fragments—but never a central scene or a hierarchy that competes with the comparison.

## Four-topic composition

- **Top zone (14%):** title, subtitle, and a short question, balanced by a cropped original project-landscape or adaptation-infrastructure vignette at the upper-right with a teal diagonal field.
- **Topic 1 — shared preparation gate (18%):** a full-width card showing six compact evidence inputs: adaptation purpose, activities, feasibility, risk, environmental/social assessment, and economic justification.
- **Topics 2 and 3 — equal route comparison (48%):** two equal-width vertical panels placed side by side. Frame the outer edges with subtle cropped financial-document and project-delivery fragments; do not place imagery between or over the panels.
  - **GCF panel:** Concept Note → NDA review/NOL → Accredited Entity full proposal → GCF review/approval → FAA.
  - **AF panel:** Project concept → Accredited/Implementing Entity → national endorsement/NOL → fund submission. Add a clear small note: “Fund-specific requirements: verify separately.”
- **Topic 4 — shared submission checkpoint (14%):** a full-width card: partner identified + endorsement/NOL addressed + evidence ready + fund-specific requirements checked.
- **Bottom role strip (6%):** project developer | Accredited/Implementing Entity | NDA/DA | fund, with a restrained lower-corner project-team or resilient-infrastructure fragment to complete the vertical rhythm.

Reader job: prepare once → compare two equal routes → complete the shared submission gate.
