# Roll-up 02 — Generation handoff

## Image-generation brief — no text

Generate a **text-free** 60 × 160 cm portrait comparison scaffold on a white canvas. Do not render letters, words, numbers, labels, badges, logos, currency denominations, icons containing text, or pseudo-typography.

The visual scaffold has one empty full-width card at the top, two empty equal-width and equal-height panels in the middle, one empty full-width card below, and a narrow footer strip. The two middle panels must have identical visual hierarchy and white space. Restore Draft 0-level editorial density through original distributed edge vignettes: cropped project/adaptation infrastructure at upper-right, financial-document fragments at the outer mid-edges, and a project-team or delivery fragment in a lower corner. Use diagonal color wedges to frame these vignettes. Do not create a central illustration or any imagery between or over the two route panels.

Use only a faint outer-edge abstract texture inspired by `00_project/climate finance un theme.jpg`; never use its objects, composition, crop, or colour fields. Use teal `#358799`, sage `#95C6A6`, mint `#1DBF99`, rose `#D9788D`, and blush `#EBB8BB`.
