# Visual plan — Nature-based Solutions rollup

## Format and composition authority

- Output: one 60 × 160 cm portrait rollup (3:8).
- Layout authority: `layout_prototype.html`.
- Style authority: `DESIGN.md` and `iunc-reference.png` for flat editorial-vector illustration language only.
- Final typography, citations, and detailed evidence remain editable in Canva.
- Composition rule: one continuous vertical illustrated field. No dashboard, tile grid, or repeated boxed modules.

## Communication thesis

NbS use healthy ecosystems to address societal challenges. EbA is the climate-adaptation application within NbS. The ecological mechanism works only at scale when an enabling route connects knowledge, policy, capable administration, finance, local action, maintenance, monitoring, and learning.

## Reading path and zones

| Zone | Height | Reader job | Visible form | Editable text space |
|---|---:|---|---|---|
| Header | 18% | Introduce NbS and its definition. | Deep-teal field that opens into the landscape below. | Large title and 2–3 sentence definition. |
| NbS / EbA relationship | 20% | Show the correct relationship between terms. | One NbS canopy/umbrella with EbA as a connected adaptation branch; no icon cluster. | Two labels and two explanatory paragraphs. |
| Ecosystem mechanism | 37% | Show how ecosystems reduce climate risk. | Connected forest, wetland/river, farm/soil, and urban green-blue landscape. Thin leader lines point to descriptive callouts. | Introductory sentence and four 1–2 sentence callouts. |
| Adoption route | 19% | Explain why uptake stalls and what enables concrete action. | The same landscape becomes one vertical yellow-green route; small cayenne friction marks sit beside it. | Introductory paragraph, short barrier phrase, eight route labels. |
| Footer | 6% | State safeguard and sources without competing with the story. | Quiet soft-linen strip and fine evergreen rule. | Caution and source line. |

## Visual system

- The connected landscape is the dominant visual anchor; it must show functions, not decorative scenery.
- The NbS canopy/umbrella and the vertical enabling route are the two recurring connectors.
- Use one NbS/EbA relationship marker, leader lines, roots, water curves, and path lines instead of cards, icon grids, or arrows inside boxes.
- Text is the primary explanatory medium. Illustration supports the sentences; it does not replace them with repeated symbols.
- Use flat organic silhouettes, thin-to-medium evergreen outlines, flat fills, minimal layered depth, and calm editorial rhythm.
- Palette roles: soft linen for breathing space; dark teal and evergreen for structure; yellow-green for ecosystem vitality and enabling action; cayenne only for hazard, friction, or safeguard emphasis.
- Do not introduce extra colours, gradients, photorealism, painterly texture, cinematic lighting, 3D rendering, or generic corporate infographic motifs.

## Physical and accessibility controls

- Keep all essential text in one central reading column; do not rely on narrow side columns.
- Make the title and section headings readable from several steps away; keep callouts short.
- Pair colour with form: route versus friction, roots versus water, and open-circle symbols remain distinct in greyscale.
- Preserve clear soft-linen negative space around text; no generated final citations or dense paragraphs.
- The HTML prototype must retain the 3:8 proportion and each zone height above. It is not rendered as an image.
