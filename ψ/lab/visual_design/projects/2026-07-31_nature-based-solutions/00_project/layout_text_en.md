# Layout text — Nature-based Solutions rollup

## Header

**Title**  
What are Nature-based Solutions?

**Definition**  
Nature-based Solutions protect, conserve, restore, sustainably use, or manage ecosystems to address societal challenges. They can benefit people, biodiversity, and resilience when they are well designed and maintained.

## NbS and EbA

**Nature-based Solutions**  
The broad approach: working with ecosystems to address societal challenges.

**Ecosystem-based Adaptation**  
The climate-adaptation application: using biodiversity and ecosystem services to help people adapt to climate change.

## How NbS helps adaptation

**Heading**  
Healthy ecosystems can change the path from climate hazard to human risk.

**Forest and soil**  
Roots stabilise land, reduce erosion, and help regulate the movement of water.

**Wetlands and rivers**  
Slow, store, and absorb water—reducing flood risk while supporting water security.

**Farms and healthy soils**  
Support food, water, livelihoods, and ecological resilience through healthier land and farming systems.

**Urban green-blue space**  
Helps regulate heat, support health, and improve everyday resilience in cities.

## What enables adoption

**Heading**  
What makes adoption concrete?

**Explanation**  
Policy announcements and pilots alone do not deliver NbS. Implementation needs a connected system of knowledge, policy, capable public administration, finance, local action, maintenance, and learning.

**Friction**  
Fragmented authority · weak appraisal · limited capacity · scattered finance · unclear maintenance

**Route**  
Knowledge → policy → capacity → appraisal → budget and finance → local pilot → monitor and learn → scale

## Footer

NbS are context-specific and complementary. They require inclusive governance, ecological safeguards, long-term maintenance, and monitoring.
