# Project brief — Nature-based Solutions

**Project type:** Single rollup  
**Date:** 2026-07-31  
**Audience:** General policy and climate-adaptation audience; final audience wording remains to be confirmed during layout planning.  
**Purpose:** Explain what Nature-based Solutions are, how Ecosystem-based Adaptation relates to NbS, how NbS supports climate change adaptation, and what enables implementation at scale.

## Required questions

1. What is Nature-based Solutions?
2. How do Ecosystem-based Adaptation and Nature-based Solutions relate?
3. How do NbS help climate change adaptation?
4. What enables NbS: knowledge, policy, budgeting, public administration, governance, finance, and participation?

## Working thesis

Nature-based Solutions use healthy, protected, restored, or sustainably managed ecosystems to address societal challenges. Ecosystem-based Adaptation is the climate-adaptation application of this broader approach. NbS can reduce climate risk and provide co-benefits, but only when ecological integrity, local knowledge, inclusive governance, policy integration, and long-term finance are in place.

## Scope boundaries

- Explain the relationship between NbS, EbA, and CCA.
- Show adaptation mechanisms rather than listing projects only.
- Explain enabling conditions as an implementation system.
- Do not imply that NbS replace all grey infrastructure, emissions reduction, or other adaptation measures.
- Do not claim that every action involving vegetation or ecosystems is automatically a high-integrity NbS.
