The broad approach: protecting, restoring, and sustainably managing ecosystems to address societal challenges.
EbA

Ecosystem-based Adaptation

The climate-adaptation application within NbS: biodiversity and ecosystem services help people adapt.

Healthy ecosystems can change the path from climate hazard to human risk.

They regulate water, soil, heat, food, and livelihoods—reducing exposure and strengthening adaptive capacity.

Forest and soil

Roots stabilise land, reduce erosion, and help regulate water.

Farms and healthy soils

Support food, water, livelihoods, and ecological resilience.

Wetlands and rivers

Slow and store water to reduce flood risk and support water security.

Urban green-blue space

Helps regulate heat and supports health and everyday resilience.
