# Stage 2 Visual Plan — Roll-up 01: ข้อมูลฉายภาพการเปลี่ยนแปลงสภาพภูมิอากาศ

## 1. Visual Concept & Layout Aspect Ratio
- **Banner Aspect Ratio**: `1:2.5` (Physical banner 80 cm x 200 cm).
- **Design Tokens**: Egyptian Blue (`#0A369D`), Sapphire Sky (`#4472CA`), Glaucous (`#5E7CE2`), Baby Blue Ice (`#92B4F4`), Pale Sky (`#CFDEE7`).
- **Reading Stack**: 3 Vertical Zones (Zone A: Projections vs Forecasts, Zone B: 5 Sector Applications & Scale Gap, Zone C: Insight Card 01 & 5-Element Checklist).

---

## 2. Zone Architecture & Element Register

### **Zone A (Top 30%): Hero & Foundation**
- DCCE Brand Header + Main Headline + Badges (`CMIP6`, `GCM`, `SSP`, `Decision Support`).
- Comparison Matrix Card (Weather Forecast vs Climate Projection).

### **Zone B (Middle 45%): Decision Support & Spatial Scale**
- 5 Sector Application Cards (Water, Agriculture, Infrastructure, Disaster Risk, Policy).
- Spatial Scale Contrast Graphic (GCM 100km coarse grid over Thailand map vs 5km fine grid).

### **Zone C (Bottom 25%): Insight Card 01 & Checklist**
- Insight Card 01 (Multi-model ensemble necessity).
- 5-Element Stacked Checklist Container (`Model`, `Scenario`, `Time Horizon`, `Variable`, `Spatial Scale`).
