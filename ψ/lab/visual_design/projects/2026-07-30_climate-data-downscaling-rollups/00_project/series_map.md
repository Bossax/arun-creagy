# Series map — Climate data downscaling roll-ups

## Shared series promise

**From global climate simulations to usable local climate information for Thailand.**

The panels should read as a sequence, but each must stand alone without relying on the other for basic comprehension.

| Roll-up | Working reader question | Role in the sequence | Candidate source basis | Boundary for Stage 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 — Climate projection data & decision support | What is climate projection data, why does it matter for Thailand, and how can scenarios support decisions under uncertainty? | Explains projection fundamentals, scenario logic, and decision applications across key sectors. | IMG-01, SCI-02, WEB-02 | Differentiate projections from forecasts; establish 5-element interpretation framework. |
| 02 — Downscaling & DCCE product family | How does downscaling translate global models into local information, and what are the 4 DCCE data products? | Explains spatial scale gap, statistical vs dynamical downscaling, and compares GridData, WRF-Chem, RegCM5, and Statistical Downscaling. | IMG-02, IMG-03, WEB-01, SCI-01 | Verify product names and downscaling physics; establish 4-product comparison matrix. |

## Continuity rules

- Use one shared project-level design system, while giving each panel a distinct dominant visual and reader job.
- Roll-up 01 ends with the requirement for spatial downscaling and multi-model ensemble interpretation.
- Roll-up 02 begins with the scale translation bridge, then introduces the four DCCE high-resolution products.
- Keep any comparison, resolution, model, scenario, or time-range details in the panel that can evidence them; do not duplicate numbers merely for symmetry.

## Stage 0 decisions still open

- Final physical dimensions, display environment, logos, mandatory partner marks, and safe zones.
- Whether the platform access sequence is a general orientation or an exact current procedural guide.
- Whether an official DCCE design system or event-specific visual identity must supersede the supplied reference images.
