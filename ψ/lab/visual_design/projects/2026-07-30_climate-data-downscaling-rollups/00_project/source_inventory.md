## Reference-use rules

- The images are supplied content and visual references. Do not clone their layouts, icon arrangements, or generated-style imagery.
- Distinguish source-supported facts from design metaphors. A globe-to-grid motif may explain scale translation, but cannot add a claim.
- Any future platform screenshot must be captured or verified from the live service during Stage 1/3; supplied screenshots are not assumed current.
