# Series map — Climate data downscaling roll-ups

## Shared series promise

**From global climate simulations to usable local climate information for Thailand.**

The panels should read as a sequence, but each must stand alone without relying on the other for basic comprehension.

| Roll-up | Working reader question | Role in the sequence | Candidate source basis | Boundary for Stage 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 — From global model to local climate data | Why are global climate-model projections translated to a finer spatial scale, and what are the two approaches? | Explains the data-production logic and the methodological distinction. | IMG-01, IMG-02, IMG-03 | Establish one defensible source-specific insight; do not turn this into a model catalogue. |
| 02 — Find and request Thailand climate projections | What can the Thailand Climate Projection Platform help a user locate, and what is the verified request path? | Turns the production story into a practical access and use path. | IMG-04, IMG-05, WEB-01 | Verify current platform fields, services, terms, and access steps before any final instruction or interface depiction. |

## Continuity rules

- Use one shared project-level design system, while giving each panel a distinct dominant visual and reader job.
- Roll-up 01 ends with the outcome: high-resolution climate information for decision support.
- Roll-up 02 begins with that outcome, then shows the verified platform pathway.
- Keep any comparison, resolution, model, scenario, or time-range details in the panel that can evidence them; do not duplicate numbers merely for symmetry.

## Stage 0 decisions still open

- Final physical dimensions, display environment, logos, mandatory partner marks, and safe zones.
- Whether the platform access sequence is a general orientation or an exact current procedural guide.
- Whether an official DCCE design system or event-specific visual identity must supersede the supplied reference images.
