# Edit Note — Roll-up 02 Draft 1

**Asset reviewed:** `img/draft1.png`  
**Project:** Downscaling and DCCE Climate-Data Products  
**Iteration:** Draft 1 → Draft 2

## Overall assessment

Draft 1 is directionally strong and substantially aligned with Roll-up 01. It uses the shared DCCE blue visual language and presents a clear sequence:

> spatial problem → two mechanisms → four products → selection questions

The main remaining problems are production accuracy, copy capacity, and physical readability.

## What works

- The overall visual language is consistent with Roll-up 01.
- The scale-translation visual makes the spatial problem understandable.
- Dynamical and statistical downscaling are visibly distinct.
- The four product cards have separate visual identities.
- The central message strip gives the panel a clear thesis.
- The composition is suitable for later Canva assembly.

## What did not go as planned

### 1. The aspect ratio is incorrect

Draft 1 is `793 × 1983 px`, approximately `0.40`. The required 60 × 160 cm format is `0.375`.

At 160 cm high, the current image becomes approximately 64 × 160 cm. Cropping it to 60 cm would remove side content and reduce the width of the product cards.

### 2. Explanatory copy is still placeholder content

Most of the following areas contain lines instead of the approved Thai copy:

- Why downscaling
- Dynamical/statistical comparison
- Four product descriptions
- Insight card body
- Four-question checklist

This is acceptable as a visual scaffold, but Draft 2 must typeset the exact copy from `layout_text_th.md` in Canva.

### 3. Product cards are visually stronger than textually informative

The illustrations communicate that the products differ, but the reader cannot yet understand the differences without editable copy. Each product card needs room for:

- What the product is
- What makes it different
- Typical use
- Verified format information where available

### 4. The insight card is incomplete

The heading is present, but the explanation of why both methods exist is missing. This is a key meaning-bearing section and must contain the full approved insight copy.

### 5. Palette drift

The Statistical Downscaling product card uses a purple accent that is outside the approved Roll-up 01 palette. Replace it with Glaucous (`#5E7CE2`) or Sapphire Sky (`#4472CA`).

### 6. Statistical mechanism needs a clearer relationship

The current visual shows charts and a station-like symbol, but the mechanism should read more clearly as:

> model data + local/reference observations → statistical relationship → local estimate

### 7. Physical readability is unresolved

After correcting the aspect ratio, the four product cards will become narrower. The Thai copy may become too small unless the card descriptions are shortened or the product section receives more vertical space.

## Required changes for Draft 2

1. Rebuild on an exact 60 × 160 cm / 3:8 canvas.
2. Preserve the overall composition and successful illustrations.
3. Replace all placeholder lines with the exact Thai copy from `layout_text_th.md`.
4. Give the insight card enough space for its full explanation.
5. Make the statistical data flow visually explicit.
6. Remove the purple accent and use the approved blue palette.
7. Check product-card copy at physical viewing distance.
8. Keep all final text, product labels, formats, citations, and QR elements editable in Canva.
9. Verify product metadata and resolution claims before final typesetting.

## Acceptance criteria for Draft 2

- Exact 60 × 160 cm / 3:8 composition.
- Same illustration family as Roll-up 01.
- No placeholder lines remain in the final Canva layout.
- The two mechanisms are understandable without relying only on product names.
- Each product has a clear role and use case.
- The insight card explains why both methods are needed.
- The four selection questions remain readable at roll-up distance.
- No unsupported universal resolution or file-format claim appears.
