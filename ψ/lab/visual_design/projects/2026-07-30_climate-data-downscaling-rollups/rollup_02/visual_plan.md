# Stage 2 Visual Plan — Roll-up 02: การลดสเกลและชุดผลิตภัณฑ์ข้อมูล

## 1. Visual Concept & Layout Aspect Ratio
- **Banner Aspect Ratio**: `1:2.5` (Physical banner 80 cm x 200 cm).
- **Design Tokens**: Sky Blue Accent (`#0284C7`), Egyptian Blue (`#0A369D`), Sapphire Sky (`#4472CA`), Baby Blue Ice (`#92B4F4`), Pale Sky (`#CFDEE7`).
- **Reading Stack**: 3 Vertical Zones (Zone A: Downscaling Mechanism Bridge, Zone B: 4 DCCE Data Product Matrix, Zone C: Insight Card 02 & Format Access).

---

## 2. Zone Architecture & Element Register

### **Zone A (Top 30%): Hero & Mechanism Bridge**
- DCCE Brand Header + Main Headline + Badges (`Dynamical`, `Statistical`, `RCM`, `NetCDF / CSV`).
- Downscaling Mechanism Comparison Card (RCM Physics vs Machine Learning Regression).

### **Zone B (Middle 45%): DCCE Data Product Family Matrix**
- 4 Product Cards Grid / Table:
  1. `GridData`: Gridded Raster (.nc, .csv) for GIS analysis.
  2. `WRF-Chem`: Dynamical RCM + Atmospheric Chemistry & Aerosols.
  3. `RegCM5`: Dynamical RCM Physics + Extreme Weather & Monsoons.
  4. `Statistical Downscaling`: Empirical ML Station Calibration.

### **Zone C (Bottom 25%): Insight Card 02 & Access Methods**
- Insight Card 02 (Why both dynamical & statistical options exist).
- Formats & Open Data Access Card (NetCDF, CSV, Thailand Climate Projection Platform).
