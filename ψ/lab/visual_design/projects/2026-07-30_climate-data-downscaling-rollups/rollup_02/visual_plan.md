# Draft 3 Visual Plan — Roll-up 02

## Direction

Roll-up 02 is the technical companion to Roll-up 01. It must use the same DCCE visual language, 60×160 cm format, Thai-first editable typography, rounded blue panels, thick Egyptian-blue outlines, pale-blue surfaces, restrained shading, and clear reading sequence.

## Format and shared design system

- **Final size:** 60 × 160 cm vertical roll-up
- **Aspect ratio:** 3:8
- **Palette:** Egyptian Blue `#0A369D`, Sapphire Sky `#4472CA`, Glaucous `#5E7CE2`, Baby Blue Ice `#92B4F4`, Pale Sky `#CFDEE7`, white, and restrained green accents.
- **Typography:** Sarabun or Kanit for Thai; Outfit for English technical labels.
- **Reference:** Use Roll-up 01 Draft 2 as the style reference, not as a source for Roll-up 02 content.
- **Text authority:** `layout_text_th.md` is exact editable copy. Do not render final text inside generated artwork.

## Core visual concept

**Translate the scale, then choose the tool.** A large global model grid enters the panel, passes through two different downscaling mechanisms, and becomes a family of products for different decisions.

## Reading sequence and zones

### Zone A — Establish the spatial problem

- DCCE header, headline, subheadline, and badges.
- Definition card: why global model information needs spatial translation.
- Scale-translation visual: coarse global grid → local context; use qualitative language and avoid universal downscaled resolution claims.

**Reader job:** Understand why the user needs a translated dataset before comparing methods.

### Zone B — Compare the mechanisms and products

- Two mechanism cards:
  - Dynamical downscaling: regional physical simulation through RCMs.
  - Statistical downscaling: empirical relationship and station/reference calibration.
- Four product cards with the exact product names, distinct role, typical use, and verified format information.

**Reader job:** Understand that methods are complementary and products serve different analytical jobs.

### Zone C — Make the choice actionable

- Central message strip: method choice depends on the question and decision.
- Insight card: why both methods exist.
- “ก่อนเลือกข้อมูล ควรถาม 4 คำถาม” checklist.
- DCCE platform/source footer and editable source note.

**Reader job:** Leave with a selection rule, not just four product names.

## Visual element register

| ID | Element | Visual role |
|---|---|---|
| `V02-01` | Coarse global grid entering a local-context lens | Show the spatial problem without implying a universal output resolution. |
| `V02-02` | Dynamical mechanism diagram | Show RCM physics, terrain, and atmospheric process. |
| `V02-03` | Statistical mechanism diagram | Show model/reference relationship and rapid calibration. |
| `V02-04` | Four product cards | Distinguish the DCCE product family by role and use. |
| `V02-05` | Four-question selection checklist | Convert comparison into an actionable choice. |

## Risks and controls

- Generated icons must not invent product labels, numbers, maps, or file metadata.
- Product cards must not imply that one product is universally best.
- Exact resolution and temporal coverage must be added only from verified metadata.
- Use the same visual weight and panel rhythm as Roll-up 01; avoid a dense spreadsheet-style table.
