## Core message

Downscaling translates global model information into data that can support local questions. Dynamical and statistical methods are complementary, and the right product depends on the decision, variable, scale, and analysis method.

## Required composition

1. Header: downscaling for local decision support.
2. Why downscaling: global grid and local-context translation.
3. Two mechanisms: dynamical versus statistical.
4. Central message: methods differ, but the goal is locally useful information.
5. Four DCCE products: GridData, WRF-Chem, RegCM5, Statistical Downscaling.
6. Insight card: why both methods exist.
7. Four-question product-selection checklist.
8. DCCE platform/source footer.
