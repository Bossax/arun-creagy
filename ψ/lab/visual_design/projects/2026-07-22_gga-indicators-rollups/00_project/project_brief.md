# Project Brief

## Working title
Thailand's Global Goal on Adaptation (GGA) Indicators Integration Roll-up Series

## Project type
Two connected 60 × 160 cm roll-up infographics designed to be read together as a single story.

## Core idea
The series provides an educational and technical overview of the Global Goal on Adaptation (Paris Agreement Article 7.1) and the UAE Framework for Global Climate Resilience. It details the evolutionary journey of these international frameworks and presents the comprehensive catalog of the 59 Belém Adaptation Indicators (in both English and Thai) to establish a shared reference for national policy planners.

## Intended audience
Policy officers, DCCE/sectoral agency staff, GGGI Thailand team, and stakeholders participating in national adaptation review workshops.

## Intended use
Exhibition banners for policy consultation workshops to orient participants on the global framework and the specific metrics adopted at CMA7 / COP30.

## Output format
- Physical roll-up banner
- Size: 60 cm wide × 160 cm high
- Orientation: vertical portrait
- Series length: 2 panels (designed as a continuous narrative)
- Intended viewing distance: 1–2.5 m
- Readable in standing workshop settings

## Storytelling objective
The two roll-ups lead the reader from the global framework's context to the specific indicators:

1. **Roll-up 1 — Understanding the GGA and UAE Framework**: Establish what the GGA is (Article 7.1), the timeline of its evolution (Paris Agreement to Glasgow and Dubai COP28), and the core structure of the UAE Framework (7 Thematic Targets and 4 Dimensional Targets).
2. **Roll-up 2 — The Belém Adaptation Indicators Catalog**: Present a complete, reference-grade bilingual index of all 59 Belém Adaptation Indicators (Target 9a-g and Target 10a-d) to serve as a checklist and translation dictionary for policy planners.

## Required content
- Overview of the UAE Framework for Global Climate Resilience (COP28) and Belém Adaptation Indicators (CMA7, 2025).
- Evolution timeline: 2015 Paris Agreement → 2021-2023 Glasgow-Sharm el-Sheikh work programme → 2023 UAE Framework → 2023-2025 UAE-Belém work programme → 2025 Belém Indicators.
- Mappings of the Thematic targets (9a-g) and Dimensional targets (10a-d).
- The complete bilingual catalog of the 59 indicators in English and Thai.
