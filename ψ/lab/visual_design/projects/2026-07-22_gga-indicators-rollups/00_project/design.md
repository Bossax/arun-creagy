# 1. Style DNA

## 1.1 Style keywords
`[abstract-geometric]`
`[minimalist]`
`[flat-color-blocked]`
`[restrained]`
`[institutional]`
`[sovereign]`
`[calm]`

## 1.2 Core style rules
1. **Abstract Geometry over Realism**: Graphics must use clean, abstract geometric shapes (concentric circles, semi-circle arcs, flat faceted polygons) rather than realistic or detailed drawings of objects. Real-world objects must be reduced to their simplest geometric icons.
2. **Restrained Graphic Density**: Graphics must NOT cover every inch of the canvas. Visuals must be isolated, precise focal anchors surrounded by ample negative space to avoid messy visual noise.
3. **Discrete, Flat Color Blocking**: No smooth continuous gradients or soft shading. Colors must use flat, jumpy/discrete color blocks and hard-edged steps.

| Light neutral | Sage Gray | `#789890` | Card borders, connectors, divider lines |

## 2.2 Color treatment
- **No Gradients**: Strictly zero continuous gradients or soft shadows. All color transitions must be flat, hard-edged, jumpy color blocks.

---

# 3. Illustration & Shape Language

## 3.1 Shape language
- Abstract geometric arcs, concentric circles, clean linear connectors, and flat faceted shapes.
- Corner style: Softly rounded (corner radius 8–12px).
- Outline style: Thin Sage Gray outlines (`#789890`).

## 3.2 Object simplification
- **High Abstraction**: Real-world objects (water, crops, health, buildings) must be expressed as minimalist, 2-color flat geometric symbols (e.g. a water drop is a simple geometric teardrop; a leaf is a flat geometric oval).
- **Prohibited**: Detailed drawings of real objects, realistic human figures, detailed trees, photorealistic solar panels, or complex landscapes.

# 5. Reusable Image-Generation Prompt Structure

```text
An abstract geometric flat vector infographic illustration for climate policy.

Visuals:
Abstract minimalist geometric shapes (concentric circles, semi-circle arcs, clean flat faceted polygons) and minimalist 2-color geometric icons. No realistic drawings of real objects.

Composition & Spacing:
Vertical 60x160cm layout. Restrained, minimalist artwork isolated strictly within the middle height zone of a plain mint-white background (#f4f8f7). Ample empty negative space. Top 25% and bottom 10% completely blank.

Color & Shading:
Flat color-blocked steps using deep forest teal (#105040), marine jade (#14856b), and sage gray (#789890). Strictly zero smooth gradients, zero continuous shading, zero drop shadows.

Avoid:
Text, letters, words, detailed objects, realistic drawings, busy decoration, smooth gradients, soft shading, heavy black outlines.
```
