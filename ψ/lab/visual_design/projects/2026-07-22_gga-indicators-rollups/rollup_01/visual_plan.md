# Panel 01 Visual Plan: Understanding the GGA and UAE Framework

## 1. Composition & Reading Direction
* **Format & Size**: 60 × 160 cm vertical roll-up banner.
* **Dominant Reading Path**: Linear vertical top-to-bottom path, guiding the viewer from the high-level mandate to the historical journey, concluding with the structural targets.
* **Layout Grid (3 Horizontal Zones)**:
  ```mermaid
  graph TD
      Zone1[Zone 1: Header - Title, Mandate & Paris Art 7.1] --> Zone2[Zone 2: Timeline - Journey from Paris to Belém]
      Zone2 --> Zone3[Zone 3: Framework Structure - Thematic vs. Dimensional targets]
  ```

---

## 2. Visual Elements & Content Mapping

### Zone 1: Header (Top 25% height)
* **Visual Role**: Establish the finding and define the global mandate.
* **Elements**:
  - Main Title: Bold, bilingual hierarchy. Large English title with medium Thai subtitle in Sovereign Dark (`#0b2e25`).
  - High-impact quote/badge: Article 7.1 text ("enhance adaptive capacity, strengthen resilience, reduce vulnerability") framed in a distinct, semi-transparent DCCE Forest Teal (`#105040`) block to serve as the "Guiding Star" visual anchor.

### Zone 2: The Evolutionary Timeline (Middle 40% height)
* **Visual Role**: Explain the temporal journey and operationalization stages.
* **Elements**:
  - Vertical timeline chain: A central dotted line in Sage Gray (`#789890`) with 4 circular milestones nodes in Marine Jade (`#14856b`).
  - Nodes:
    1. **2015 Paris Agreement**: Core mandate defined.
    2. **2021–2023 Glasgow-Sharm el-Sheikh**: Setting the methodology guidelines.
    3. **2023 UAE Framework (COP28)**: Launch of the structured framework targets.
    4. **2025 Belém Indicators (COP30)**: Completion of specific indicator metrics.
  - Styling: Alternating left and right content blocks with clean bilingual summaries next to each milestone node.

### Zone 3: Framework Structure Grid (Bottom 35% height)
* **Visual Role**: Show the dual-structure of adaptation targets.
* **Elements**:
  - Split two-column grid layout:
    - **Left Column**: Thematic Outcomes (Sectors). Visualized as an overlapping pile of 7 horizontal blocks representing Water, Food, Health, Ecosystems, Settlements, Livelihoods, and Heritage.
    - **Right Column**: Dimensional Processes (Policy Stages). Visualized as a 4-step circular feedback loop (Assessment → Planning → Implementation → MEL).
  - Styling: Cards are filled with soft backgrounds and outlined with Sage Gray (`#789890`).

---

## 3. Colors & Typography (Design System Tokens)
* **Palette (DCCE calm & sovereign)**:
  - *Core color (DCCE Forest Teal)*: `#105040` (primary branding sections).
  - *Sovereign Dark (Deep Jade Black)*: `#0b2e25` (text titles and headers).
  - *Background (Mint White Canvas)*: `#f4f8f7` (overall banner canvas).
  - *Primary Accent (Marine Jade)*: `#14856b` (active nodes and badges).
  - *Supporting Neutral (Sage Gray)*: `#789890` (borders, connectors, timeline lines).
* **Typography**:
  - Headers: Sans-serif bold (e.g., Outfit or Inter).
  - Body Text: Clean geometric sans-serif (e.g., Roboto or Inter) for optimal legibility at 1–2.5 m standing distance.

---

## 4. Accessibility, Risk & Verification
* **Readability Gate**:
  - Text sizes: Titles at least 72pt, subtitles 36pt, body text 18–24pt.
  - Contrast ratio: Minimum 4.5:1 between text and background blocks.
* **Scientific & Data Risk**: Ensure the timeline dates and COP session numbers are accurate.
* **Editable Layout Areas**: Keep all timeline summaries, quote blocks, and column descriptions fully editable outside raw image exports.
