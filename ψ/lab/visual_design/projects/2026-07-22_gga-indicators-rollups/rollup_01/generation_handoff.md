# Panel 01 Visual Generation Handoff Guidelines (Abstract-Geometric Refinement)

## 1. Technical Output Requirements
* **Infographic Type**: Vertical roll-up banner
* **Output Dimensions**: 60 cm wide × 160 cm high
* **Required Resolution**: Minimum 150 DPI (3543 × 9449 px) for physical banner printing
* **Target Format**: High-resolution PNG (lossless) or layered PSD/TIFF with text fields left fully editable.

---

## 2. Layout & Canvas Spacing Rules

| Height Coordinates | Zone Name | Description & Visual Content |
|---|---|---|
| **0.0 – 30.0 cm** | **Header Zone** | **Negative Space**: Plain canvas background (`#f4f8f7`) for banner clamps (0–10 cm) and main titles (10–30 cm). Must remain completely free of artwork. |
| **30.0 – 95.0 cm** | **Timeline Art Zone** | **Primary Illustration**: Abstract geometric vertical pathway line with 4 minimalist circular nodes (`#14856b`), framed by subtle semi-circle arcs (`#789890`). Restrained, non-crowded composition. |
| **95.0 – 150.0 cm** | **Structure Art Zone** | **Secondary Illustration**: A clean 2-column abstract diagram displaying 7 minimalist geometric icons (left) and a flat 4-stage geometric loop (right). |
| **150.0 – 160.0 cm** | **Footer Zone** | **Negative Space**: Plain canvas background (`#f4f8f7`) for credits and banner stand wrapping. |

---

## 3. Style & Color Guidelines
* **Palette (DCCE System)**:
  - Core Brand Color: `#105040` (DCCE Forest Teal)
  - Canvas Background: `#f4f8f7` (Mint White)
  - Active Accents: `#14856b` (Marine Jade)
  - Borders & Lines: `#789890` (Sage Gray)
* **Illustration Rules**:
  - **Abstract Geometry**: Use clean geometric forms (arcs, circles, flat faceted polygons). No detailed or realistic drawings of real objects.
  - **Flat Color Blocking**: Discrete, jumpy color steps with hard edges. Strictly zero smooth gradients, zero continuous shading.
  - **Restrained Density**: Graphics must be isolated focal anchors surrounded by ample negative space; never cover the canvas with messy decoration.

---

## 4. Reusable Image-Generation Prompts

### Midjourney / DALL-E 3 Prompt (Abstract-Geometric Vector)

```text
An abstract geometric flat vector infographic illustration for climate policy.

Artwork & Motifs:
An abstract minimalist vertical pathway line in sage-gray (#789890) with 4 small circular milestone nodes in marine teal (#14856b), framed by elegant geometric semi-circle arcs and clean concentric rings. Below the timeline is a minimalist 2-column diagram with 7 simple 2-color geometric icons and a flat 4-stage geometric circle loop. All graphics are abstract geometry; zero realistic or detailed drawings of objects.

Color & Shading:
Flat color-blocked steps using forest teal (#105040), marine jade (#14856b), and sage gray (#789890). Hard-edged color transitions, strictly zero smooth gradients, zero soft shading, zero drop shadows.

Composition & Spacing:
Vertical 60x160cm layout. Restrained minimalist artwork isolated strictly within the middle height zone on a clean mint-white background (#f4f8f7). Ample empty negative space. The top 30cm and bottom 10cm are completely plain and empty canvas for text.

Avoid:
Text, letters, words, photorealism, detailed objects, realistic drawings, busy messy decoration, smooth continuous gradients, soft shading, heavy black outlines.
```

---

## 5. Handoff Checklist for Layout Artist
- [ ] Confirm that no text labels or dates are baked into the generated artwork.
- [ ] Verify that graphics remain strictly abstract-geometric without realistic drawings.
- [ ] Ensure the canvas background `#f4f8f7` provides high contrast and ample breathing space.
