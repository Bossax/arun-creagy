# Panel 01 Layout Text (English Copy)

## ZONE 1: HEADER & MANDATE

### Main Title
Operationalizing the Global Goal on Adaptation (GGA)

### Subtitle
From the Paris Agreement Mandate to the UAE Framework & Belém Indicators

### Core Mandate (Article 7.1)
"Parties hereby establish the global goal on adaptation of enhancing adaptive capacity, strengthening resilience and reducing vulnerability to climate change, with a view to contributing to sustainable development and ensuring an adequate adaptation response in the context of the temperature goal."
*— Article 7.1 of the Paris Agreement*

## ZONE 2: THE EVOLUTIONARY JOURNEY

### Milestone 1 (2015)
* **Header**: 2015 — The Paris Mandate
* **Detail**: Article 7.1 of the Paris Agreement formally establishes the Global Goal on Adaptation (GGA) as the global guiding star to coordinate adaptation efforts.

### Milestone 2 (2021–2023)
* **Header**: 2021–2023 — Operationalization
* **Detail**: Parties launch the two-year Glasgow-Sharm el-Sheikh work programme on the Global Goal on Adaptation to define monitoring methodologies and targets.

### Milestone 3 (2023)
* **Header**: 2023 — The UAE Framework
* **Detail**: COP28 adopts the UAE Framework for Global Climate Resilience, structuring the GGA into 7 thematic sectors and 4 planning dimensions.

### Milestone 4 (2025)
* **Header**: 2025 — The Belém Indicators
* **Detail**: COP30 (CMA7, Belém) formally adopts the Belém Adaptation Indicators, completing the framework with 59 specific metrics to track collective progress.

## ZONE 3: FRAMEWORK STRUCTURE

### Thematic Targets (Target 9)
* **Header**: 7 Thematic Outcomes
* **Detail**: Direct, sector-specific resilience outcomes designed to protect human and ecological systems:
  - 9(a) Water Supply & Sanitation
  - 9(b) Food & Agriculture
  - 9(c) Health Impacts & Services
  - 9(d) Ecosystems & Biodiversity
  - 9(e) Infrastructure & Human Settlements
  - 9(f) Poverty Eradication & Livelihoods
  - 9(g) Cultural Heritage

### Dimensional Targets (Target 10)
* **Header**: 4 Planning Dimensions
* **Detail**: Iterative, continuous policy stages that countries must execute to build adaptive capacity:
  - 10(a) Impact, Vulnerability & Risk Assessment
  - 10(b) Planning & Strategy Formulation
  - 10(c) Implementation of Adaptation Actions
  - 10(d) Monitoring, Evaluation & Learning (MEL)
