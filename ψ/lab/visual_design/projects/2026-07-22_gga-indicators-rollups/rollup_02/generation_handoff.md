# Panel 02 Visual Generation Handoff Guidelines (Abstract-Geometric Refinement)

## 1. Technical Output Requirements
* **Infographic Type**: Vertical roll-up banner
* **Output Dimensions**: 60 cm wide × 160 cm high
* **Required Resolution**: Minimum 150 DPI (3543 × 9449 px) for physical banner printing
* **Target Format**: High-resolution PNG (lossless) or layered PSD/TIFF with text fields left fully editable.

---

## 2. Layout & Canvas Spacing Rules

| Height Coordinates | Zone Name | Description & Visual Content |
|---|---|---|
| **0.0 – 25.0 cm** | **Header Zone** | **Negative Space**: Plain canvas background (`#f4f8f7`) for banner clamps (0–10 cm) and main titles (10–25 cm). Must remain completely free of artwork. |
| **25.0 – 130.0 cm** | **Grid Art Zone** | **Primary Illustration**: A 2-column minimalist visual grid containing 11 modular summary cards with soft sage-gray outlines (`#789890`). Each card features a simple 2-color abstract geometric icon badge in its top-left corner. |
| **130.0 – 150.0 cm** | **Action Art Zone** | **Secondary Illustration**: A single wide card frame with a high-contrast square placeholder box on the left (for QR code placement) decorated with subtle geometric data lines. |
| **150.0 – 160.0 cm** | **Footer Zone** | **Negative Space**: Plain canvas background (`#f4f8f7`) for credits and banner stand wrapping. |

---

## 3. Style & Color Guidelines
* **Palette (DCCE System)**:
  - Core Brand Color: `#105040` (DCCE Forest Teal)
  - Canvas Background: `#f4f8f7` (Mint White)
  - Active Accents: `#14856b` (Marine Jade)
  - Borders & Lines: `#789890` (Sage Gray)
* **Illustration Rules**:
  - **Abstract Geometry**: Use clean geometric shapes (circles, square frames, flat faceted polygons). No detailed or realistic drawings of objects.
  - **Flat Color Blocking**: Discrete, jumpy color steps with hard edges. Strictly zero smooth gradients, zero continuous shading.
  - **Restrained Density**: Graphics must be isolated focal anchors surrounded by ample negative space; never cover the canvas with messy decoration.

---

## 4. Reusable Image-Generation Prompts

### Midjourney / DALL-E 3 Prompt (Abstract-Geometric Vector)

```text
An abstract geometric flat vector infographic layout for climate policy indicators.

Artwork & Motifs:
A minimalist dual-column grid on a clean mint-white background (#f4f8f7). The left column contains 7 modular horizontal cards, and the right column contains 4 modular horizontal cards. Each card is outlined in thin sage-gray (#789890) with soft rounded corners, and features a simple 2-color abstract geometric icon badge in its top-left corner. At the bottom, a single wide card spans both columns with a square placeholder box on the left. All graphics are abstract geometry; zero realistic or detailed drawings of objects.

Color & Shading:
Flat color-blocked steps using forest teal (#105040), marine jade (#14856b), and sage gray (#789890). Hard-edged color transitions, strictly zero smooth gradients, zero soft shading, zero drop shadows.

Composition & Spacing:
Vertical 60x160cm layout. Restrained minimalist grid isolated strictly within the middle height zone on a clean mint-white background (#f4f8f7). Ample empty negative space. The top 25cm and bottom 10cm are completely plain and empty canvas for text.

Avoid:
Text, letters, words, photorealism, detailed objects, realistic drawings, busy messy decoration, smooth continuous gradients, soft shading, heavy black outlines.
```

---

## 5. Handoff Checklist for Layout Artist
- [ ] Confirm that no text labels or numbers are baked into the generated graphic background.
- [ ] Ensure that the QR code placeholder box remains blank in the background export.
- [ ] Verify that graphics remain strictly abstract-geometric without realistic drawings or smooth gradients.
