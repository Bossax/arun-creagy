# Panel 02 Content Plan: The Belém Adaptation Indicators Catalog (Hybrid Layout)

## 1. Core Metadata & Hierarchy
* **Primary Message**: The Belém Adaptation Indicators define the specific themes and metrics required to track climate resilience across human systems (Thematic outcomes) and policy stages (Dimensional progress).
* **Audience Takeaway**: A clear, visual roadmap explaining what each theme and policy stage measures, paired with a digital link (QR code) to access the complete, detailed 59-indicator bilingual database.
* **Reading Sequence**:
  1. **Header Zone**: Title and explanation of the Belém Indicators (COP30, Belém, Nov 2025).
  2. **Thematic Targets Grouping (Target 9)**: Visual summary cards for the 7 sectors, highlighting what they measure and giving key indicator examples.
  3. **Dimensional Targets Grouping (Target 10)**: Visual summary cards for the 4 policy cycle stages.
  4. **Interactive Action Zone**: Styled QR Code block labeled: *"Scan to access the full 59-indicator bilingual (TH/EN) reference catalog"* (linking to [evidence_map.md](file:///C:/Users/sitth/OracleWorkspace/Arun_Creagy/ψ/lab/visual_design/projects/2026-07-22_gga-indicators-rollups/00_project/evidence_map.md)).

## 2. Reader-Value Gate: Insight Card

1. **Reader question**: What do the Belém Adaptation Indicators measure, and how are they structured?
2. **Source-specific finding**: Finalized at COP30, the Belém indicators map climate resilience across 7 thematic sectors (Target 9, measuring outcomes in water, food, health, ecosystems, settlements, livelihoods, and heritage) and 4 policy stages (Target 10, measuring progress in risk assessment, planning, implementation, and MEL).
3. **Mechanism**: Summarizing the core measurement focus of each target block reduces visual clutter, making a massive framework of 59 complex metrics easily understandable at a glance.
4. **Consequence**: Policy planners gain a comprehensive overview of the global reporting expectations while retaining full, on-demand access to raw metrics via a digital QR crosswalk.
5. **Visual proof**: A dual-logic layout:
   - A grid of 11 thematic/dimensional cards showing summaries and sample metrics.
   - A call-to-action QR code card linking to the full spreadsheet index.
6. **Evidence anchor**: [GGA_indicator_TH.csv](file:///C:/Users/sitth/OracleWorkspace/Arun_Creagy/ψ/incubate/GGGI/NAP_AP/inbox_source/GGA_indicator_TH.csv).

## 3. Evidence to Visual Mapping (Micro-Scoping)

The panel summarizes what each target group measures, showcasing representative indicators where space permits:

### Stream A: Thematic Targets (Target 9) — What They Measure
* **9(a) Water Supply & Sanitation**: Measures water stress, water-use efficiency, resilience of critical water/sanitation infrastructure, and access to safe drinking water.
  * *Sample Indicator*: `GGA-9a(a)` Water stress levels / ระดับความตึงเครียดด้านน้ำ
* **9(b) Food & Agriculture**: Measures adoption of adaptation technologies, yield stability, management of degraded areas, and equitable food security.
  * *Sample Indicator*: `GGA-9b(a)` Area under adaptation management / สัดส่วนพื้นที่เกษตรกรรมที่ปรับตัว
* **9(c) Health Impacts & Services**: Measures climate-related mortality and morbidity, health facility resilience, continuous health services, and mental health access.
  * *Sample Indicator*: `GGA-9c(f)` Climate-resilient health facilities / ร้อยละสถานพยาบาลที่ยืดหยุ่น
* **9(d) Ecosystems & Biodiversity**: Measures ecosystem resilience, species threat status, protected areas management, and EbA/NbS implementation.
  * *Sample Indicator*: `GGA-9d(a)` Resilient ecosystem services / สัดส่วนระบบนิเวศที่ให้บริการต่อประชากร
* **9(e) Infrastructure & Settlements**: Measures climate-proofing of settlement upgrading programs and planned relocations of vulnerable assets.
  * *Sample Indicator*: `GGA-9e(b)` Vulnerable asset relocation / สัดส่วนการย้ายถิ่นฐานของโครงสร้างพื้นฐานที่เปราะบาง
* **9(f) Poverty & Livelihoods**: Measures poverty mitigation levels and vulnerable populations' access to climate-responsive social protection.
  * *Sample Indicator*: `GGA-9f(b)` Social protection access / สัดส่วนประชากรที่เข้าถึงการคุ้มครองทางสังคม
* **9(g) Cultural Heritage**: Measures adaptation of tangible and intangible heritage sites, digitization of assets, and indigenous engagement.
  * *Sample Indicator*: `GGA-9g(a)` Heritage sites adaptation / ร้อยละของแหล่งมรดกที่ดำเนินมาตรการปรับตัว

### Stream B: Dimensional Targets (Target 10) — What They Measure
* **10(a) Risk & Vulnerability Assessment**: Measures early warning system (EWS) establishment, coverage per 100k, and use of warming-scenario risk assessments.
  * *Sample Indicator*: `GGA-10a(a)` Multi-hazard EWS setup / ระดับการจัดตั้งระบบเตือนภัยล่วงหน้า
* **10(b) Adaptation Planning**: Measures adoption of country-driven, gender-responsive NAPs and integration of traditional indigenous knowledge.
  * *Sample Indicator*: `GGA-10b(a)` National adaptation planning status / สถานะแผนการปรับตัวระดับชาติ (NAPs)
* **10(c) Implementation Progress**: Measures plan execution levels, avoided economic loss (GDP savings), and tracking of adaptation finance, technology transfer, and capacity support.
  * *Sample Indicator*: `GGA-10c(d)` Adaptation finance tracked / ปริมาณเงินทุนเพื่อการปรับตัว
* **10(d) M&E and Learning (MEL)**: Measures design and operationalization of national M&E systems, publication of findings, and policy feedback loops.
  * *Sample Indicator*: `GGA-10d(b)` MEL operationalization / ระดับการทำให้ระบบติดตามประเมินผลดำเนินงานได้จริง
