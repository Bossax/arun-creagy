## ZONE 1: HEADER & INTRO

### Main Title
The Belém Adaptation Indicators Catalog

### Subtitle
Bilingual Reference Index of the UAE Framework Targets

### Introduction
Formally adopted at COP30 (CMA7, Belém), the framework features 59 indicators designed to measure climate resilience across human systems (Thematic Targets) and policy cycles (Dimensional Targets).

## ZONE 2 & 3: DUAL-COLUMN REFERENCE CARDS

### Column 1: Thematic Outcome Cards (Target 9)

#### Card 9(a): Water Supply & Sanitation
* **Summary**: Measures water stress levels, water-use efficiency, the resilience of critical water supply infrastructure, and safe, affordable drinking water and sanitation services.
* **Sample Indicator**: `GGA-9a(a)` Level of water stress, accounting for hazard intensity and frequency.

#### Card 9(b): Food & Agriculture
* **Summary**: Measures the adoption of adaptation technologies and practices in farming, crop yields, management of degraded soil areas, and equitable food security access.
* **Sample Indicator**: `GGA-9b(a)` Proportion of area under agricultural management utilizing adaptation practices.

#### Card 9(c): Health Impacts & Services
* **Summary**: Measures climate-related mortality and morbidity rates, the resilience of health facilities, continuous essential health services, and mental health support access.
* **Sample Indicator**: `GGA-9c(f)` Percentage of health facilities that are resilient to climate-related hazards.

#### Card 9(d): Ecosystems & Biodiversity
* **Summary**: Measures ecosystem resilience, species threat statuses, the coverage of adaptation actions in habitats, and the implementation of Nature-based Solutions (NbS).
* **Sample Indicator**: `GGA-9d(a)` Proportion of climate-resilient ecosystems providing services to dependent populations.

#### Card 9(e): Infrastructure & Human Settlements
* **Summary**: Measures the integration of adaptation measures in settlement upgrading programs and the planned relocation of highly vulnerable infrastructure assets.
* **Sample Indicator**: `GGA-9e(b)` Proportion of vulnerable infrastructure and human settlements relocated to safer areas.

#### Card 9(f): Poverty Eradication & Livelihoods
* **Summary**: Measures absolute poverty levels and vulnerable groups' access to climate-responsive, adaptive social protection and risk management services.
* **Sample Indicator**: `GGA-9f(b)` Proportion of the population in climate-vulnerable areas with access to social protection.

#### Card 9(g): Cultural Heritage
* **Summary**: Measures the resilience of tangible and intangible heritage sites, digital archiving of cultural assets, emergency preparedness plans, and indigenous participation.
* **Sample Indicator**: `GGA-9g(a)` Percentage of at-risk cultural and natural heritage sites with adaptation measures.

### Column 2: Dimensional Process Cards (Target 10)

#### Card 10(a): Impact, Vulnerability & Risk Assessment
* **Summary**: Measures the establishment of multi-hazard early warning systems (EWS), forecasting stations, EWS coverage per 100k, and the use of warming-scenario vulnerability maps.
* **Sample Indicator**: `GGA-10a(a)` Level of establishment of multi-hazard early warning systems.

#### Card 10(b): Adaptation Planning
* **Summary**: Measures the formulation of country-driven, gender-responsive NAPs, policy strategies, and the integration of traditional indigenous knowledge in planning.
* **Sample Indicator**: `GGA-10b(a)` Status of having national adaptation plans and planning processes in place.

#### Card 10(c): Implementation of Adaptation Actions
* **Summary**: Measures plan execution progress, avoided loss GDP savings, and tracking of international adaptation finance, technology transfer, and capacity-building.
* **Sample Indicator**: `GGA-10c(d)` Amount of finance for climate adaptation reported under national channels.

#### Card 10(d): Monitoring, Evaluation & Learning (MEL)
* **Summary**: Measures the design and operationalization of national M&E frameworks, the periodic publication of results, and the integration of findings back into policy.
* **Sample Indicator**: `GGA-10d(b)` Level of operationalization of national M&E frameworks for adaptation efforts.
