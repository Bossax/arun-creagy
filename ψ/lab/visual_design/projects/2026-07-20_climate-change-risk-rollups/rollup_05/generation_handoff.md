# Roll-up 5 — Generation and layout handoff

## Objective

Create a **text-free composition prototype** for the final 60 × 160 cm vertical roll-up. The panel shows BTR2’s four-part NAP process as an iterative adaptation-management cycle: climate change risk, vulnerability and impact assessment; development of policies, strategies and plans; implementation of adaptation actions; and monitoring, evaluation and learning. A feedback arc returns learning to the next assessment and decision.

This is a framework for managing risk. It does not claim that Thailand has completed the cycle, that all adaptation actions are effective, or that a single measure works everywhere.

## Main instruction

Design a tall, minimal scientific-editorial adaptation infographic. Reserve a calm header and a compact premise strip. The centre must be a large radial four-stage cycle around a quiet central hub. It is the dominant visual.

Arrange four equal circular bubbles at top, right, bottom and left. Connect them clockwise with thick near-black directional arcs. Reserve a dark-green feedback arc from the left-stage bubble back to the top-stage bubble. Keep every bubble interior blank for controlled Thai text. Reserve the central hub for a short controlled-layout cycle label.

The four blank BTR2 stages are: risk, vulnerability and impact assessment; policies, strategies and plans; implementation of adaptation actions; and monitoring, evaluation and learning. Do not add a fifth stage. The feedback arc is the update of the next assessment and decision.

Around the four stages, add one small low-detail flat editorial scene fragment per stage: climate-data/grid and rain-gauge cue; simple landscape/planning-table cue; linked water/crop/service/coast cue; observation/measurement/feedback cue. Use only two or three layered shapes and one recognisable silhouette per fragment. Follow the Arctic reference’s simplification: broad irregular colour fields, sparse silhouette, occasional thin dotted connector, no texture, hatching, realistic perspective or decorative icon cluster. These support the system context and must stay outside the bubbles, arcs and text zones.

Around each cycle bubble, reserve two small external annotation positions for `what BTR2 reports is already in place` and `the next decision question`. Below the cycle, reserve a prominent white or pale-grey four-question response-risk test. It needs four equal blank capsule spaces for: what risk determinant changes; who actually benefits; what trade-off or unequal burden appears; and what outcome evidence proves risk reduction. Add one compact BTR2 note space explaining that an M&E framework and database exist while outcome indicators, avoided-loss evidence and policy-feedback links are still being strengthened. End with a narrow dark-green feedback bridge to the next decision, then a clean source and identity footer. All final text, source labels, evidence status markers and logos must be added in layout software.

## Production parameters

- **Canvas:** 60 × 160 cm portrait; protect the 4 cm side margins and 8–152 cm vertical working zone.
- **Cycle bubbles:** four equal 9–10 cm circles at final print size; central hub remains smaller and visually quiet.
- **Primary process arcs:** near-black, 1.5–2.0 mm at final print size, with clear arrowheads.
- **Feedback arc:** dark green `#2E6F5E`, same weight as the primary process arcs.
- **Bubble outlines:** 1.0–1.25 mm; secondary annotation lines 0.75–1.0 mm in grey.
- **Text:** no generated text. Use `layout_text_th.md` for all controlled display copy.

## First-prototype review gate

- [ ] The four BTR2 stages and central hub dominate the composition.
- [ ] The feedback arc clearly returns MEL to the next assessment; it is not a fifth stage.
- [ ] Thick near-black process arcs are visibly stronger than thin grey annotation lines.
- [ ] Low-detail scene fragments stay outside bubbles and arcs, and never become a project catalogue.
- [ ] The four-question response-risk test is the dominant lower module; it informs a decision and does not resemble a performance dashboard.
- [ ] The BTR2 evidence note explains why outcome proof and feedback matter.
- [ ] Blank zones remain usable for the Thai stage labels, BTR2 evidence cues, source notes and DCCE identity.
- [ ] No generated text, false quantitative claim, map, agency logo or completed-cycle claim appears in the artwork.
