## Purpose

Trace every adaptation-cycle element to a source-supported statement. The panel communicates a risk-management framework; it does not evaluate the performance of a specific project or agency.

## Content controls

- Use the four stages as a managed cycle, not a linear promise that assessment automatically produces success.
- Mark the risk gate as a required test before the next decision; do not depict it as an alarm or a failed-action claim.
- Keep the six priority sectors as small contextual cues, not a dashboard or a claim that every sector uses the same measure.
- Use BTR2’s outcome-evidence limitations to explain why the response-risk test matters; do not portray them as institutional failure.
- Do not place an adaptation-effectiveness number or project result without a specific evaluated source.
