# Negative Prompt — Roll-up 1

Avoid:

- final text rendered inside generated image;
- fake or inaccurate IPCC diagram labels;
- distorted hazard/exposure/vulnerability/response relationship;
- risk shown as only a storm, flood, drought, or heatwave;
- fixed equation such as hazard + exposure + vulnerability = risk as the dominant structure;
- implication that all adaptation is maladaptation;
- implication that key-risk examples are Thailand-specific;
- Thailand map or Thailand-specific spatial risk evidence;
- generic green sustainability campaign style;
- glossy 3D globe;
- photorealistic climate disaster scene;
- cartoon climate apocalypse;
- excessive leaves, recycling icons, or decorative eco motifs;
- heavy black outlines;
- neon colors;
- saturated rainbow palette;
- crowded dashboard layout;
- too many arrows crossing the diagram;
- callouts covering the central risk node;
- low-poly stock-image composition copied directly;
- decorative icons inside the scientific diagram;
- DCCE green replacing IPCC category colors.
