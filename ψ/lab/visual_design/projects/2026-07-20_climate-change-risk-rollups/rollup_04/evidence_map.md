## Evidence-to-visual matrix

| Visual element | Claims | Display wording boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Method module | E01 | `Hazard → direct impact → cascading impact`, then `exposure + sensitivity + adaptive capacity → resulting risk and entry points`. |
| Urban cascade lane | E02, E04 | `Heavy rainfall/cyclone → flooded roads and power outage → transport and economic activity disrupted`, with density, routes, water management and backup-grid capacity as the mechanism. |
| Agriculture cascade lane | E03–E05 | `Drought → reduced irrigation water and crop damage → farmer income loss`, with irrigation access, crop sensitivity, savings and alternative income as the mechanism. |

## Content controls

- Use arrows only to show the selected illustrative sequence; do not imply a universal or deterministic outcome.
- Keep `direct impact`, `cascading consequence`, `exposure`, `sensitivity`, `adaptive-capacity gap` and `resulting risk` visibly distinct.
- Do not use a Thai place name, regional marker, casualty figure or economic estimate in the three lanes.
- Keep the chain’s end state at a system consequence and identify the analytical entry point without detailing actions. Roll-up 5 will show adaptation options and decision pathways.
