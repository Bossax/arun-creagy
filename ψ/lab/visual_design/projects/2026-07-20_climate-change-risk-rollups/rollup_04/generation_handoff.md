# Roll-up 4 — Generation and layout handoff

## Objective

Create a **text-free composition prototype** for a 60 × 160 cm vertical roll-up. The panel explains how a climate hazard creates a direct impact that then cascades through connected systems, and how exposure, sensitivity and adaptive-capacity gaps shape the risk. It is a teaching diagram, not a national forecast, a comprehensive causal-loop model or a Thailand risk ranking.

## Main instruction

Design a tall, minimal scientific-editorial climate infographic. Reserve a calm header and a compact method strip. The method strip must leave controlled-layout space for: `hazard → direct impact → cascading impact`, then `exposure + sensitivity + adaptive capacity`, then `resulting risk + entry points`. Make the centre a large, open scientific field containing three horizontal, left-to-right impact-chain lanes. Each lane contains exactly three equal circular bubbles: climate hazard, direct impact and cascading impact. Use soft grey or white bubbles, tiny navy/cyan/magenta category dots, near-black primary arrows and abundant white space.

The three unlabelled lanes must leave enough interior room for controlled Thai layout text. Their intended meanings are: high accumulated rainfall/tropical cyclone → flooded roads and power outage → transport and economic activity disrupted; drought → reduced irrigation water and crop damage → farmer income loss, livelihood and food pressure; storms/sea-level variability/warmer sea → tourism assets damaged and coral bleaching pressure → operations and visitor demand decline, affecting local income. Reserve three small external annotation positions per lane for exposure, sensitivity/adaptive-capacity gap and resulting risk. Add one quiet low-detail flat editorial scene fragment per lane, positioned at its outer edge or as a shallow background band: rain/road/service; dry field/water/crop; and coast/reef or mangrove/tourism service. These are atmosphere and system cues only, never a fourth node or a place-specific claim.

Below the diagram, reserve a compact amplifier rail with three equal blank capsules and a small dark-green connected-response bridge. All words, labels, sources, numbers, logos and status markers must be added later in layout software.

## Image-generation prompt

```text
Create a text-free 60 × 160 cm vertical scientific-editorial infographic composition about climate-risk impact chains.

Use a very pale grey and white background with generous negative space. At the top, reserve a calm title/subtitle area. Below it, add a narrow context strip made from three small soft-grey circular bubbles and two short near-black arrows, with clear blank label space.

The centre is the dominant visual: a wide pale scientific field with three horizontally aligned impact-chain lanes. Every lane has exactly three equal, large circular bubbles arranged left to right, each with blank interior space for later Thai text. Leave generous separation between lanes. Make the bubbles white or soft grey, use a refined neutral outline and a very subtle soft shadow. Put only a small category dot near the top of each bubble: navy for climate hazard, cyan for direct impact, magenta for cascading impact. Connect each pair with a thick, calm, near-black curved arrow and a crisp arrowhead. The arrows are clearly more prominent than the thin grey annotation lines elsewhere. Do not add cross-links between the lanes. Reserve three small external annotation positions beside each lane, connected by thin grey lines, for exposure, sensitivity/adaptive-capacity gap and resulting risk.

Around the main field, reserve a few small white circular endpoint callout positions with thin light-grey connectors. Below it, create a compact pale amplifier rail with three equal blank capsule spaces and small amber dot positions. Add a short, restrained dark-green linked-node bridge below the rail, then a clean footer strip for sources and identity.

At the outer edge or beneath each of the three lanes, add a quiet low-detail flat editorial scene fragment: pale rain band with simple road and service/transport silhouette; muted dry-field and water-channel band with crop silhouette; pale cyan waterline with reef, mangrove or coast edge and a small tourism-service cue. Use only two or three simplified layered shapes per fragment. Keep every fragment low-contrast, smaller than a bubble, and clear of the bubble interiors, arrows and text zones.

Style: minimal IPCC-like scientific diagram, institutional climate communication, open white canvas, soft-grey bubbles, compact category dots, carefully controlled curved arrows, precise vector geometry and calm print-ready hierarchy. The composition should be unmistakably related to a clean scientific roll-up, not a business-process diagram or a dashboard.

Do not render text, letters, numbers, logos, flags, citations, watermarks, maps, charts, people, disaster scenes, photorealism, 3D, low-poly illustration, thick coloured bubbles, rectangular process cards, a dense causal-loop network, polar animals, polar landscapes, random icons, or more than the three primary lanes.
```

## Production parameters

- **Canvas:** 60 × 160 cm portrait; protect the 4 cm side margins and 8–152 cm vertical working zone.
- **Primary bubble diameter:** 10–11 cm at final print size; three equal bubbles per lane.
- **Primary cascade arrows:** near-black, 1.5–2.0 mm at final print size, with clean arrowheads.
- **Bubble outlines:** 1.0–1.25 mm; secondary annotation lines 0.75–1.0 mm in grey.
- **Cross-links:** none in the first prototype. Add one only after review shows that it improves, rather than reduces, clarity.
- **Text:** no generated text. Use `layout_text_th.md` for all controlled display copy.

## First-prototype review gate

- [ ] The three horizontal chains, not the callouts or lower rail, are the first thing a viewer reads.
- [ ] Every primary node is a circular bubble with usable interior text space.
- [ ] Each lane also has three clear external annotation positions for exposure, sensitivity/adaptive-capacity gap and resulting risk.
- [ ] Thick near-black arrows clearly dominate thin grey annotation connectors.
- [ ] Category colour is limited to compact dots and label accents, not filled process cards.
- [ ] Each lane has at most one low-detail flat scene fragment that supports its system context without competing with the chain.
- [ ] The diagram retains generous white space and does not become a dense causal-loop network.
- [ ] No text, false labels, map, forecast, local ranking or unsupported quantitative claim appears in the artwork.
- [ ] The lower amplifier rail and response bridge remain a secondary transition to Roll-up 5.
