# Project Brief: DCCE Climate Adaptation Platform Budget Defense Widescreen Deck

## 1. Metadata
* **Project Name**: National Climate Adaptation Platform Budget Defense Widescreen Deck
* **Date**: 2026-07-31
* **Author**: Antigravity Visual Design System
* **Output Format**: 16:9 Widescreen Slide Deck (4 Slides)
* **Language**: Thai-first (English for established technical terms)

---

## 2. Audience & Context
* **Target Audience**: DCCE Director and Chief Executive-level budget scrutinizers.
* **Context**: A 15-minute high-stakes budget defense session. The deck must project immediate technical credibility, national urgency, and strict alignment with national digital government standards to secure the 25M THB investment envelope.

---

## 3. Communication Goals & Key Questions
The deck must address the following four core questions:
1. **Slide 1**: What are the current structural data gaps/fragmentation issues, and why is this platform a national necessity to protect Thailand's economy and citizens?
2. **Slide 2**: How does the platform translate surveyed stakeholder needs into a concrete frontend (user journeys/mockups) and backend (PostGIS/CDM engine) design?
3. **Slide 3**: How does the backend architecture design support data governance (DGA-aligned) and strengthen the national climate data ecosystem for BTR reporting?
4. **Slide 4**: What are the concrete, build-ready modules to be delivered in FY2027 based on TOR70 under the 25M THB budget?

---

## 4. Visual Vibe & Reference Roles
* **Theme**: Minimal Corporate.
* **Visual Reference Roles**:
  * *Composition*: Two-column layouts, clear vertical narrative flows, clean metadata grids, and explicit visual schema partitions.
  * *Illustration Style*: Minimal flat vectors, technical diagrams, clean outline icons.
  * *Information Hierarchy*: Widescreen headings, high-density stats callouts, and structured labels.

---

## 5. Design Constraints & Exclusions
* **Financial Constraints**: Strictly exclude all cost breakdowns, itemized hardware/software pricing, and raw ROI formulas. Reference only the overall 25M THB envelope on Slide 1 & Slide 4.
* **Illustration Exclusions**: No flat hand-drawn cartoons, doodles, generic stock vector illustrations, or decorative elements that do not bear analytical meaning. All visuals must convey data or architecture.
