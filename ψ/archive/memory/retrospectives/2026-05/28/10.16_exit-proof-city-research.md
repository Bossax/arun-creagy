## Lessons Learned
1. **Talent-First Realism**: In the knowledge economy, firms follow talent, and talent follows QoL. Traditional "Science Parks" that ignore urban form, mobility, and housing are economically obsolete.
2. **The Commute Penalty is Quantifiable**: 45+ minute commutes are a direct predictor of brain drain (40% flight risk). Walkability isn't an amenity; it's a retention strategy.
3. **The Displacement Paradox**: High-QoL innovation districts naturally generate price premiums (75% for walkability) that threaten to exclude the very talent (early founders/researchers) they were built for. Active housing intervention is required.
