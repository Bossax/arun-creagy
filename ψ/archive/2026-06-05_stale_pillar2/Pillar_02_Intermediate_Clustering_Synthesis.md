## 1. Objective
This memo details the methodology used to consolidate the 18 identified decision requirements from the `Intermediate_Extraction_Matrix.md` into the **7 National Climate Services**. This synthesis ensures the NCAIF is structured as a technical service framework following the **Copernicus Climate Data Store (CDS)** architecture.

---

## 2. Consolidation of National Services

### Service 1: Authoritative Metadata & Quality Certification Service
*   **Logic**: Aggregates the requirement for "Official Baselines" across the financial, insurance, and policy sectors. Corresponds to the **Metadata Discovery & Quality Layer** of the CDS.
*   **Purpose**: To provide a technically verified and legally recognized metadata schema for Hazard, Exposure, and Vulnerability assessments.
*   **Primary Application**: Regulatory compliance and financial stress testing.

### Service 2: Integrated Socio-Economic & Hazard Vulnerability Analysis
*   **Logic**: Responds to the requirement for granular, sub-district level analytics combining physical climate risk with socio-economic vulnerability metrics.
*   **Purpose**: To enable data-driven resource allocation for social welfare and municipal planning.
*   **Primary Application**: Local-scale adaptation and social protection programs.

### Service 3: Adaptation Investment Justification & Budgetary Compliance
*   **Logic**: Addresses the recurring institutional bottleneck where adaptation projects fail to secure funding due to lack of technical ROI evidence.
*   **Purpose**: To provide the techno-economic analytics required to justify proactive climate resilience spending to national budgetary authorities.
*   **Primary Application**: Capital expenditure planning and budgetary audits.

### Service 4: Standardized National Impact, Loss, and Damage Monitoring
*   **Logic**: Consolidates fragmented sectoral impact data into a unified national profile.
*   **Purpose**: To standardize the quantification of climate-related economic losses according to international (e.g., UNDRR) and national standards.
*   **Primary Application**: National status reporting and post-disaster economic forecasting.

### Service 5: Engineering Design Parameters for Climate Resilience
*   **Logic**: Maps the requirement for "Climate Parameters" into the national infrastructure and construction sectors.
*   **Purpose**: To provide future-oriented climatological variables (e.g., thermal thresholds, future rainfall intensity) for integration into engineering codes.
*   **Primary Application**: Infrastructure design and national construction standards.

### Service 6: Sector-Specific Risk Monitoring & Health Advisories
*   **Logic**: Groups the requirements for sector-specific habitability monitoring and public health risk surveillance.
*   **Purpose**: To provide threshold-based analytics and advisories for sectors sensitive to climate extremes.
*   **Primary Application**: Public health safety and economic hotspot management.

### Service 7: Authoritative Data Provisioning & Institutional Access Services
*   **Logic**: Addresses the requirement for a centralized, managed access point for institutional climate data.
*   **Purpose**: To act as the authoritative clearinghouse, reducing inter-agency data friction and technical liability.
*   **Primary Application**: Cross-agency data integration and research support.

---

## 3. Re-Classification of System Components
Technical requirements identified in the source documentation have been re-classified as **Supporting Infrastructure Products** (Pillar 7/8) to maintain the distinction between "System Functions" and "User Services":
*   **Data Provisioning Mechanisms**: Classified as the "Stewardship Ledger."
*   **Technical Quality Assurance**: Classified as the "Fidelity Verification System."
*   **Interoperability Standards**: Classified as the "Conceptual Data Model (CDM)."

---

## 4. Synthesis Conclusion
The refactoring of the NCAIF into these 7 services ensures that the framework directly addresses the operational, budgetary, and regulatory bottlenecks identified during the stakeholder engagement phase.
