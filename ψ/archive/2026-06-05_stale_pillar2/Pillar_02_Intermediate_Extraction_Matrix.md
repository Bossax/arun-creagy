## 3. Thematic Analysis of Requirements
*   **Budgetary Compliance**: A primary requirement across DLA, OTP, NESDC, and UDDC is the provision of **authoritative evidence** that allows for the justification of proactive adaptation expenditures to national auditors.
*   **Institutional Stewardship**: NXPO and BMA identify the need for a **formal national baseline** to standardize inter-agency data sharing and provide a technical safeguard for predictive planning.
*   **Spatial Granularity**: NSO, MSDHS, and DOH emphasize the requirement for **sub-district resolution**, ensuring that national services are actionable at the local implementation level.
