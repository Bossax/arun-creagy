## 1. Executive Summary: Architectural Consolidation
This memo documents the synthesis of 35+ granular use cases (extracted in Phase 1) into 7 **Canonical Service Platforms**. This consolidation follows the **Single Source of Truth (SSOT)** mandate and the **Intelligence Value Chain** framework. Each platform is grounded in specific institutional "Drama" (real-world decision friction) and enriched with international technical benchmarks.

## 2. Service Platform Synthesis & Technical Enrichment

### S01: National Authoritative Data Catalog (SSOT)
*   **Objective**: To provide the semantic and technical foundation for all other services, ensuring data interoperability across 13+ agencies.
*   **Core Anchors**: [NSO - EA Unit], [DGA - GDX Highway].
*   **Technical Enrichment**: **DCAT-AP 3.0.0 (June 2024)**.
    *   *Implementation*: Adoption of Mandatory Classes (`Catalog`, `Dataset`, `Distribution`) and controlled vocabularies for Themes/Formats to enable federated discovery between DCCE, NSO, and GISTDA.
    *   [Technical Enrichment: https://joinup.ec.europa.eu/collection/semantic-interoperability-community-semic/solution/dcat-application-profile-data-portals-europe/release/300]
*   **The Narrative**: The "Secure Highway" where fragmented agency data (UDDC, GISTDA, DPT) is transformed into legally-binding, machine-readable assets.

### S02: Vulnerability & Social Intelligence (MSDHS/NSO)
*   **Objective**: Predictive humanitarian response by matching social welfare data with live hazard overlays.
*   **Core Anchors**: [MSDHS - Evacuation], [NSO - EA Unit].
*   **The Narrative**: Moving from reactive rescue to predictive triggers for "Bedridden" and "Disabled" groups using 13-digit ID matching against rising water levels at the Enumeration Area (EA) scale.

### S03: Fiscal Planning & Investment ROI (DLA/NESDC)
*   **Objective**: To provide a "Regulatory Shield" for local and national administrators deploying climate budgets.
*   **Core Anchors**: [DLA - Accumulated Funds], [FTI - Green Loan].
*   **Technical Enrichment**: **World Bank "Triple Dividend of Resilience"**.
    *   *Implementation*: Metrics for Avoided Losses (1st Div), Unlocked Economic Potential (2nd Div), and Development Co-benefits (3rd Div). Benefit-Cost Ratio (BCR) targets of 2:1 to 10:1.
    *   [Technical Enrichment: https://www.worldbank.org/en/topic/climatechange/publication/resilience-rating-system]
*   **The Narrative**: Local administrators (DLA) using Accumulated Funds (เงินสะสม) can now justify projects to the State Audit Office (OAG) using certified ROI certificates.

### S04: Macro-Economic Loss & Damage (NESDC/TBA/BoT)
*   **Objective**: Integration of physical risk into national financial stability frameworks and GDP forecasting.
*   **Core Anchors**: [TBA - Stress Test], [NESDC - GDP Adjustment].
*   **Technical Enrichment**: **Sendai Framework Target C (Economic Loss)**.
    *   *Implementation*: Sub-indicators C-2 (Agriculture) to C-6 (Cultural Heritage) using the "Replacement Cost" methodology for direct economic loss calculation.
    *   [Technical Enrichment: https://www.undrr.org/publication/technical-guidance-monitoring-and-reporting-progress-achieving-global-targets-sendai]
*   **The Narrative**: Risk officers at TBA/BoT moving from provincial-level indexes to asset-level financial modeling for ICAAP compliance.

### S05: Infrastructure Engineering & Hazard Analysis (OTP/DPT)
*   **Objective**: Climate-proofing national infrastructure through engineering-grade projections.
*   **Core Anchors**: [DPT - Drainage Design], [OTP - KM-Marker], [UDDC - City Scale].
*   **Technical Enrichment**: **PIANC WG 178 / TG 193 (Resilience for Waterborne Transport)**.
    *   *Implementation*: 4-Stage Adaptation Framework (Context -> Info -> Risk -> Options) and use of "Tipping Points" for asset operational thresholds.
    *   [Technical Enrichment: https://www.pianc.org/publications/envicom/wg178]
*   **The Narrative**: DPT and OTP engineers shifting from 30-year historical rainfall to "Climate-Adjusted IDF" curves to prevent infrastructure failure under "Rain Bomb" scenarios.

### S06: Operational Monitoring & Response (BMA/DDPM)
*   **Objective**: Unified operational awareness for rapid-onset disasters.
*   **Core Anchors**: [BMA - Flood Ops].
*   **The Narrative**: A single "Basin-City" API view that eliminates the need for staff to manually monitor multiple websites (RID, TMD, BMA) during flood events.

### S07: Policy Monitoring & Tracking (DCCE/NXPO)
*   **Objective**: Intelligence-driven policy steering and research gap identification.
*   **Core Anchors**: [NXPO - Gap Analysis].
*   **The Narrative**: Using AI-driven thematic clustering to ensure billions in research funding (NXPO) target the specific "Unknowns" identified in the NCAIF hazard maps.

## 3. Consolidation Logic
1.  **Anchor S01 First**: No specialized service (S02-S07) can function without the EA-level exposure resolution (S01/NSO) and the GDX security protocols (S01/DGA).
2.  **Originator Attribution**: Every intelligence product retains its Originator Tag (e.g., `[DPT - Drainage Design]`) to ensure the functional specs remain traceable to the original agency interview.
3.  **Benchmark Mapping**: International standards (PIANC, World Bank, Sendai) are not used as "filler" but as the specific math/logic for the "Hard Technical Specs" column.
