## 1. Executive Summary: An Agency-Centered Intelligence Framework
The National Climate Change Adaptation Information Framework (NCAIF) is structured as a **National Intelligence Service Portfolio** following the **Copernicus Climate Data Store (CDS)** decoupled architecture. Each service category solves specific operational, budgetary, and regulatory bottlenecks identified by Thai line agencies. This framework ensures that the DCCE delivers actionable information products that translate raw climate data into institutional decisions.

---

## 2. The 7 National Climate Services

### Service 01: Authoritative Metadata & Quality Certification Service
*   **Primary Agencies Served**: Thai Bankers' Association (TBA), ONEP, NESDC, NXPO.
*   **Service Definition**: Provision of technically verified and legally recognized hazard, exposure, and vulnerability metadata schemas, ensuring all information products meet the DCCE "Official Seal" for quality and standard compliance (ISO/STAC).
*   **Tangible Agency Examples**:
    *   **TBA**: Providing standardized **Sectoral Damage Functions** to ensure consistency in ICAAP Stress Testing and BoT compliance across all member banks.
    *   **ONEP**: Issuing **Certified Biodiversity Risk Baselines** to support protected area management and national conservation strategy.

### Service 02: Integrated Socio-Economic & Hazard Vulnerability Analysis
*   **Target Users**: Provincial Administrations, Local Authorities, Social Welfare Officers.
*   **Operational Requirement**: Spatial identification of vulnerable populations to prioritize social assistance and adaptation resources.
*   **Information Product**: **Sub-District Vulnerability Analytics** (Integrated physical hazard and NSO/MSDHS socio-economic datasets).
*   **Outcome**: Evidence-based resource allocation; reduction in climate-induced social displacement.
*   **Supporting Standard**: Multi-Scale Data Analytics Framework (Pillar 6).

### Service 03: Adaptation Investment Justification & Budgetary Compliance
*   **Target Users**: Agency Heads, CFOs, Budget Proponents.
*   **Operational Requirement**: Provision of technical evidence to justify increased capital expenditure for climate-resilient infrastructure to the Bureau of the Budget (BoB).
*   **Information Product**: **Adaptation Investment Justification Reports** (Techno-economic ROI and risk-avoidance analytics).
*   **Outcome**: Formalization of proactive resilience budgets; alignment with national budgetary auditing standards.
*   **Supporting Standard**: Institutional Stewardship Ledger (Pillar 7).

### Service 04: Standardized National Impact, Loss, and Damage Monitoring
*   **Target Users**: Disaster Management Agencies (DDPM), International Reporting Bodies (UNDRR/UNFCCC).
*   **Operational Requirement**: Consolidation of multi-sectoral loss data into a single, authoritative national figure.
*   **Information Product**: **Consolidated National Loss & Damage Report** (Standardized cross-sectoral economic impact metrics).
*   **Outcome**: Unified national status reports; improved accuracy of GDP adjustments post-disaster.
*   **Supporting Standard**: Conceptual Data Model (CDM).

### Service 05: Engineering Design Parameters for Climate Resilience
*   **Target Users**: Infrastructure Engineers (Ministry of Transport, DPT), Construction Standards Boards.
*   **Operational Requirement**: Integration of future-looking climate variables into technical building codes and infrastructure design.
*   **Information Product**: **Climate-Adjusted Technical Parameters** (Projections for rainfall intensity, thermal thresholds, and sea-level rise).
*   **Outcome**: Future-proofed infrastructure assets; reduction in long-term maintenance liabilities.
*   **Supporting Standard**: Technical Fidelity Standards (Pillar 8).

### Service 06: Sector-Specific Risk Monitoring & Health Advisories
*   **Target Users**: Public Health Officers, Tourism Hotspot Managers, Local Government Units.
*   **Operational Requirement**: Real-time monitoring of habitability thresholds and public health risks related to climate extremes.
*   **Information Product**: **Habitability & Health Risk Advisories** (Threshold-based monitoring for heat-stress and vector-borne risks).
*   **Outcome**: Improved public safety response; localized protection of critical economic sectors (e.g., Tourism).
*   **Supporting Standard**: Real-time Data Protocols (Pillar 6).

### Service 07: Authoritative Data Provisioning & Institutional Access Services
*   **Target Users**: Government CIOs, Research Institutions, Application Developers.
*   **Operational Requirement**: Access to validated, institutional-grade climate data without the legal ambiguity of data ownership.
*   **Information Product**: **Authoritative Data Exchange API** (Managed access to the National Single-Source-of-Truth).
*   **Outcome**: Streamlined inter-agency data sharing; removal of institutional friction and liability in data dissemination.
*   **Supporting Standard**: Authority Data Exchange Protocol (Pillar 7).

## 3. Institutional Supporting Infrastructure
To maintain the integrity of these services, the following technical standards are established as core components of the NCAIF:
1.  **Stewardship Ledger**: Ensures data provenance and ownership transparency.
2.  **Fidelity Verification System**: Implements automated data quality labeling (e.g., Authoritative, Provisional, Experimental).
3.  **Conceptual Data Model (CDM)**: Provides the semantic interoperability required for cross-sectoral analysis.
