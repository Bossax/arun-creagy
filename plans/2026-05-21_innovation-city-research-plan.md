# Innovation City Research Plan: Density, Infrastructure, and Talent

## Core Hypotheses to Test
1. **The Density Hypothesis:** A city that can attract talent and funding to build high-skill jobs and startups is inherently a city that can build/support urban density.
2. **The Infrastructure & Retention Hypothesis:** Infrastructure development that enables density (anti-sprawl) is a founding stone of an innovation city. Sprawling cities fail to deliver the quality of life necessary to retain top talent.

## Research Strategy (Perplexity Queries)

To rigorously test these hypotheses, we will break them down into specific sub-queries to run via `perplexity_ask` (using the Sonar Pro model for factual, cited answers) or `perplexity_research` for deeper literature reviews.

### Phase 1: Testing the Density Hypothesis
We need to establish if physical and economic density is a strict prerequisite for startup ecosystem success.
*   **Query 1.1 (Agglomeration Economics):** "What is the empirical relationship between urban population/economic density and the concentration of high-skill tech jobs and venture capital funding? Cite specific urban economics studies."
*   **Query 1.2 (The Density Exception):** "Are there any successful examples of low-density or sprawling cities that have built high-tier startup ecosystems that attract significant venture capital? If so, what mechanisms compensated for the lack of density?"
*   **Query 1.3 (Ecosystem Density):** "How does 'ecosystem density' (the physical proximity of founders, universities, and investors) specifically accelerate venture capital deployment compared to distributed networks?"

### Phase 2: Testing the Infrastructure & Retention Hypothesis
We need to validate if sprawl actively harms talent retention and what specific infrastructure prevents this.
*   **Query 2.1 (Sprawl vs. Talent):** "How does urban sprawl impact the retention of high-skilled tech talent? Are there studies linking commute times and car-dependency to brain drain in emerging innovation hubs?"
*   **Query 2.2 (Quality of Life Infrastructure):** "What specific types of urban infrastructure (e.g., public transit networks, walkability, mixed-use zoning) are most strongly correlated with attracting and retaining tech talent and digital nomads globally?"
*   **Query 2.3 (Comparative Case Studies):** "Provide case studies of emerging secondary cities (similar to Da Nang or Yogyakarta) that successfully transitioned into innovation hubs through intentional infrastructure investment rather than organic sprawl. What infrastructure did they prioritize?"
