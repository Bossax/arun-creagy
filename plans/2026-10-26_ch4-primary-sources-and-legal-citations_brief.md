## 4. Benchmark Case Studies (PIER, RIICE, GRADE): Status & Citations

The project **does not hold raw external PDF publication files** in the git workspace. The academic citations, empirical models, and operational parameters synthesized in [`plans/chapter4/remote_sensing_and_secondary_sources.md`](file:///C:/Users/sitth/OracleWorkspace/Arun_Creagy/plans/chapter4/remote_sensing_and_secondary_sources.md) are derived from the following authoritative public research:

### 1. Bank of Thailand (PIER) Agricultural Disaster Relief Benchmark
- **Full Citation**: Chantarat, S., Chichaibelu, E. H., Samphantharak, K., & Sandoz, C. (2019). *Extreme Weather, Food Security and the Nutritional Status of Children in Rural Thailand*. PIER Discussion Papers No. 121. Puey Ungphakorn Institute for Economic Research.
- **Empirical Finding Cited**: Public disaster relief subsidies in Thailand cover less than 30% of farmers' actual crop damage and economic loss, demonstrating that fiscal relief is strictly a welfare proxy rather than an asset damage measure.
- **Institutional Portal**: `https://www.pier.or.th/`

### 2. RIICE (Remote Sensing-based Information and Insurance for Crops in Emerging Economies)
- **Full Citation**: IRRI, SDC, GIZ, sarmap, and Allianz Consortium. (2018). *RIICE: Remote Sensing-Based Information and Insurance for Crops in Emerging Economies — Final Project Synthesis Report*. International Rice Research Institute (IRRI) & Swiss Agency for Development and Cooperation (SDC).
- **Technical Model Cited**: Automated crop yield loss estimation combining Sentinel-1 Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) imagery with the ORYZA crop growth simulation model; demonstrated reduction of post-disaster crop damage assessment from 6 months to 15 days in Southeast Asian pilots (Vietnam and Thailand).
- **Official Portal**: `https://www.riice.org/`

### 3. World Bank GRADE (Global Rapid post-disaster Damage Estimation)
- **Full Citation**: Gunasekera, R., Ishizawa, O. A., et al. (2018, updated 2020). *GRADE Methodological Guidelines: Global Rapid Post-Disaster Damage Estimation Methodology*. World Bank Disaster Risk Financing and Insurance Program (DRFIP), Global Facility for Disaster Reduction and Recovery (GFDRR). Washington, DC: World Bank.
- **Technical Model Cited**: Spatial overlay of disaster hazard footprint onto spatial asset exposure (DOPA households, DPT building archetypes, CGD standard unit construction costs); empirical validation demonstrated $\pm 10–12\%$ fidelity (88–90% accuracy) compared to full multi-month ground PDNAs across 66 international disasters.
- **Official Portal**: `https://www.worldbank.org/en/topic/disasterriskmanagement` / `https://www.gfdrr.org/`
