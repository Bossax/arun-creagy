## 8) Canonical versus internal versus lineage boundary map

### Canonical external-facing messaging
- Experiment B is the approved working default.
- Asset indicators and governance indicators are complementary signals of resilience conditions.
- Scores help indicate conditions, not proven resilience outcomes.
- Stakeholder-facing outputs should show asset scores and six governance pillar scores, not activation chains.
- `Community Self-Organization & Stewardship` must still influence what metrics are selected and scored, but its effect stays embedded inside relevant pillar scores rather than shown as a separate public score.
- Public explanation must stay out of formula and chain-engine language.

### Internal interpretive logic
- Activation chains explain how stock may or may not become function.
- Primary bottlenecks help internal diagnosis and example writing.
- `Community Self-Organization & Stewardship` is a cross-cutting scored concept used to scan for missing community-capacity metrics across relevant pillars.
- Metrics admitted through this cross-cutting concept must be assigned back into existing pillar scores rather than left as narrative only.
- Internal notes may draw from experiment logs to preserve nuance during drafting.

### Lineage and boundary evidence
- Experiment A shows the conservative no-growth baseline.
- Experiment C shows the outer boundary under looser exploration and later pruning.
- Both remain part of the record, but neither becomes the canonical model for the three final documents.
