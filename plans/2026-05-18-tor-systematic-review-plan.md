# Plan: Granular Review of National Climate Adaptation Database TOR (Section 5)

**Goal**: To perform a clause-by-clause audit of Section 5 (Scope of Work) in the Draft TOR to ensure maximum alignment with CRDB deliverables and enterprise sustainability standards.

## Step 1: Establish the "CRDB Anchor Lens"
*   **Objective**: To define the specific technical and strategic "Baselines" from CRDB that every TOR item must respect.
*   **Implementation Direction**: Extract the non-negotiable requirements from the CDM, NCAIF v4, Data governance, and the use cases from stakeholder engagement session to be used as the evaluation criteria.

## Step 2: Item-by-Item Review (Section 5: ขอบเขตของงานจ้างที่ปรึกษา)
*   **Objective**: To identify gaps, redundancies, or "Portal Traps" in each specific requirement of the TOR.
*   **Implementation Direction**: Iterate through Section 5.1 to 5.X. For each item, perform a "Four-Way Fit Check":
    1.  **Alignment**: Does it utilize the CRDB framework (NCAIF/CDM/G1-G5)?
    2.  **Sustainability**: Does it enable automated enterprise logic (API/ETL) or fall into the "Manual Trap"?
    3.  **Utility**: Does it address the "Friction points" identified in CRDB stakeholder workshops?
    4.  **Benchmarking (Perplexity)**: Cross-reference every technical clause (Metadata, API, Ingestion, Storytelling) with 2026 industrial standards to ensure "Future-Proofing."

## Step 3: Synthesis of Redlines and Personnel Requirements
*   **Objective**: To provide the final "Corrective Text" and specify the required expertise for the project team.
*   **Implementation Direction**: Consolidate the item-by-item findings into specific Thai/English replacement clauses for Section 5 and update Section 11 (Personnel) to include the necessary Data Engineering and Enterprise Architecture roles.

## Implementation Schedule
1.  **Immediate**: Complete Step 1 (Lens extraction).
2.  **Current Session**: Begin Step 2 (Item-by-item review starting at 5.1).
3.  **Final**: Generate the Review Memo (Step 3).
